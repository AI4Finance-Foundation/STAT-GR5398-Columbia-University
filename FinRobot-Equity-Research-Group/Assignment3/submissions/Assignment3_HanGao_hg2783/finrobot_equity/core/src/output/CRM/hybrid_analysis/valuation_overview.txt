Salesforce Inc. (CRM) is currently trading on moderate revenue growth and improving profitability metrics. Over the past several years, revenue has grown from $26.5 billion in 2022 to $41.5 billion in 2026, reflecting an average historical growth rate of around 12%. However, forward estimates show a deceleration in top-line expansion, with revenue growth projected at 5.0% in 2025, 6.0% in 2026, and 4.0% in 2027. These more modest forecasts are underpinned by ongoing product launches, segment expansion, and continued customer adoption, as highlighted in both the SEC filings and revenue driver analysis.

Profitability is a key area of improvement: EBITDA margin expands from 14.5% in 2022 to an estimated 38.9% in 2026, driven by margin discipline and operating leverage. SG&A expenses are declining as a percentage of revenue, supporting this margin expansion. The filings reinforce management’s focus on product innovation, strategic execution, and cost control amid macroeconomic and competitive pressures.

In peer context, Salesforce’s projected revenue CAGR of roughly 6% (2024-2027) is moderate compared to high-growth software peers, but its margin trajectory is notably strong. The balance of moderate growth and substantial margin expansion positions CRM more in line with mature, disciplined SaaS leaders rather than aggressive growth stories.

Fair value context should account for this shift: while growth is slowing, the improved profitability and resilient demand, as cited in filings, support the more conservative revenue assumptions. Risks include macro volatility, inflation, and competition, but current assumptions appear reasonable and well-supported by the company’s identified growth drivers and operating discipline. Overall, valuation should reflect Salesforce’s transition to a more mature, high-margin phase, with fair value anchored by both earnings quality and persistent, if slower, growth.
