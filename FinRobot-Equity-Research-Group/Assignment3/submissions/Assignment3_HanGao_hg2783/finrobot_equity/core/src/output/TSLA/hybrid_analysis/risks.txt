- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty Impacting Demand:** SEC filings highlight that economic slowdowns and inflationary pressures could weaken discretionary consumer demand for Tesla’s premium vehicles, slowing replacement cycles and directly pressuring revenue growth, which has already decelerated (0.9% in 2024A, -2.9% in 2025A forecast).

- **Margin Compression from Cost Pressures:** Sustained high inflation in components, logistics, and labor is limiting margin upside, as reflected in declining EBITDA and contribution margins (EBITDA margin down to 12.4% in 2025A from 21.7% in 2022A), threatening profitability if cost control measures are ineffective.

- **Supply Chain Disruption:** Filings warn that supply chain challenges can disrupt product availability, increase costs, and delay launches, creating execution risk and potential inventory issues—especially critical amid tight operating margins and ambitious product roadmaps.

- **Intensifying Competitive Pressure:** SEC disclosures and company updates cite increasing competition in EVs and adjacent technologies, which could erode market share and force price reductions, directly impacting revenue trajectory and margin discipline.

- **Regulatory Scrutiny and Execution Risk:** Heightened regulatory oversight and the risk of failing to execute on product innovation and launches are flagged as material, with potential to disrupt operations, delay new offerings, and impact Tesla’s ability to monetize its ecosystem and sustain revenue growth.
