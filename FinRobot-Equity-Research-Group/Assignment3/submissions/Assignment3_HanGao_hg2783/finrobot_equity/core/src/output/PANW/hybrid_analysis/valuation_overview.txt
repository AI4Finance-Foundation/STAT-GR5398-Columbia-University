Peer comparison, though not provided directly, would typically focus on other cybersecurity and cloud security firms. The transition from negative to strongly positive EBITDA margins also suggests PANW is moving into a more mature and profitable phase, supporting a premium valuation relative to peers still in heavy investment mode.

However, these estimates are well-supported by identified revenue drivers: product innovation, customer demand, and expansion into new segments and markets. SEC filings reinforce that management’s focus on innovation, strategic execution, and margin discipline underpin these forecasts, while also acknowledging macroeconomic and competitive risks.
