**Key Investment Risks for Snowflake Inc. (SNOW):**

- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** Recent SEC filings highlight that macroeconomic pressures—including inflation and potential economic slowdowns—could weaken discretionary enterprise spending and slow Snowflake’s revenue growth, particularly as revenue growth projections moderate sharply from 29.2% in 2026A to 4.0% in 2027E.

- **Margin Compression from Inflation:** Persistent inflationary pressures on labor, logistics, and components may limit margin improvement, threatening Snowflake’s reliance on operating leverage and cost control to achieve positive EBITDA margins (transition forecasted from -23.1% in 2026A to 12.9% in 2025E).

- **Execution Risk in Product Innovation and Expansion:** The company’s growth forecast is highly dependent on the successful execution of product innovation and strategic expansion. Failure to deliver on new products or to monetize its installed base as projected could significantly impact both revenue and margins.

- **Rising Competitive Pressure:** SEC filings and company commentary stress intensifying competition in the cloud, AI, and data platform markets, which could erode Snowflake’s pricing power, slow customer acquisition, and pressure market share.

- **Supply Chain and Operational Disruption:** Disruptions in the supply chain could affect product/service availability, cost structure, and the timing of new launches—potentially delaying revenue recognition and increasing cost volatility, as specifically flagged in recent filings.
