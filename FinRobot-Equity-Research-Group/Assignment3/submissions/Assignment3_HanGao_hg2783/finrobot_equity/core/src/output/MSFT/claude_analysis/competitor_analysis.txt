Microsoft Competitor Analysis

Microsoft has demonstrated consistent financial strength relative to its mega-cap technology peers, though the competitive landscape is evolving rapidly. With a revenue CAGR of 13.8% from 2021 to 2025, Microsoft has grown at a competitive pace, reaching $281.7 billion in fiscal 2025 revenue. Its EBITDA expanded from $85.1 billion in 2021 to $160.2 billion in 2025, reflecting strong operational leverage and margin expansion.

In terms of EBITDA generation, Microsoft has historically trailed Apple but has closed the gap meaningfully. By 2025, Microsoft's EBITDA of $160.2 billion surpassed Apple's $144.4 billion, a notable shift in competitive positioning. Amazon has emerged as the fastest-growing EBITDA generator among peers, reaching $165.3 billion in 2025 and projected to surge to $230.1 billion in 2026, which represents the most significant near-term competitive threat. Alphabet also posted strong 2025 EBITDA of $180.0 billion, exceeding Microsoft's figure and reflecting the strength of its advertising and cloud businesses.

Microsoft's EBITDA margin trajectory is a key differentiator. At 56.9% in 2025 and projected to reach 61.6% by 2027, its margin profile is superior to most peers, reflecting the high-margin nature of its cloud and software businesses.

On valuation, Microsoft trades at an EV/EBITDA of approximately 23.6x in 2025, a premium to Amazon at 15.3x, Alphabet at 21.2x, and Meta at 16.4x, though roughly in line with Apple at 27.0x. This premium reflects investor confidence in Microsoft's earnings quality and AI-driven growth prospects, though it leaves limited room for multiple expansion relative to peers.
