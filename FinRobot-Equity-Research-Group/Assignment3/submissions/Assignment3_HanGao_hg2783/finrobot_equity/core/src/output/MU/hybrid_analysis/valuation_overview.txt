EPS is forecast to rebound from a loss in 2023 to $8.11 in 2025E, rising further to $9.12 in 2027E.

Peer comparison would likely show Micron trading at a discount on trailing metrics due to recent losses, but the forward earnings rebound could support a rerating, especially as margins and growth normalize. No specific current PE ratio is given, but the implied multiple will become more meaningful as positive earnings return.

Revenue driver analysis supports the forecast: growth is underpinned by new product launches, portfolio expansion, and resilient demand, all cited as ongoing priorities by management.
