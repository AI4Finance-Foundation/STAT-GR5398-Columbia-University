Amazon.com Inc. (AMZN) continues to ride strong momentum following a stellar Q1 performance and all-time stock highs, propelled by accelerating revenue in its AWS cloud segment. Recent coverage highlights that AWS, benefiting from the ongoing AI build-out, is a central growth engine for Amazon, with the company already generating billions from AI-driven demand. However, competition is intensifying: Google Cloud posted a remarkable 63% growth in Q1, outpacing AWS’s 28% and Microsoft Azure’s 40%, signaling a more aggressive race in cloud services.

Financial forecasts for Amazon are robust. Revenue is projected to maintain an 11.1% CAGR through 2027, reaching nearly $830 billion. Contribution and EBITDA margins are expected to climb steadily, with EBITDA margin rising from 15.6% in 2023 to 46.7% by 2027, indicating both operational leverage and efficiency improvements. EPS is forecast to grow significantly, with estimates rising from $2.95 in 2023 to $8.69 by 2027.

Beyond cloud, Amazon’s robotics technology is seen as an underappreciated long-term growth catalyst. The company is also positioned as a key partner in the AI ecosystem, alongside peers like Oracle and CoreWeave, making it resilient to shifts in major AI partnerships.

A notable risk on the horizon is infrastructure strain from gigawatt-scale AI data center buildouts, with experts warning of potential rolling blackouts if energy and grid upgrades lag AI demand—a factor infrastructure investors should watch.

Retail sentiment is generally positive but mixed: while buzz is high across platforms, bullishness is moderate, especially on Reddit (38%) versus X.com (60%). Overall, Amazon remains a favored long-term investment due to its diversified growth engines in cloud, AI, and logistics, but investors should monitor competitive dynamics and infrastructure risks.
