Earnings analysis for Amazon.com, Inc. is constructive but execution-sensitive. The latest actual period shows revenue growth and earnings resilience, while the forecast depends on continued margin expansion, cost discipline, and demand durability. The key debate is whether Amazon.com, Inc. can sustain EPS growth and justify its valuation premium through product-cycle execution, recurring revenue growth, and operating leverage.

Key Earnings Read-Through
- Amazon.com, Inc. generated $716.9B of revenue in 2025A; latest observed revenue growth was 12.4%, providing the starting point for the earnings forecast.
- Year-over-year revenue growth was 12.4%, indicating a strong acceleration in top-line momentum.
- EPS increased to $7.29 in 2025A and is forecast to reach $8.69 by 2027E; the implied earnings upside depends on sustaining revenue growth while converting margin expansion into per-share profit growth.
- EBITDA is forecast to increase from $165.3B in 2025A to $387.5B in 2027E; this makes operating leverage and margin execution the central earnings debate.
- EBITDA margin is projected to expand from 23.1% in 2025A to 46.7% in 2027E; the magnitude of this step-up should be treated as an important execution assumption rather than a mechanical extrapolation.
- Contribution margin is expected to rise from 50.3% to 53.3%, supporting the earnings growth outlook through pricing discipline, services mix, and cost control.
- SG&A margin is expected to move from 8.1% to 6.6%, indicating continued cost discipline.
- Relative to peers, AMZN remains a major EBITDA generator, with peer comparison data available through 2026; this supports the quality of the earnings base even if growth is more moderate than some peers.
- AMZN trades at approximately 15.3x EV/EBITDA versus a peer average of 20.7x in 2025, implying that earnings delivery must support the valuation premium.

Key Watch Items
