- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** Deterioration in global economic conditions could slow premium-device replacement cycles and weaken discretionary consumer demand, directly impacting Amazon's revenue trajectory and growth assumptions (10-K, 10-Q).

- **Inflationary Pressure:** Persistent inflation may raise costs for components, logistics, and labor, while also eroding consumer purchasing power—potentially limiting margin improvement and EPS upside despite Amazon's focus on operating discipline (10-K, 10-Q).

- **Supply Chain Disruptions:** Ongoing supply chain exposure could affect product availability, cost structure, inventory timing, and the execution of new product or service launches, risking operational delays and increased costs (10-K, 10-Q).

- **Intense Competitive Pressure:** Heightened competition across core segments (e-commerce, cloud, devices, AI, and services) may impact Amazon’s pricing power and market share, challenging both top-line growth and margin improvement (10-K, 10-Q).

- **Regulatory and Cybersecurity Risks:** Increased regulatory scrutiny and potential cybersecurity or data-protection incidents could disrupt operations, cause reputational harm, and erode customer trust, leading to financial and strategic setbacks (10-K, 10-Q).
