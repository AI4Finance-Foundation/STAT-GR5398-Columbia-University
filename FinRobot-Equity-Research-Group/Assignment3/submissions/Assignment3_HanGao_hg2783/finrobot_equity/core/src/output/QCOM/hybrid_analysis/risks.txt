- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty**: Prolonged macroeconomic pressure could slow premium-device replacement cycles and weaken discretionary consumer demand, directly impacting QUALCOMM’s revenue growth and product-cycle execution (10-K, 10-Q, 2026-05-03).

- **Inflationary Cost Pressures**: Persistent inflation could increase component, logistics, and labor costs, while also eroding consumer purchasing power, threatening QUALCOMM’s margin discipline and limiting upside in EBITDA and contribution margins.

- **Supply Chain Disruption**: Ongoing or renewed disruptions in the global supply chain could negatively affect product availability, inventory management, cost structure, and timely execution of product launches—posing risk to revenue stability and operating leverage.

- **Intense Competitive Pressure**: Heightened competition across smartphones, wearables, cloud, services, and emerging AI features could erode pricing power and market share, constraining top-line growth and margin expansion as flagged in SEC disclosures.

- **Regulatory and Execution Risks**: Increasing regulatory scrutiny and the risk of operational missteps (e.g., in cybersecurity or strategic initiatives) could lead to compliance costs, reputational damage, and customer trust issues, undermining forecasted growth and profitability.
