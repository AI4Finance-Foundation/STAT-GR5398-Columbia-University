Meta Platforms Inc. (META) has been in the spotlight following a series of significant developments that underscore both its growth trajectory and emerging challenges.

Financially, Meta has demonstrated robust performance with 2023 revenue rising 15.7% year-over-year to $134.9 billion and first-quarter 2026 revenue surging 33%, the fastest pace in nearly five years. Looking ahead, revenue is expected to grow at a 14.3% CAGR through 2027, reaching over $232 billion. Importantly, contribution and EBITDA margins are projected to expand, with EBITDA margin climbing from 43.8% in 2023 to over 74% by 2027, indicating improving operational efficiency and profitability. EPS is also forecast to rise steadily, signaling continued earnings power.

However, Meta’s aggressive capital expenditure plans—exemplified by its recent acquisition of Assured Robot Intelligence (ARI) to accelerate its push into AI-powered humanoid robotics—have frustrated some investors. The company’s increased capex outlook has raised concerns about near-term returns, particularly as the broader "Magnificent 7" tech cohort commits a staggering $710 billion to AI infrastructure. This trend reflects a broader market theme, with AI investments driving record profits and stock market gains across the tech sector.

On the regulatory front, Meta faces a trial in New Mexico that could mandate sweeping changes to its core platforms, potentially impacting its U.S. operations and business model.

Investor sentiment is mixed. Retail buzz remains high, but bullishness is muted, particularly on social platforms like Reddit (28% bullish) and X.com (52% bullish), with a downward trend in positive sentiment. This divergence suggests caution amid Meta’s ambitious AI investments and ongoing regulatory risks.

Investment implications: Meta remains a high-growth, high-margin tech leader, but faces elevated capex risk and regulatory uncertainty. Investors should balance optimism about AI-driven expansion with caution regarding spending discipline and potential legal headwinds.
