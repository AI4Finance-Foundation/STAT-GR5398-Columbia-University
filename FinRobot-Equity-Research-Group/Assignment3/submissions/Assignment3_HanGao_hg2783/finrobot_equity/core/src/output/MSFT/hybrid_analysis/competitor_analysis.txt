Microsoft Corporation demonstrates strong financial performance and robust growth among its peers in the technology sector. Over the 2021-2027 period, Microsoft’s revenue exhibits a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 13.8%, reflecting sustained top-line expansion. This growth outpaces many large-cap technology competitors, who often report high single-digit to low double-digit revenue CAGRs.

Microsoft’s profitability metrics are notably strong. Contribution margin remains consistently high, starting at 68.9% in 2021 and rising to 71.8% by 2027E, indicating effective cost management and a scalable business model. The company’s EBITDA margin also improves from 50.6% in 2021 to an estimated 61.6% in 2027, suggesting superior operational efficiency compared to peers such as Google (Alphabet), Amazon, and IBM, whose margins are typically lower due to heavier cost structures or lower-margin business segments.

SG&A as a percentage of revenue declines from 15.0% in 2021 to 10.2% in 2027E, signifying increasing scale and leverage in administrative and selling expenses. In contrast, many competitors struggle to achieve similar SG&A efficiencies, particularly those with more diverse or hardware-centric portfolios.

EPS growth is also robust, growing from $8.12 in 2021 to an estimated $16.34 in 2027, more than doubling over the period—an indicator of strong shareholder value creation. While the PE ratio is not provided, the consistent EPS growth and margin expansion position Microsoft favorably against peers.

In summary, Microsoft’s superior revenue growth, expanding margins, and disciplined cost management set it apart from its major technology competitors, underscoring its position as an industry leader with significant operating leverage and profitability.
