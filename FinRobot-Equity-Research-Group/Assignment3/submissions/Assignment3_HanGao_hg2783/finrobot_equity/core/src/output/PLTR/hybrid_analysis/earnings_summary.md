The key debate is whether Palantir Technologies Inc. can sustain EPS growth and justify its valuation premium through product-cycle execution, recurring revenue growth, and operating leverage.

- Year-over-year revenue growth was 56.2%, indicating a strong acceleration in top-line momentum.
