Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) is a leading global technology company specializing in the design, development, and supply of a wide range of semiconductor and infrastructure software solutions. The company’s business model is built around two primary segments: Semiconductor Solutions and Infrastructure Software. Broadcom generates revenue by selling high-performance chips and components used in data centers, networking, broadband, wireless communications, and enterprise storage, as well as mission-critical software for cybersecurity, mainframe, and enterprise applications.

Broadcom’s products and services cater to a diverse customer base, including major equipment manufacturers, telecom firms, cloud service providers, and large enterprises. The company is well known for its extensive semiconductor portfolio, which powers wireless connectivity, networking equipment, and storage devices. On the software side, Broadcom delivers enterprise-grade solutions that enable organizations to manage, secure, and optimize their IT infrastructure. This dual focus on both hardware and software allows Broadcom to benefit from recurring revenue streams and cross-selling opportunities.

Market position is a clear strength for Broadcom. The company is recognized as a dominant player in both the semiconductor and infrastructure software markets, often holding significant market share in its core segments. Its diverse product lineup and broad customer base provide resilience against sector-specific downturns and foster stable long-term growth.

Financially, Broadcom has demonstrated robust performance over the past several years. Revenues grew from $27.45 billion in 2021 to $35.82 billion in 2023, with a particularly sharp projected jump to $51.57 billion in 2024, reflecting an estimated compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 23.5% through 2027. This growth is supported by improving contribution margins, rising from 61.4% in 2021 to an expected 70.8% in 2027, underscoring operational efficiency and strong pricing power. EBITDA has also increased significantly, with margins expected to reach 65.7% by 2027. While selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses temporarily spiked in 2024, margins are projected to normalize and decline to 5.1% by 2027. Earnings per share (EPS) are expected to rise steadily, reaching 5.86 by 2027.

In summary, Broadcom stands out as a technology powerhouse with a strong business model, diversified product offerings, and a solid market position. Its recent and projected financial performance signals sustained growth, profitability, and resilience in an increasingly digital and connected world.
