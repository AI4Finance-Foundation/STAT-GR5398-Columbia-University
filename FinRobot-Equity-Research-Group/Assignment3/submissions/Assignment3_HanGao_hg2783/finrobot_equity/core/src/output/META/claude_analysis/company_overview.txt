Meta Platforms, Inc. is a global technology conglomerate headquartered in Menlo Park, California, primarily known for operating the world's largest social media ecosystem. The company's core business model is built around advertising revenue generated through its family of applications, which includes Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Messenger. These platforms collectively serve billions of users worldwide, and Meta monetizes this massive user base by offering highly targeted digital advertising solutions to businesses of all sizes. In addition to its core advertising operations, Meta has made significant investments in its Reality Labs segment, which focuses on augmented and virtual reality hardware and software, including its Quest headset line and the broader metaverse initiative.

From a financial performance standpoint, Meta has demonstrated a strong recovery and growth trajectory following a difficult 2022, when revenue declined approximately 1.1 percent to roughly $116.6 billion and EBITDA fell sharply to $37.7 billion, reflecting a period of heavy investment and macroeconomic headwinds. The company rebounded decisively in 2023 and 2024, with revenue growing 15.7 percent and 21.9 percent respectively, reaching $164.5 billion in 2024. This growth accelerated further in 2025, with revenue reaching approximately $201 billion, representing 22.2 percent growth. Profitability has improved substantially alongside revenue growth, with EBITDA expanding to $104.5 billion in 2025 and EBITDA margins holding near 52 percent. Contribution margins have also steadily improved, rising from 78.3 percent in 2022 to 82 percent in 2025, reflecting disciplined cost management and operating leverage across the business. SG&A as a percentage of revenue has declined consistently, falling from over 23 percent in 2022 to 12 percent in 2025, underscoring the company's focus on operational efficiency following its widely publicized Year of Efficiency initiative.

In terms of market position relative to peers, Meta competes directly with Apple, Amazon, Alphabet, and Microsoft for advertising dollars, technology talent, and investor capital. While Meta's absolute EBITDA of $104.5 billion in 2025 remains below Alphabet's $180 billion and Microsoft's $160.2 billion, its growth rate has been among the most impressive in the peer group. Meta's EV/EBITDA multiple of approximately 16.4 times in 2025 positions it at a modest discount relative to Apple and Microsoft, suggesting the market continues to weigh execution risks around its Reality Labs investments against its core advertising strength. Earnings per share have grown from $8.63 in 2022 to $23.98 in 2025, reflecting both earnings growth and the impact of share repurchases. Looking ahead, forward estimates project continued revenue growth reaching approximately $232.6 billion by 2027, with EBITDA margins expected to expand meaningfully toward 74.5 percent, driven by operating leverage and the scaling of artificial intelligence-driven advertising tools across its platforms.
