**Key Investment Risks – Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN):**

- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty and Consumer Demand:** SEC filings highlight that ongoing macroeconomic pressures, including inflation and potential economic slowdowns, could reduce discretionary consumer spending and slow demand for Amazon’s products and services, directly impacting revenue growth and margin expansion forecasts.

- **Inflationary Pressures on Costs:** Rising costs for components, logistics, labor, and fulfillment—highlighted in recent filings—may limit Amazon’s ability to expand margins, even as revenue grows, potentially constraining forecasted EBITDA and EPS improvements.

- **Supply Chain Disruption:** Amazon’s filings flag supply chain exposure as a significant risk. Disruptions could impact product availability, increase costs, and result in inventory or launch timing issues, threatening both top-line growth and margin discipline.

- **Competitive Pressure Across Segments:** Intense competition in e-commerce, cloud (AWS), and emerging technology areas (AI, devices, services) could erode pricing power and market share, as noted in filings. This may limit Amazon’s ability to maintain its projected operating leverage and profitability relative to peers.

- **Regulatory and Cybersecurity Risks:** Heightened regulatory scrutiny and the potential for cybersecurity or data-protection breaches—explicitly cited in filings—pose risks of operational disruption, reputational damage, and lost customer trust, with potential negative impacts on both revenue and margin outlook.
