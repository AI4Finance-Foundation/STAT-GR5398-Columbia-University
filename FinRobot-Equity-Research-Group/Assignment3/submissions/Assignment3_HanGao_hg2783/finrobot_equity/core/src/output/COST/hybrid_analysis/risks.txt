**Key Investment Risks for Costco Wholesale Corporation (COST):**

- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** Recent SEC filings highlight the risk that economic slowdowns or persistent inflation could weaken discretionary consumer demand, potentially slowing revenue growth and impacting premium-product sales cycles.

- **Inflationary Pressures:** Rising costs in components, logistics, and labor—exacerbated by inflation—may compress margins and limit upside, especially if consumer purchasing power is eroded (as noted in both 10-K and 10-Q risk signals).

- **Supply Chain Disruption:** Ongoing exposure to supply chain challenges could disrupt product availability, impact inventory management, and increase costs, potentially affecting both revenue trajectory and operating margins.

- **Competitive Pressure:** Intensifying competition—especially in key growth categories—could pressure Costco’s pricing power and market share, limiting the effectiveness of strategic expansion and product innovation initiatives.

- **Regulatory and Execution Risks:** Increased regulatory scrutiny and the risk of missteps in strategic execution, such as delays in new market expansion or failure to sustain operating discipline, could hinder both margin improvement and overall growth targets.
