Amazon.com Inc. (AMZN) continues to demonstrate robust financial performance, with revenue rising from $469.8 billion in 2021 to $716.9 billion in 2025, reflecting an average historical growth rate of 11.2%. The company’s latest annual revenue growth of 12.4% underscores ongoing demand strength across its diverse product and service portfolio. Key revenue drivers include strong customer adoption, new product launches, service innovation, and strategic expansion into new markets and segments. 

Margin improvement has been a notable trend. Contribution margin expanded from 42.0% in 2021 to 50.3% in 2025, while EBITDA margin improved from 15.8% to 23.1% over the same period, supported by disciplined cost management and operating leverage. SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue have declined, signaling improved efficiency. EPS rebounded to $7.29 in 2025 from a pandemic-related low and is forecast to continue increasing.

Recent SEC filings (10-K and 10-Q, dated 2026-05-03) reinforce management’s commitment to product innovation, strategic execution, and operating discipline. The filings highlight margin discipline and demand resilience as central to Amazon’s outlook, while also identifying macroeconomic uncertainty, inflation, supply chain risks, regulatory pressure, and heightened competition—especially in AI, cloud, and devices—as ongoing challenges.

Looking forward, Amazon projects revenue growth to moderate, with forecasts of 5-6% annually through 2027, driven by continued product and service expansion, ecosystem monetization, and geographic diversification. Margin expansion and disciplined execution are expected to support further EPS growth, contingent on navigating macroeconomic and competitive headwinds. Overall, Amazon’s fundamentals remain solid, but investors should monitor external risks and execution capabilities in light of the company’s ambitious growth agenda.
