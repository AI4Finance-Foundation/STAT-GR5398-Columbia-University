Costco Wholesale Corporation (COST) is currently valued based on a track record of moderate, consistent revenue growth and disciplined margin management. Over the 2021-2025 period, revenue grew at an average annual rate of 8.9%, rising from $195.9 billion in 2021 to $275.2 billion in 2025. Forward estimates anticipate continued top-line growth, albeit at a slightly decelerating pace: 5.0% in 2025E, 6.0% in 2026E, and 4.0% in 2027E. These projections are underpinned by identified revenue drivers such as product innovation, strategic expansion, and customer demand resilience, all of which are explicitly supported in recent SEC filings.

Profitability metrics show improvement: contribution margin is projected to expand from 12.9% in 2021 to 15.8% by 2027E, while EBITDA margin is expected to rise from 4.4% to 8.2% over the same period. This margin expansion is attributed to operating leverage and cost control, which management highlights as core priorities. SG&A margin is also projected to decline, reflecting ongoing efficiency efforts.

Peer comparison is not directly available in the provided data, but Costco’s ability to grow at a high single-digit pace with expanding margins suggests a premium positioning relative to many large-scale retailers. The absence of current PE ratio data limits a traditional valuation multiple comparison, but rising EPS (from $11.3 in 2021 to $21.75 in 2027E) and margin expansion signal improving intrinsic value.

SEC filings reinforce confidence in the forecast, citing product innovation, disciplined execution, and expansion as ongoing catalysts, while also acknowledging macroeconomic, inflationary, and competitive risks as key constraints. Overall, the revenue growth assumptions for the next several years appear reasonable and supported by both historical performance and management’s identified strategic drivers, though execution and external risks remain important factors to monitor.
