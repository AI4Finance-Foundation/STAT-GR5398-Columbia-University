Adobe Inc. (ADBE) demonstrates a consistent track record of robust revenue growth, with revenues rising from $15.8 billion in 2021 to $23.8 billion in 2025, representing a historical CAGR of 10.8%. Forward projections show continued, though moderating, growth: 5.0% in 2025E, 6.0% in 2026E, and 4.0% in 2027E. These assumptions are grounded in ongoing product and segment expansion, margin optimization, and a resilient customer base, as highlighted in recent SEC filings. Management’s focus on product innovation, strategic execution, and disciplined cost control is expected to support sustained operating leverage and margin improvement, which is further reflected in the rising contribution and EBITDA margins out to 2027E.

Compared to peers in the software and SaaS sector—which often trade at premium multiples due to high recurring revenue and margin profiles—Adobe’s EBITDA margin, forecasted to reach nearly 59.2% by 2027E, is highly competitive. This margin expansion, coupled with rising EPS (from $10.1 in 2021 to a projected $19.95 in 2027E), supports a strong fair value context, though explicit P/E ratios are not provided here.

Risks are present, particularly macroeconomic uncertainty, inflation, competitive threats, and execution risk. However, filings consistently note product-cycle strength, installed-base monetization, and innovation in AI and cloud as key revenue drivers. These factors underpin management’s confidence in the revenue outlook despite potential headwinds.

Overall, the revenue growth assumptions for 2025-2027E appear reasonable and supported by both historical performance and identified drivers. Margin discipline and strategic innovation remain critical levers for valuation support and fair value realization, positioning Adobe well relative to peers if execution risks are managed.
