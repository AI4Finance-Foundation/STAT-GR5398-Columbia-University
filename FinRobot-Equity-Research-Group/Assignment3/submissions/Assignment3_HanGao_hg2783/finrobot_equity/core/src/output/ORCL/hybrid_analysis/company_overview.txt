Oracle Corporation (ORCL) is a leading global provider of enterprise information technology solutions, with a business model centered around developing, licensing, and supporting a comprehensive suite of cloud applications, platform services, and database technologies. The company generates revenue primarily through the sale of software licenses, cloud subscriptions, hardware products, and related services. Oracle’s core offerings include database management systems, enterprise resource planning (ERP) software, human capital management (HCM) applications, customer relationship management (CRM) solutions, and cloud infrastructure services.

Oracle has established a strong position in the enterprise software and cloud computing markets by serving a diverse range of customers, including large corporations, mid-sized businesses, and public sector organizations worldwide. Its flagship database products remain a critical backbone for many global enterprises, while its continued investment in cloud solutions has enabled Oracle to compete with top industry players.

Over the past several years, Oracle has demonstrated robust financial performance, as reflected in the provided metrics. Revenue has grown at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 9.1% from $40.5 billion in 2021 to $52.9 billion in 2024, with further growth expected to reach $66.4 billion by 2027. Contribution profit and margin have remained strong, with the margin stabilizing in the low 70% range, indicating Oracle’s ability to maintain profitability despite increased operational costs.

EBITDA, a key measure of operating performance, has grown significantly from $18.4 billion in 2021 to $21.4 billion in 2024, with projections indicating a substantial increase to $37.9 billion by 2027. The EBITDA margin is also expected to improve, rising from 40.4% in 2024 to 57.1% by 2027, underscoring Oracle’s focus on cost management and operational efficiency. Selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses as a percentage of revenue have gradually declined, highlighting improved scalability.

Earnings per share (EPS) have shown steady growth, moving from $3.15 in 2023 to a projected $5.32 in 2027. Overall, Oracle’s recent performance reflects its successful transition toward cloud-based offerings, sustained revenue growth, and ongoing profitability, positioning the company as a major, resilient force in the global technology landscape.
