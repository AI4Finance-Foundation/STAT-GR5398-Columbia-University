**IBM: 5 Key Investment Risks**

- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** Persistent global macroeconomic pressures could weaken enterprise IT spending, slow premium product and service upgrade cycles, and reduce discretionary budgets among IBM’s core customers, potentially constraining revenue growth as noted in recent SEC filings.

- **Inflationary Cost Pressures:** Elevated inflation remains a risk to IBM’s component, logistics, and labor costs, with potential to compress margins and offset operating leverage, especially as margin discipline is a critical forecast driver per management disclosures.

- **Supply Chain Disruption:** Ongoing supply chain issues may disrupt product availability, inflate input costs, affect inventory management, and delay new product launches, directly impacting IBM’s ability to execute on its product-cycle and revenue targets.

- **Intense Competitive Pressure:** Heightened competition across cloud, AI, services, and next-gen enterprise IT could erode IBM’s pricing power and market share, challenging both top-line growth and margin improvement as highlighted by management.

- **Execution Risk and Regulatory Scrutiny:** IBM’s growth relies on successful strategic execution and product innovation. Operational missteps, regulatory changes, or data-protection/cybersecurity incidents could result in financial underperformance, reputational harm, and heightened compliance costs.
