KLA Corporation (KLAC) has reported strong financial performance in its fiscal Q3 2026 results, with total revenues of $3.415 billion, marking an 11% year-over-year increase and a 4% sequential rise. The company’s non-GAAP diluted EPS was $9.40, exceeding analyst estimates and well above the $8.41 per share from a year ago. Gross margins improved to 62.2%, reflecting robust operational efficiency. KLA’s contribution margin and EBITDA margin are also projected to rise steadily in the coming years, signaling the company’s growing profitability and cost discipline.

KLA’s positive results are attributed to continued strong demand for its semiconductor process control tools, particularly as semiconductor manufacturers ramp up production for AI-driven applications and high-end processors. The company’s near-monopoly position in process control systems provides a significant competitive moat, supporting its long-term growth trajectory. Revenue is projected to grow at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 15.1% through 2027, with further margin expansion expected.

Despite these strong fundamentals, KLA shares recently fell 3.6% to $1,750.35 after reaching a 52-week high, and some valuation models suggest the stock remains overvalued at current levels (trading at around 50x 2026 P/E). Additionally, there are emerging competitive concerns as China’s domestic semiconductor equipment companies gain market share despite U.S. sanctions, which could create headwinds in the long term.

Retail sentiment is mixed and trending lower, particularly on social media platforms, with bullish sentiment below 60% and falling engagement. Overall, while KLA’s market leadership and financial strength remain compelling, investors should be mindful of valuation risks and shifting competitive dynamics in the global semiconductor industry.
