**Key Investment Risks for Arista Networks Inc. (ANET):**

- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** Persistent macro headwinds could slow premium-device replacement cycles and weaken discretionary enterprise spending, directly impacting Arista’s revenue growth trajectory (SEC 10-K/10-Q, 2026-05-03).

- **Inflationary Pressure:** Rising costs for components, logistics, and labor due to inflation may compress margins, challenging Arista’s ability to sustain its forecasted margin expansion and operating leverage (SEC 10-K/10-Q).

- **Supply Chain Disruption:** Ongoing supply chain constraints could disrupt product availability, increase inventory costs, and delay new product launches, threatening both near-term revenue and long-term product-cycle execution (SEC 10-K/10-Q).

- **Competitive Intensity:** Heightened competition across networking, cloud, and AI-driven segments may pressure pricing power and market share, particularly as rivals innovate or undercut on price, impacting Arista’s growth and profitability (SEC 10-K/10-Q).

- **Regulatory and Cybersecurity Risk:** Increased regulatory scrutiny and evolving cybersecurity threats pose risks of operational disruption, reputational damage, and potential legal or compliance costs, which could materially impact customer trust and business continuity (SEC 10-K/10-Q).
