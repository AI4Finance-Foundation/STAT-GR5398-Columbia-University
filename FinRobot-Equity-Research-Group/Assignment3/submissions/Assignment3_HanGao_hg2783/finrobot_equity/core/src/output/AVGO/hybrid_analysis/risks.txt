- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** Broadcom’s revenue growth and premium-device replacement cycles are highly sensitive to macroeconomic conditions; economic downturns could weaken discretionary consumer demand and negatively impact sales, as highlighted in both recent 10-K and 10-Q filings.

- **Inflationary Pressure:** Rising costs for components, logistics, and labor, coupled with weaker consumer purchasing power, may constrain margin improvement and limit upside, directly impacting Broadcom’s ability to sustain margin discipline and EPS growth.

- **Supply Chain Disruptions:** Ongoing exposure to supply chain volatility could delay product launches, disrupt product availability, and increase costs, jeopardizing Broadcom’s ability to execute on its product-cycle roadmap and meet revenue forecasts.

- **Competitive Pressure:** Intense competition in core markets—such as smartphones, wearables, services, cloud, and AI features—threatens pricing power and market share, presenting risks to both top-line growth and margin sustainability despite recent expansion.

- **Regulatory and Execution Risks:** Heightened regulatory scrutiny and execution risks related to strategic initiatives may challenge Broadcom’s ability to deliver on forecasted operating leverage, cost control, and margin targets, as underscored in recent SEC disclosures.
