- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** Ongoing macroeconomic pressures (e.g., inflation, slowing replacement cycles, weakened discretionary demand) could negatively impact Adobe’s revenue growth and margin outlook, as highlighted in both recent 10-K and 10-Q filings.

- **Inflationary Cost Pressures:** Rising inflation may increase component, logistics, and labor costs while reducing consumer purchasing power, potentially limiting Adobe’s ability to expand margins despite disciplined cost control efforts.

- **Supply Chain Disruption:** Persistent supply chain challenges could disrupt product availability, drive up costs, and impact inventory management and the timing of product launches, posing risks to revenue and operating leverage.

- **Intensifying Competitive Pressure:** Heightened competition across cloud, AI, and digital services markets could erode Adobe’s pricing power and market share, particularly if rivals innovate more quickly or aggressively on features and services.

- **Regulatory and Cybersecurity Risks:** Increasing regulatory scrutiny and potential cybersecurity or data-protection incidents could disrupt operations, damage Adobe’s reputation, and undermine customer trust, all of which are flagged in recent SEC filings as material risks.
