Micron Technology (MU) is experiencing a significant resurgence, driven largely by booming demand for memory chips in artificial intelligence (AI) infrastructure. After a sharp revenue and earnings contraction in 2023—revenue dropped nearly 50% and EPS turned negative—the company has rebounded strongly in 2024, with revenues jumping 61.6% and further rapid growth expected through 2025 (projected 48.9% revenue growth). Profitability is also recovering, with contribution and EBITDA margins rebounding to pre-downturn levels, reflecting improved pricing power and operational leverage. By 2025, Micron’s contribution margin is forecast to reach nearly 41%, and EBITDA margin to climb above 49%.

This turnaround is being fueled by the global AI boom, which has triggered massive infrastructure investments and insatiable demand for advanced memory solutions. Market commentary highlights Micron’s parallels with Nvidia—both have emerged as essential suppliers for AI, transforming their markets into “goldmines.” The renewed “memory supercycle” is also benefiting competitors like Samsung and SK Hynix, but Micron stands out as a top pick among investors and analysts, with speculation that it could join the trillion-dollar market cap club.

Retail sentiment is highly positive, with rising buzz and bullishness on social platforms like Reddit and X.com. Shares of Micron surged 53% last month, reflecting investor enthusiasm and confidence in its earnings growth trajectory. However, some divergence exists among sources regarding the stock’s relative attractiveness versus peers like SanDisk.

Investment implications are clear: Micron is positioned as a prime beneficiary of AI-driven secular trends, with strong financial recovery underway and robust growth forecasts. While sector cyclicality and competition remain risks, current momentum and the scale of forecasted profitability suggest continued upside potential for long-term investors.
