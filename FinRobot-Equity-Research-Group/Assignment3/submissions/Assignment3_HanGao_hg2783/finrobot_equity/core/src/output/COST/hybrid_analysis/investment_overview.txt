Costco Wholesale Corporation (COST) continues to deliver consistent financial performance, underpinned by disciplined operations and steady revenue growth. From FY2021 to FY2025, revenue increased from $195.9 billion to $275.2 billion, representing an average historical CAGR of 8.9%. The latest actual revenue growth was 8.2%, with projections indicating continued, albeit moderating, expansion of 5.0% in 2025, 6.0% in 2026, and 4.0% in 2027. This outlook is supported by drivers such as resilient customer demand, segment and product expansion, and ongoing innovation.

Profitability metrics show a positive trajectory. Contribution margin improved from 12.9% in 2021 to 12.8% in 2025, with forecasts pointing to further gains (reaching 15.8% by 2027). EBITDA margin is also set to expand, from 4.4% in 2021 to 5.2% in 2025E and up to 8.2% by 2027E, reflecting enhanced operating leverage and a disciplined approach to cost control. EPS is projected to grow from $11.3 in 2021 to $19.34 in 2025E and $21.75 in 2027E.

Recent SEC filings emphasize Costco’s focus on product innovation, strategic execution, and margin discipline. Management views demand resilience and effective product-cycle execution as essential to sustaining revenue growth. Risks identified include macroeconomic uncertainty, inflation, supply chain disruptions, competitive pressure, and regulatory scrutiny, all of which could impact both top-line growth and profitability.

Looking forward, Costco’s growth strategy—anchored by expansion into new markets and the introduction of new products—should continue to drive revenue and margin improvement. However, investors should remain mindful of macroeconomic headwinds and competitive pressures that could temper near-term upside. Overall, Costco’s operating discipline, innovation, and demand resilience position it well for continued, if moderating, growth.
