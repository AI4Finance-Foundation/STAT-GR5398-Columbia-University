Investment Update: Microsoft Corporation (MSFT)

Microsoft continues to deliver robust financial performance, underpinned by strong revenue growth and expanding margins. From FY2021 to FY2025, revenue increased at a 13.8% CAGR, rising from $168.1 billion in 2021 to $281.7 billion in 2025. Recent growth has been particularly strong, with revenue up 14.9% in 2025A. Contribution margin has remained stable and strong, moving from 68.9% in 2021 to 68.8% in 2025, with forecasts pointing to further improvement toward 71.8% by 2027. EBITDA margins have expanded meaningfully, reaching 56.9% in 2025 and projected to exceed 60% by 2027.

Key revenue drivers include ongoing product and segment innovation, margin discipline, resilient demand, and strategic investments in AI and ecosystem expansion. Microsoft’s ability to monetize its installed base and broaden its service penetration remains core to its growth thesis. SEC filings highlight management’s focus on product innovation, operating discipline, and strategic execution as pillars of the business strategy. Growth and expansion language in recent filings suggest continued upside potential from new markets and product cycles.

However, the outlook is not without risks. The company continues to flag macroeconomic uncertainty, inflationary pressures, supply chain exposure, competitive threats in devices, cloud, and AI, as well as regulatory scrutiny. These factors could impact both top-line growth and margin realization, especially if premium device cycles slow or cost pressures intensify.

Relative to peers, Microsoft’s EBITDA performance is strong and steadily rising, with sector-high EV/EBITDA multiples (23.6x for 2025E), reflecting market confidence in its growth and operating leverage.

Looking forward, Microsoft appears well-positioned for continued growth, supported by innovation, operating discipline, and demand resilience. Sustained margin improvement and strategic expansion should underpin further earnings growth, though investors should monitor macro and competitive risks closely.
