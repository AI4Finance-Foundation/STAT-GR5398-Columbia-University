Advanced Micro Devices Inc. (AMD) has demonstrated robust revenue growth in recent years, with a CAGR of 20.5% from 2021 to the 2027E forecast. The company’s 2022 revenue growth was exceptional at 43.6%, though 2023 saw a temporary contraction of -3.9%, likely reflecting cyclical or market-specific headwinds. Projections through 2027 suggest a continued upward trajectory, with annual growth stabilizing between 4% and 6% after a notable 34.3% rebound expected in 2025.

AMD’s contribution margins have remained strong, improving from 44.9% in 2022 to a projected 52.5% by 2027E, indicating effective cost management and pricing power relative to cost of operations. EBITDA margins dipped to 18.3% in 2023 but are forecasted to recover sharply, reaching 42.0% by 2027E. This margin expansion outpaces typical industry trends and suggests AMD is positioned to improve profitability, potentially due to operating leverage and anticipated product mix improvements.

SG&A as a percentage of revenue has inched up, peaking at 12.0% in 2025E but trending downward afterward, which may signal scaling efficiencies as revenues grow. EPS is projected to rise steadily from $0.53 in 2023 to $3.18 in 2027E, reflecting the company’s earnings recovery and future growth expectations.

Compared to industry peers, which include Intel and NVIDIA, AMD’s recent revenue volatility and margin expansion stand out. NVIDIA typically enjoys higher margins and stronger EPS growth, while Intel has faced stagnation and margin pressure. AMD’s strong contribution and EBITDA margin forecasts, coupled with aggressive revenue and earnings growth, position it as a formidable competitor poised to gain market share and improve financial returns relative to its peers over the forecast period.
