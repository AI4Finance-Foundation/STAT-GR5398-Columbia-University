Arista Networks Inc. (ANET) demonstrates robust financial performance among networking hardware and software peers, driven by rapid revenue expansion and strong profitability metrics. With a revenue compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 32.2% from 2021 to 2027E, Arista is growing significantly faster than most large competitors in the sector, such as Cisco Systems or Juniper Networks, which typically report single-digit or low double-digit growth rates. This rapid expansion signals strong market share gains, likely fueled by increased demand for high-performance cloud networking solutions.

Arista’s profitability metrics are particularly notable. The company’s contribution margin remains consistently above 60%, climbing to a projected 67.1% in 2027E. EBITDA margin also expands meaningfully, from 33.1% in 2021 to an estimated 61.1% in 2027E. This margin profile is substantially higher than industry norms, where EBITDA margins often range from 20% to 35%. These results reflect both a highly efficient operating model and strong pricing power, suggesting Arista enjoys a competitive advantage in cost structure or product differentiation.

Operating leverage is evident in declining SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue (from 12.5% in 2021 to a projected 6.0% by 2027E), supporting further margin expansion. EPS growth is also strong, rising from $0.69 in 2021 to a projected $3.33 in 2027E, indicating effective conversion of revenue growth into shareholder value.

Compared to peers, Arista stands out for high growth, superior margins, and operational efficiency. This positions the company as a best-in-class performer in the networking sector, with a sustainable advantage over more mature, slower-growing incumbents. Continued margin expansion and revenue growth suggest Arista is well positioned to outpace its competition in the medium term.
