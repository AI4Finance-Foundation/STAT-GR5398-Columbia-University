- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** Recent SEC filings highlight that global macroeconomic pressures—such as slowing premium-device replacement cycles and weaker discretionary consumer demand—pose a direct risk to AMD’s revenue growth trajectory and demand resilience.  

- **Inflationary Pressures:** Elevated inflation increases component, logistics, and labor costs, which could constrain AMD’s margin upside and erode operating leverage, particularly as the company’s forecasts rely on sustained margin discipline.  

- **Supply Chain Disruption:** AMD’s filings and financials indicate significant supply chain exposure; disruptions could affect product availability, cost structure, inventory timing, and the execution of new product launches, impacting both revenue and profitability.  

- **Intensifying Competitive Pressure:** Heightened competition across smartphones, wearables, services, cloud, and AI features threatens AMD’s pricing power and market share, potentially limiting growth and pressuring margins as reflected in recent filings.  

- **Execution and Regulatory Risks:** Strategic execution risk—including timely product-cycle execution—remains high, especially with ongoing regulatory scrutiny; any missteps could delay launches, impact compliance, or result in operational disruption, affecting financial performance.
