Micron Technology Inc. (MU) has demonstrated a strong recovery and growth trajectory following a challenging fiscal 2023. After a significant revenue decline in 2023 (-49.5% YoY, $15.5B), the company rebounded to $25.1B in 2024 and is projected to reach $39.2B by 2025E. The average historical revenue growth stands at 18.0%, supported by robust product launches, portfolio expansion, and sustained demand, particularly in technology segments reliant on memory and storage.

Key revenue drivers include ongoing product innovation, strategic expansion into new markets, volume growth from increased adoption, and a focus on improved pricing and yield management. SEC filings reinforce management’s emphasis on product innovation and margin discipline as central to the company’s strategy. Margin improvement and sustained operating leverage are expected to support further EPS upside, with EPS projected to rise from $0.70 in 2024 to $8.11 in 2025E. Contribution and EBITDA margins are forecast to recover to over 40% by 2027, reflecting strong cost control and favorable product mix.

However, filings also highlight several risks: macroeconomic uncertainty could dampen device replacement cycles and discretionary spending; inflation may constrain margin expansion by increasing input and logistics costs; and supply chain, regulatory, and competitive pressures remain persistent challenges. Execution and cybersecurity risks are also noted as potential headwinds.

Looking ahead, revenue growth assumptions for 2025-2027 (4-6% annually) are underpinned by new product cycles and portfolio breadth. Expansion into AI, cloud, and premium device markets may provide additional upside. Overall, Micron’s recent performance and forward outlook suggest a positive trajectory, provided the company maintains margin discipline and effectively navigates external risks.
