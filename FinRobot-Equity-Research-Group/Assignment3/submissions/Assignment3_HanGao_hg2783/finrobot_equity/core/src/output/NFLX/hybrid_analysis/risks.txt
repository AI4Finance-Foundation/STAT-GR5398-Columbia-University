- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** Recent SEC filings highlight that macroeconomic pressures—including inflation and potential economic downturns—could weaken discretionary consumer demand and slow subscriber growth, directly impacting Netflix’s revenue trajectory and operating leverage.

- **Inflation & Cost Pressures:** Elevated inflation could increase Netflix’s operating costs (content production, logistics, labor) and erode consumer purchasing power, limiting the company’s ability to expand margins despite revenue growth.

- **Competitive Pressure:** Intensifying competition from other streaming platforms and evolving digital services threatens Netflix’s pricing power, subscriber retention, and market share, as flagged in recent disclosures.

- **Execution & Supply Chain Risks:** The filings note that supply chain disruptions could impact content availability, increase costs, and delay product or feature launches, undermining Netflix’s ability to sustain growth and meet margin targets.

- **Regulatory & Cybersecurity Risks:** Heightened regulatory scrutiny and the risk of cybersecurity or data protection breaches could lead to operational disruptions, reputational damage, or legal liabilities, adversely affecting Netflix’s business performance and customer trust.
