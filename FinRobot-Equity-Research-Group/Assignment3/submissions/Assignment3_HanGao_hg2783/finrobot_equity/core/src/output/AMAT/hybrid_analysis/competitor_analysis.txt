Applied Materials Inc. (AMAT) is a leading player in the semiconductor equipment industry, and its financial performance between 2021 and 2027 reflects steady growth and improving profitability. Over this period, AMAT’s revenue is projected to increase at a 5.3% CAGR, growing from $23.1 billion in 2021 to an estimated $32.8 billion in 2027. While annual revenue growth is moderate—ranging from 2.5% to 6%—the company demonstrates consistent top-line expansion, which is an advantage in a cyclical sector.

Compared to industry peers, AMAT stands out with robust profitability metrics. Contribution margin improves from 47.3% in 2021 to a projected 51.7% in 2027, indicating effective cost management and potential pricing power. The EBITDA margin shows a marked increase, growing from 32% in 2021 to an impressive 47% by 2027, suggesting operating leverage and efficiency gains that likely outpace many competitors with lower margins.

SG&A expenses remain well-controlled, decreasing as a percentage of revenue from 5.3% to 4.7% over the forecast period, further supporting margin expansion. EPS growth is also strong, climbing from $6.47 in 2021 to $10.39 in 2027—an indicator of shareholder value creation through both revenue growth and margin expansion.

While the data does not include direct peer figures, AMAT’s rising margins, disciplined cost structure, and steady earnings growth position it favorably relative to typical industry rivals, who often face more volatile margins and less consistent EPS growth due to market cycles and high R&D overhead. In summary, AMAT’s financial trajectory suggests it is outperforming peers on efficiency and profitability, reinforcing its leadership position in the semiconductor equipment market.
