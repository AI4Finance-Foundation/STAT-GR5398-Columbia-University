KLA Corporation’s current and projected valuation metrics indicate a business with healthy margins, strong historical growth, and disciplined cost management. Revenues have risen from $6.9B in 2021 to an estimated $12.8B in 2025E, representing a 15.1% CAGR. Margins remain robust: EBITDA margin is forecast to expand from 41.2% in 2021 to 55.7% in 2025E and beyond, driven by operating leverage and SG&A discipline, with SG&A margin projected to decline to 6.6% by 2027E.

The SEC filings reinforce the credibility of these forecasts, highlighting product innovation, geographic expansion (notably in China), and resilient demand as core revenue drivers. Management’s focus on margin discipline supports continued EPS growth, provided cost control and favorable product mix persist. However, filings also caution that macroeconomic uncertainty, inflation, supply chain risk, and heightened competition could constrain both top-line growth and margin expansion.

Peer comparison is not directly available in the data, but KLA’s margin and growth profile suggest it is operating at the high end of its peer group in the semiconductor equipment sector, where high single-digit revenue growth and EBITDA margins above 40% are considered strong.

The fair value context appears constructive: the forecast 2025–2027 revenue growth of 4–6% is conservative relative to the 16.1% historical average, and is explicitly supported by identified drivers—product innovation, segment expansion, Chinese market growth, and pricing power. The company’s ability to sustain or improve margins through operating discipline further supports the case for premium valuation. Risks remain, but the growth and margin assumptions are well-grounded in both recent performance and management’s outlined strategy.
