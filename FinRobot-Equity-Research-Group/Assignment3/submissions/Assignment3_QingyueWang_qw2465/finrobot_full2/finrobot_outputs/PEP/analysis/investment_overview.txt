PepsiCo Inc. (PEP) delivered solid financial performance over the past three years, with revenue rising from $79.5 billion in 2021 to $91.5 billion in 2023, representing a 7.3% CAGR. Growth momentum is expected to continue, with revenue projected to reach $108.7 billion by 2027, reflecting a CAGR of 4.3% over the forecast period. However, recent results suggest a moderation in top-line growth, with 2024 revenue expected to increase only 0.4% year-over-year, before re-accelerating in 2025 and beyond.

Contribution margins have remained healthy and are projected to improve further, rising from 53.3% in 2021 to 57.1% by 2027. This reflects PepsiCo’s ability to pass on costs, optimize its supply chain, and focus on higher-margin categories. EBITDA has also grown steadily, reaching $15.8 billion in 2023 and forecasted to exceed $20 billion by 2027. Despite some margin compression in 2025, long-term EBITDA margins are projected to recover, demonstrating resilience amid inflationary pressures and increased SG&A spending.

EPS growth has been steady, moving from $5.51 in 2021 to $6.98 in 2024, with further improvement expected. Valuation is becoming more attractive, with the PE ratio expected to compress from 31.5x in 2021 to 20.5x by 2027.

Relative to peers, PepsiCo remains competitive in both EBITDA generation and EV/EBITDA valuation. Its 2023 EV/EBITDA of 17.1x is broadly in line with Coca-Cola (KO) and below Monster (MNST), reflecting a balanced risk/reward profile.

Outlook: PepsiCo is well-positioned for steady, profitable growth driven by its diversified portfolio, ongoing efficiency initiatives, and ability to navigate cost headwinds. While near-term growth may moderate, the long-term trajectory remains positive, supported by strong brand equity and global scale.
