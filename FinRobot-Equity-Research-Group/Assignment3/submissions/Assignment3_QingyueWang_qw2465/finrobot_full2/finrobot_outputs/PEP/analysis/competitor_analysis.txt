PepsiCo Inc. (PEP) stands as a dominant player in the global beverage and snack industry, competing closely with The Coca-Cola Company (KO), Keurig Dr Pepper (KDP), and Monster Beverage Corp. (MNST). PepsiCo’s revenue base is the largest among peers, with 2023 revenue at $91.5 billion, reflecting a 4.3% CAGR through 2027E, supported by both beverage and snack portfolios. In comparison, KO and KDP focus predominantly on beverages, while MNST specializes in energy drinks.

EBITDA performance positions PepsiCo as a strong, but not leading, peer. For 2023, PepsiCo’s EBITDA was $15.8 billion, trailing KO’s $15.6 billion but ahead of KDP ($4.0 billion) and MNST ($2.0 billion). However, PepsiCo’s EBITDA margin (17.2% in 2023) trails KO’s (typically above 30%), reflecting PepsiCo’s heavier exposure to lower-margin snacks and higher SG&A (40.1% in 2023). Contribution margins, however, are robust and projected to improve from 53.3% (2021) to 57.1% (2027E), indicating operational efficiency gains.

In terms of valuation, PepsiCo trades at a discount to KO and MNST on an EV/EBITDA basis, with a 2023 multiple of 17.1x versus KO’s 18.5x and MNST’s 28.6x. This discount is likely due to PepsiCo’s lower EBITDA margins and more diversified product mix, which moderates its profitability profile. PepsiCo’s PE ratio is also lower than historical levels, indicating relatively attractive valuation compared to peers, especially given its consistent EPS growth and scale advantages.

In summary, PepsiCo’s strengths lie in its diversified revenue streams, strong top-line growth, and scale. However, its margins and valuation multiples trail KO and MNST, reflecting a trade-off between diversification and profitability. The company remains a stable, lower-risk investment within the sector, with room for margin expansion as operational efficiencies continue.
