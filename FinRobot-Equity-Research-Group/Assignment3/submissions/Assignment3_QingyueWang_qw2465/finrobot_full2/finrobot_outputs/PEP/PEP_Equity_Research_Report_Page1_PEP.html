Company Overview

PepsiCo Inc. (PEP) is a leading global food and beverage company recognized for its diverse product portfolio and expansive international footprint. The company operates through a dual business model, generating revenues from both its beverage and convenient foods segments. PepsiCo’s iconic brands include Pepsi, Mountain Dew, Gatorade, Tropicana, Lay’s, Doritos, Quaker, and Cheetos, enabling it to maintain strong consumer loyalty and brand equity worldwide.

PepsiCo’s products span carbonated and non-carbonated beverages, juices, water, sports drinks, and a wide array of snack foods and packaged goods. The company’s integrated supply chain, extensive distribution network, and focus on innovation (including healthier product offerings and sustainable packaging) have helped it adapt to shifting consumer preferences and maintain relevance in a highly competitive market.

In terms of market position, PepsiCo is one of the largest players in the global food and beverage industry, competing directly with The Coca-Cola Company, Keurig Dr Pepper (KDP), and Monster Beverage (MNST). Based on recent EBITDA figures, PepsiCo remains competitive with its peers, reporting $15.8 billion in 2023 compared to Coca-Cola’s $15.6 billion and significantly ahead of KDP and MNST. While PepsiCo’s EV/EBITDA multiple has trended slightly below that of Coca-Cola, it still commands a premium relative to most peers, reflecting investor confidence in its diversified model and steady growth prospects.

Financially, PepsiCo has demonstrated consistent revenue growth, with a 4.3% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) projected through 2027. Revenues grew from $79.5 billion in 2021 to $91.5 billion in 2023, with further increases expected. Contribution margins have remained robust, improving from 53.3% in 2021 to a projected 57.1% by 2027, indicating strong pricing power and operating efficiency. EBITDA margins have ranged between 16.5% and 18.8%, reflecting stable profitability despite inflationary pressures and rising SG&A expenses. Earnings per share (EPS) have grown steadily, reaching $6.59 in 2023 and forecasted to rise to $7.19 by 2027, while the price-to-earnings (PE) ratio is expected to decline from 31.5 in 2021 to 20.5 in 2027, suggesting improving value for shareholders.

Overall, PepsiCo’s resilient business model, diversified product range, and strong market positioning underpin its solid financial performance and growth outlook in the global food and beverage sector.

Investment Update

PepsiCo Inc. (PEP) delivered solid financial performance over the past three years, with revenue rising from $79.5 billion in 2021 to $91.5 billion in 2023, representing a 7.3% CAGR. Growth momentum is expected to continue, with revenue projected to reach $108.7 billion by 2027, reflecting a CAGR of 4.3% over the forecast period. However, recent results suggest a moderation in top-line growth, with 2024 revenue expected to increase only 0.4% year-over-year, before re-accelerating in 2025 and beyond.

Contribution margins have remained healthy and are projected to improve further, rising from 53.3% in 2021 to 57.1% by 2027. This reflects PepsiCo’s ability to pass on costs, optimize its supply chain, and focus on higher-margin categories. EBITDA has also grown steadily, reaching $15.8 billion in 2023 and forecasted to exceed $20 billion by 2027. Despite some margin compression in 2025, long-term EBITDA margins are projected to recover, demonstrating resilience amid inflationary pressures and increased SG&A spending.

EPS growth has been steady, moving from $5.51 in 2021 to $6.98 in 2024, with further improvement expected. Valuation is becoming more attractive, with the PE ratio expected to compress from 31.5x in 2021 to 20.5x by 2027.

Relative to peers, PepsiCo remains competitive in both EBITDA generation and EV/EBITDA valuation. Its 2023 EV/EBITDA of 17.1x is broadly in line with Coca-Cola (KO) and below Monster (MNST), reflecting a balanced risk/reward profile.

Outlook: PepsiCo is well-positioned for steady, profitable growth driven by its diversified portfolio, ongoing efficiency initiatives, and ability to navigate cost headwinds. While near-term growth may moderate, the long-term trajectory remains positive, supported by strong brand equity and global scale.

Recent News Summary

PepsiCo Inc. (PEP) has remained in the financial spotlight with a steady stream of news and notable financial developments. The company’s recent quarterly results highlighted resilience in a challenging macro environment, though a “widening gap” with Coca-Cola in performance was noted. PepsiCo’s revenue growth has slowed from 8.7% in 2022 to just 0.4% in 2024, but is forecast to recover to a 4.3% CAGR through 2027. Profitability metrics are stable, with contribution margins rising to 54.6% in 2024 and EBITDA margins rebounding to 18.2% after a dip in 2023.

Cost control remains a strategic focus. PepsiCo’s ongoing productivity initiatives are seen as an important hedge against persistent inflationary pressures, which should help protect margins. SG&A as a percentage of revenue is also expected to gradually decline, supporting operating leverage.

Sustainability is also in the spotlight. A new partnership with TalusAg aims to decarbonize fertilizer use, aligning PepsiCo with broader ESG trends and potentially reducing long-term input costs.

Comparatively, PepsiCo maintains healthy EBITDA levels relative to peers like Keurig Dr Pepper (KDP), Monster (MNST), and Coca-Cola (KO), though its EV/EBITDA multiple has compressed to 14.7x for 2024, below KO’s 19.2x, suggesting it trades at a relative discount.

Dividend investors continue to see PepsiCo as an attractive option, with its status as a Dividend King and consistent EPS growth (projected to rise to $7.19 by 2027). However, a recent discounted cash flow (DCF) analysis suggests shares may be modestly overvalued versus intrinsic value.

Investor sentiment is mixed-to-positive, with stronger bullishness on X.com than on Reddit, possibly reflecting a divide between retail and broader market perspectives.

Investment Implications: PepsiCo offers stable growth, a strong dividend, and improving margins, though near-term valuation may be stretched. Long-term investors may see value in its defensive characteristics and ESG initiatives, while short-term upside could be limited without a reacceleration in revenue or margin expansion.

Valuation

Valuation Overview – PepsiCo Inc. (PEP)

PepsiCo currently trades at a 2024E P/E ratio of approximately 21.8x and an EV/EBITDA multiple of 14.7x, both below its recent historical averages (25.8x P/E, 17.1x EV/EBITDA for 2023). Earnings per share are expected to grow modestly from $6.59 in 2023 to $6.98 in 2024E, though consensus forecasts a dip in 2025 before rebounding. Revenue growth is projected to slow to low single digits, with a 2024E-2027E CAGR of 4.3%. EBITDA margins are expected to show some recovery, moving from a 2025E trough (15.8%) back toward 18.8% in 2027E.

Compared to peers, PepsiCo’s EV/EBITDA valuation is at a discount to Coca-Cola (KO), which trades at 19.2x 2024E and 18.0x 2025E, and is below Monster Beverage (MNST) at 25.7x 2024E and 27.9x 2025E. Keurig Dr Pepper (KDP) trades at a similar level, though with lower margins and growth. Historically, PEP’s premium to KDP is justified by its stronger brand, scale, and margin profile, while its discount to KO and MNST reflects slightly slower top-line growth and margin pressure in the near term.

Given the relative valuation reset, PepsiCo appears attractively valued on a forward basis. The current discount to KO and historical averages provides a margin of safety, especially as margin recovery is anticipated post-2025. On a normalized basis, a fair value for PEP would be closer to 17-18x EV/EBITDA, implying potential upside from current levels as margin and earnings growth stabilize. Overall, PEP offers a solid blend of defensive cash flows and long-term growth, suggesting a favorable risk/reward at current valuation.

Risks

Key Investment Risks for PepsiCo Inc. (PEP):

  • ▪ Margin Compression Risk: EBITDA margins have declined from 18.7% (2021) to 16.5% (2025A) despite healthy revenue growth, indicating rising costs and pressure on profitability.
  • ▪ Slowing Revenue Growth: Revenue growth is decelerating—dropping from 8.7% (2022) to 0.4% (2024A) and 2.3% (2025A)—raising concerns about future top-line expansion.
  • ▪ Competitive Pressure: PepsiCo’s EV/EBITDA multiples are at or below key peers like KO, and EBITDA growth lags behind KO, suggesting intensifying competition and potential loss of market share.
  • ▪ Earnings Volatility: EPS is projected to decline from $6.98 (2024A) to $6.03 (2025A), reflecting potential operational challenges or increased costs impacting bottom-line performance.
  • ▪ High SG&A Expenses: SG&A margin remains elevated (near or above 39% through 2027E), which could limit operational flexibility and reduce leverage to improve profitability if sales growth continues to slow.
