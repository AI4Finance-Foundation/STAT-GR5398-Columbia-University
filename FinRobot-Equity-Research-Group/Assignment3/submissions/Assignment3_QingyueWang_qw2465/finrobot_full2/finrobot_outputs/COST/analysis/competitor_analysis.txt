Costco Wholesale Corporation (COST) stands out among its key peers—Walmart (WMT), Target (TGT), and BJ’s Wholesale Club (BJ)—for its robust financial performance, premium valuation, and consistent growth trajectory.

In terms of revenue, Costco is second only to Walmart but outpaces Target and BJ’s by a wide margin. Costco’s revenue grew at an impressive 8.9% CAGR from 2021 to 2027E, underlining steady expansion compared to the generally slower growth rates seen at Walmart and Target. Contribution margin and EBITDA margin are also on a positive trajectory, with projected EBITDA margin rising from 4.4% in 2021 to 8.2% by 2027E—highlighting improving operational efficiency.

Costco’s EBITDA of $10.7B in 2023 is substantially higher than BJ’s ($0.93B) and Target ($6.6B), though Walmart remains the industry leader ($30.1B). However, Costco’s margins are increasing more rapidly, with a projected EBITDA margin advantage over both Target and BJ’s by 2027E.

Costco trades at a premium valuation, evidenced by its consistently high PE ratio (38-53x) and sector-leading EV/EBITDA multiples—over 30x in 2025E versus sub-15x for Target, BJ’s, and Walmart. This premium reflects Costco’s strong membership model, high renewal rates, and resilience in various economic cycles.

In summary, Costco’s superior growth, expanding profitability, and operational discipline have driven strong investor confidence, resulting in premium market multiples. While Walmart remains larger, and Target offers value, Costco’s differentiated warehouse model and financial momentum position it as a leader in value creation among its wholesale and big-box retail peers.
