Valuation Overview: Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN)

Amazon’s current valuation profile reflects strong expected growth, but also a premium relative to peers. As of 2024, Amazon trades at 18.98x forward EV/EBITDA, above Walmart (13.64x) and Google (17.24x), but below Microsoft (26.1x). On a P/E basis, Amazon’s 2024E multiple is 38.8x, and is projected to decline to 31.7x (2025E) and 27.2x (2027E), reflecting rapid earnings growth (2023-2027 EPS CAGR >30%).

From a profitability perspective, Amazon’s EBITDA margin is expanding sharply, projected to reach 23.1% in 2025 and above 40% by 2026-2027. This is converging with, and even exceeding, the margins of peers like Google and Microsoft. Amazon’s EBITDA is expected to surpass $230 billion by 2027, outpacing Microsoft and Google in absolute dollar terms.

Relative to its peer set, Amazon is currently more expensive than Walmart and Google on an EV/EBITDA basis but trades at a discount to Microsoft. The premium to Walmart is justified by Amazon’s much higher growth rate (revenue CAGR 11% vs. low single digits for Walmart) and margin expansion. Compared to Google, Amazon’s higher multiple is supported by its superior scalability and growth in both e-commerce and cloud.

Fair Value Assessment: While Amazon’s shares trade at a premium to most peers, this is justified by its accelerating margin profile and robust top-line growth. With EBITDA margins set to expand and earnings multiples declining as profitability grows, Amazon’s current valuation appears fair, if not modestly attractive on a long-term basis. Upside remains supported by continued operating leverage and leadership in high-growth sectors. Investors should expect modest multiple compression as Amazon matures, but ongoing EPS growth supports a positive long-term fair value outlook.
