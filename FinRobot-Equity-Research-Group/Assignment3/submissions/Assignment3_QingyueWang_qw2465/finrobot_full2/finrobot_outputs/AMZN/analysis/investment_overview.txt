Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) delivered robust financial performance in 2023, with revenue growing 11.8% year-over-year to $575 billion. This momentum is expected to continue, with consensus forecasts projecting an 11.1% revenue CAGR through 2027. Amazon’s contribution margin expanded steadily from 42.0% in 2021 to 47.0% in 2023, reflecting improved operational efficiency and a growing mix of higher-margin businesses such as AWS and advertising.

EBITDA rebounded sharply in 2023 to $89.4 billion, more than doubling from 2022’s $38.4 billion, as the company recovered from inflationary pressures and cost headwinds. EBITDA margin also improved to 15.6% in 2023 and is projected to rise to 23.1% in 2025 and nearly 47% by 2027, signaling significant profitability gains. Compared to peers, Amazon’s 2023 EBITDA was second only to Microsoft and Google, and its margin improvement trajectory stands out in the sector.

Key growth drivers include continued expansion of AWS, which benefits from secular demand for cloud computing, and increasing advertising revenues. Operational cost control and declining SG&A margins are also contributing to improved earnings leverage. EPS recovered to $2.95 in 2023 and is expected to grow to $8.69 by 2027.

Valuation appears reasonable relative to large-cap tech peers, with a forward PE ratio declining from 51x in 2023 to 27x by 2027 and EV/EBITDA projected to fall to 15.3x in 2025. Overall, Amazon’s growth outlook remains compelling, underpinned by strong market positioning, margin expansion, and ongoing innovation. Continued execution in AWS and advertising, along with operational discipline, should support further upside for long-term investors.
