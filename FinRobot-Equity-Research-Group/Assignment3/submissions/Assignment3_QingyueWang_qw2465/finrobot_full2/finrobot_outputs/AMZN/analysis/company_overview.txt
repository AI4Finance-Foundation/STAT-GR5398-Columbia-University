Amazon.com, Inc. is a global leader in e-commerce, cloud computing, and digital services, with a diversified business model that spans retail, technology infrastructure, and digital media. Founded in 1994, Amazon has evolved from an online bookstore into one of the world’s largest and most influential companies, serving hundreds of millions of customers worldwide.

Amazon’s business model is built on three primary pillars: its online retail marketplace, Amazon Web Services (AWS), and a growing portfolio of subscription and advertising services. The company’s e-commerce platform offers a vast selection of goods, leveraging third-party sellers alongside its own inventory, supported by a world-class logistics and fulfillment network. AWS, Amazon’s cloud computing arm, provides infrastructure-as-a-service (IaaS), platform-as-a-service (PaaS), and a variety of other cloud-based solutions to businesses, governments, and developers globally. In addition, Amazon has established a strong presence in digital content (Prime Video, Kindle, Audible), smart devices (Echo, Alexa), and increasingly, advertising, which is becoming a key profit driver.

Amazon holds a dominant position in both global e-commerce and cloud computing markets. It is the world’s largest online retailer and commands a significant share of the cloud infrastructure market, often competing with Microsoft and Google. Compared to peers, Amazon’s EBITDA has shown robust growth, surpassing Walmart and closely tracking with Microsoft and Google in recent years.

Financially, Amazon demonstrated consistent revenue growth, achieving a CAGR of 11.1% from 2021 to 2027E, with annual revenues projected to reach over $800 billion by 2027. The company’s contribution margin has steadily improved, rising from 42.0% in 2021 to a projected 53.3% in 2027, reflecting operational efficiencies and the higher-margin profile of AWS and advertising. After a challenging 2022, marked by a rare negative EPS due to macroeconomic headwinds and investment cycles, Amazon rebounded in 2023 with strong profitability and continues to expand its EBITDA margin, expected to rise from 15.6% in 2023 to 46.7% by 2027E. The company’s valuation, as measured by EV/EBITDA, remains competitive within its peer set.

In summary, Amazon’s diversified business model, scale, and ongoing innovation position it as a market leader with strong growth prospects and improving profitability, underpinned by its expansion in high-margin, technology-driven segments.
