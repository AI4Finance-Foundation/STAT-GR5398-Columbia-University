**Key Investment Risks for Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN):**

- **Margin Volatility and Forecast Uncertainty:** Significant EBITDA margin swings (e.g., 15.8% in 2021, down to 7.5% in 2022, then up to 43.7% forecasted for 2025E) suggest uncertainty in cost control, operational efficiency, or changes in business mix, making future profitability unpredictable.

- **Aggressive Forecast Assumptions:** Projected EBITDA and margins for 2025–2027 (e.g., EBITDA margin rising to 46.7% in 2027E) are substantially higher than historical levels and peers, raising concerns about the realism of forecasts and potential for investor disappointment if not achieved.

- **Competitive Pressure:** Peer metrics show that Amazon’s EV/EBITDA multiples are in line with or slightly higher than competitors (e.g., 18.98x in 2024 vs. GOOGL at 17.24x and MSFT at 26.11x), while EBITDA size and growth are closely matched, indicating persistent competitive threats from major tech and retail firms.

- **Earnings Volatility:** EPS turned negative in 2022 (-$0.27), reflecting exposure to earnings shocks from operational, macroeconomic, or regulatory factors, and highlighting potential for future bottom-line volatility.

- **Valuation Risk:** Despite volatile earnings and operational results, Amazon’s PE and EV/EBITDA multiples remain elevated (e.g., 31.7x PE in 2025, 18.98x EV/EBITDA in 2024), exposing investors to downside risk if growth expectations are not met or if market multiples compress.
