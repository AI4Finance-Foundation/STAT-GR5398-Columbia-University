Intuitive Surgical Inc. (ISRG) is the clear leader in the robotic-assisted surgery market, consistently outperforming peers such as Edwards Lifesciences (EW), Medtronic (MDT), and Stryker (SYK) in terms of revenue growth and margins. ISRG’s revenue has grown at a robust 15.2% CAGR from 2021 to 2027E, substantially outpacing typical medtech industry growth rates and reflecting strong adoption of the da Vinci surgical system. In comparison, peer companies like Medtronic and Stryker, while larger in absolute EBITDA, have not matched ISRG’s top-line growth rate.

ISRG’s EBITDA margin, while lower than some peers historically, is projected to improve significantly—rising from 30% in 2023 to nearly 47% by 2027E. This margin expansion is driven by strong operating leverage and disciplined SG&A management (SG&A margin declining from 27.6% in 2023 to 22.2% in 2027E). Stryker and Medtronic, although generating higher EBITDA in absolute terms, maintain lower growth and are more diversified, lacking ISRG’s focus and pricing power in the robotic surgery space.

From a valuation perspective, ISRG trades at a premium, with EV/EBITDA multiples consistently in the 47–65x range (2022–2024), far above MDT (16–18x) and SYK (24–30x). This reflects market expectations for sustained high growth and margin expansion. However, this premium also exposes ISRG to higher expectations and potential downside if growth slows.

In summary, ISRG stands out for its strong revenue growth, improving profitability, and focus on a high-growth niche, justifying its valuation premium over diversified medtech peers. Continued execution and innovation will be crucial for ISRG to maintain its leadership and premium valuation.
