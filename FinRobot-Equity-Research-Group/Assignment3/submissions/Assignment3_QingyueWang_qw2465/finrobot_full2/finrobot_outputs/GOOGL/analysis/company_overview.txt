Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) is the parent company of Google and a diversified technology conglomerate, operating in digital advertising, cloud computing, consumer electronics, and emerging technology investments. The company’s business model is predominantly driven by its core advertising platforms—Google Search, YouTube, and the Google Display Network—which generate the majority of Alphabet’s revenue through targeted digital ads. In addition, Alphabet has expanded into Google Cloud, offering infrastructure, platform services, and productivity tools to businesses, which is now a significant growth driver. Other Bets, such as Waymo (autonomous vehicles), Verily (life sciences), and various AI initiatives, represent Alphabet’s commitment to innovation and long-term diversification.

Alphabet enjoys a dominant market position, particularly in online search (over 90% global market share) and digital video via YouTube. Google Cloud has established itself as the third-largest global cloud provider, behind Amazon Web Services and Microsoft Azure, growing steadily within a rapidly expanding industry. Alphabet’s integrated ecosystem—Android, Chrome, Google Maps, and hardware products like Pixel devices—enhances user engagement and data collection, further strengthening its advertising business.

Financially, Alphabet continues to deliver robust top-line growth, with revenue rising from $257.6 billion in 2021 to $307.4 billion in 2023, and is projected to reach $402.9 billion in 2025, reflecting a CAGR of 11.8%. Contribution margin remains strong, improving from 56.9% in 2021 to a projected 62.7% in 2027, indicating operational efficiency and resilient profitability. EBITDA has rebounded after a dip in 2022, reaching $97.97 billion in 2023 and is expected to surpass $179.9 billion by 2025. Compared to peers, Alphabet’s EBITDA is competitive, outpacing Amazon and Meta, and rivaling Microsoft, while its EV/EBITDA valuation remains in line with sector norms.

Recent performance highlights Alphabet’s ability to maintain leading margins—EBITDA margin is forecasted to exceed 44% by 2025—while controlling SG&A expenses, projected to decline as a percentage of revenue. Earnings per share are set to grow from $5.84 in 2023 to $10.91 in 2025, underscoring strong shareholder returns. Despite macroeconomic headwinds and intensifying competition, Alphabet’s scale, innovation engine, and diversified revenue streams fortify its market leadership and support continued profitable growth.
