Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) has been making headlines for a string of significant developments that reinforce its position as both a technology and market leader. Most notably, Google recently secured a landmark $200 billion, five-year deal with Anthropic, a leading AI startup, to provide cloud services and chips. This deal is expected to deliver a major boost to Google Cloud’s revenue and will likely strengthen Google’s competitive positioning in AI infrastructure, directly challenging rivals like Amazon and Microsoft.

In addition, Google is innovating its core search business by integrating richer AI-powered features, including expert advice from online forums like Reddit. This aims to enhance user engagement and maintain Google’s dominance as the internet’s primary gateway, even as it faces evolving competition from AI-native search tools.

Financially, Alphabet continues to outperform. The company posted a robust 13.9% revenue growth in 2024, with projected double-digit annual growth through 2027. Margins are improving across the board; EBITDA margin is forecast to hit 51.7% by 2027, up from 31.9% in 2023, reflecting operating leverage and successful cost control, especially in SG&A. Alphabet’s earnings per share are projected to more than double from $5.84 in 2023 to $13.01 by 2027.

Relative to peers, Alphabet’s forward EV/EBITDA remains competitive, and the company is outpacing Amazon and Meta in profitability growth. Meanwhile, retail sentiment is broadly bullish, especially on Polymarket, though Reddit sentiment is more cautious.

Investment implications: Alphabet’s aggressive AI moves, improving financials, and strategic cloud partnerships position it as a premier long-term growth stock. The Anthropic deal, in particular, could unlock significant upside for Google Cloud. While competition remains a risk, Alphabet’s scale, innovation, and margin expansion make it attractive for growth-oriented investors.
