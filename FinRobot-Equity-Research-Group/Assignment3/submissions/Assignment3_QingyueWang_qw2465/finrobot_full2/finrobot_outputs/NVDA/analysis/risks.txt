- **Valuation Risk:** NVIDIA’s EV/EBITDA and PE ratios are significantly higher than peers (e.g., 2023 EV/EBITDA: NVDA 81.0 vs. AVGO 18.2, INTC 22.5), indicating that the stock is priced for perfection and vulnerable to sharp corrections if growth expectations are not met.

- **Growth Sustainability Risk:** The financial projections assume extremely high revenue and EBITDA growth (2024–2026 revenue CAGR: 68.3%), which may be difficult to sustain due to increasing competition, technology shifts, or market saturation.

- **Competitive Risk:** Peer analysis shows major competitors (AMD, AVGO, INTC) are also investing and growing, which could erode NVIDIA's market share, pricing power, and margins, especially as AI and semiconductor markets evolve.

- **Operational Leverage Risk:** Rapid revenue growth is accompanied by a significant increase in cost of operations and tight SG&A margins. Any operational missteps or cost overruns could disproportionately impact profitability due to high fixed costs.

- **Market and Cyclical Risk:** NVIDIA’s revenues are heavily exposed to volatile end markets (AI, data centers, gaming), which are cyclical and sensitive to macroeconomic conditions, regulatory changes, or technology disruptions that could adversely affect demand.
