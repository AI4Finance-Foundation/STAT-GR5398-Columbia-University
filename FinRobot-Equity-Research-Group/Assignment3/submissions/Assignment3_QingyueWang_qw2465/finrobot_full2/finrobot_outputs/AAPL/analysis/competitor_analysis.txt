Apple Inc. (AAPL) remains a strong performer in the technology sector, demonstrating robust financial health relative to its key peers: Amazon (AMZN), Alphabet (GOOGL), and Microsoft (MSFT). Apple's revenue has shown steady growth, with a 2021–2027 CAGR of 3.3%. While recent revenue growth has been modest (2.0% in 2024E, 6.4% in 2025E), Apple’s contribution and EBITDA margins are industry-leading. Contribution margins are projected to rise from 46.2% in 2024 to 49.9% by 2027, and EBITDA margins are expected to expand significantly from 34.5% in 2024 to 44.8% in 2027, indicating operational efficiency and strong profitability.

In EBITDA terms, Apple led the peer group in absolute value through 2023, but projections show Amazon, Google, and Microsoft narrowing the gap or overtaking Apple by 2025–2026. For example, by 2026E, Amazon’s EBITDA ($230B) and Google’s EBITDA ($211B) are projected to surpass Apple’s ($150B), reflecting the faster growth trajectories of these peers, especially in cloud and digital advertising sectors.

Apple’s valuation, as measured by EV/EBITDA, is consistently at a premium to Google and Amazon, and often in line with Microsoft. For example, Apple’s EV/EBITDA is projected at 26.6x in 2024E, versus Amazon’s 19.0x and Google’s 17.2x, reflecting investor confidence in Apple's brand, ecosystem lock-in, and recurring revenue streams.

Overall, Apple maintains leadership in profitability and operational metrics, but faces intensifying competition from peers growing faster in emerging tech verticals. Its premium valuation reflects its brand strength and market position, though the company will need to accelerate innovation and new growth avenues to maintain its edge as industry dynamics evolve.
