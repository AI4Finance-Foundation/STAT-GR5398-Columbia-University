Investment Update: Netflix Inc. (NFLX)

Netflix continues to demonstrate robust financial momentum, extending its leadership in the global streaming market. In 2023, Netflix delivered $33.7 billion in revenue, up 6.7% year-over-year, accelerating to 15.6% growth in 2024, and is projected to maintain double-digit growth through 2025 (CAGR 11.1% through 2027). This strong top-line performance is underpinned by subscriber additions, global expansion, and successful monetization strategies, including the ad-supported tier and password-sharing crackdown.

Profitability remains a key highlight. Contribution margin improved to 46.1% in 2024 and is forecasted to rise further, reaching 51.5% by 2027. Cost discipline is evident, with SG&A margin expected to decline steadily from 13.0% in 2023 to 10.0% in 2027. EBITDA growth is robust, with 2024 EBITDA at $26.3 billion, outpacing major peers such as Disney ($14.6B) and Warner Bros. Discovery ($11.6B). While Netflix’s EBITDA margin reached a sector-leading 67.5% in 2024, it moderates in future estimates (40–41.5%) as investments in original content and technology persist.

EPS growth remains strong, with earnings per share projected to rise from $2.03 in 2024 to $3.08 by 2027. Valuation, at a forward PE of 34.3x (2025E), reflects investor confidence in Netflix’s superior growth and operating leverage, though it trades at a premium to historical levels and some peers.

Outlook: Netflix is well-positioned to benefit from ongoing secular shifts toward streaming, a growing international footprint, and enhanced monetization. The company’s superior scale, expanding margins, and consistent execution reinforce its long-term growth story. Risks include intensifying competition and content costs, but Netflix’s financial performance and market leadership support a positive investment outlook.
