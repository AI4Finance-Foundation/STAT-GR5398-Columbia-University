Amgen Inc. (AMGN) is a leading biotechnology company, competing with peers such as Biogen (BIIB), Gilead Sciences (GILD), and Regeneron (REGN). Based on recent financial metrics, Amgen demonstrates robust revenue growth with a projected CAGR of 9.1% from 2021 to 2027, outpacing typical industry averages. Amgen’s EBITDA performance is notably strong: its EBITDA in 2023 ($14.8B) is higher than all peers, with only Gilead approaching similar levels ($10.5B). This trend continues through 2026, highlighting Amgen’s superior operating scale and profitability.

Amgen maintains high EBITDA margins (52.5% in 2023, rising to 56.1% by 2027E), which consistently exceed those of its peers. The company’s contribution margin also remains solid, recovering from a dip in 2024 to 73.8% in 2027E, indicating effective cost management and operational efficiency.

On valuation, Amgen’s EV/EBITDA multiple is relatively stable (around 14x), placing it above Gilead and Biogen in 2021 but broadly in line or slightly below peers by 2025. This reflects a premium for Amgen's scale and stability, though it is not excessively overvalued relative to the group.

Amgen’s SG&A margin is steady and improving, suggesting disciplined spending. Its EPS trajectory is also upward, with a forecasted increase from $7.62 in 2024 to $17.09 by 2027E, indicating strong earnings growth.

In summary, Amgen outperforms its peer group in revenue growth, EBITDA generation, and margin stability. Its valuation is reasonable given its market position and financial strength. The company’s operational consistency and scale make it a market leader among large-cap biotech peers.
