Company Overview

Amgen Inc. is a leading biotechnology company focused on the discovery, development, manufacturing, and commercialization of innovative human therapeutics. The company’s business model centers on leveraging advanced science and biotechnology to address serious illnesses, primarily in the areas of oncology, nephrology, bone health, cardiology, inflammation, and neuroscience. Amgen generates revenue through the sale of proprietary biologic medicines and biosimilars, with a global footprint that includes both established and emerging markets.

Amgen’s diverse product portfolio includes some of the world’s best-selling biologic drugs, such as Enbrel (for rheumatoid arthritis and other autoimmune diseases), Prolia and XGEVA (for bone health), and Repatha (for cholesterol management), among others. The company continues to invest heavily in research and development to expand its pipeline, including biologics and biosimilar products that address high unmet medical needs.

Market position remains strong for Amgen, as reflected in its robust financial metrics. In 2023, Amgen reported revenues of $28.2 billion, up 7.1% versus 2022, and expects to see continued growth with a CAGR of 9.1% projected through 2027, reaching over $42.5 billion in revenue. Amgen’s EBITDA in 2023 was $14.8 billion, surpassing key biotech peers such as Biogen, Gilead, and Regeneron, and is forecasted to rise further, highlighting Amgen’s operational scale and efficiency. Contribution margins and EBITDA margins are consistently strong, with the latter rebounding to over 53% by 2025 and projected to exceed 56% by 2027. This reflects effective cost management and a high-value product mix. Amgen’s SG&A margin is also trending lower, indicative of improving operational leverage.

Recent performance has been marked by accelerating top-line growth and a resilient bottom line. While 2024 EPS is temporarily lower due to acquisition-related and integration costs, projections show a strong rebound to above $14 in 2025 and further growth thereafter. Amgen’s valuation, with a PE ratio in the low 20s and an EV/EBITDA multiple in line with or slightly above peers, reflects investor confidence in its future earnings potential and robust cash flow generation.

Overall, Amgen’s strong market position, diversified product portfolio, and consistent financial performance underpin its status as a global biotechnology leader, well-positioned for sustained growth and value creation in the coming years.

Investment Update

Amgen Inc. (AMGN) delivered robust financial results in 2023, achieving 7.1% revenue growth to $28.2 billion and a significant improvement in EBITDA margin to 52.5%. While revenue growth was steady in recent years, the company is poised for a step-change in 2024, with revenue projected to surge 18.6% to $33.4 billion, driven by the Horizon Therapeutics acquisition and continued strength in its core portfolio.

The company’s contribution margin temporarily dipped in 2024 due to acquisition-related costs but is projected to recover to over 70% in 2025 and beyond. EBITDA is expected to grow at a 9.1% CAGR through 2027, outpacing major peers such as Biogen, Gilead, and Regeneron, and Amgen’s EBITDA consistently exceeds these competitors in absolute terms. The company trades at an EV/EBITDA multiple of around 14x—at parity or below some peers—suggesting reasonable valuation relative to its growth profile.

Key growth drivers include the integration of Horizon’s rare disease portfolio, ongoing expansion of biosimilars, and the success of key products like Repatha and Prolia. Operating leverage is expected to improve, with SG&A margin declining to 17.7% by 2027, supporting further margin expansion.

Near-term headwinds include temporary cost elevation and 2024 EPS dilution, but EPS is forecast to rebound to $14.33 in 2025 and reach $17.09 by 2027. Overall, Amgen is well positioned for sustained growth, margin recovery, and shareholder value creation, supported by a strong pipeline and disciplined expense management. The outlook remains positive, with upside from pipeline execution and further business development.

Valuation

Amgen Inc. (AMGN) currently trades at a 2023 PE ratio of 22.9x, with forward PE declining to 21.7x (2025E) and 19.6x (2027E) as earnings are expected to rebound sharply after a dip in 2024. The company's EV/EBITDA multiple has remained relatively stable, at 14.0x in 2023 and projected at 14.0x in 2024, modestly decreasing to 14.0x and below through 2025. Amgen's EBITDA margins are robust, with a strong recovery forecasted post-2024, reaching 53.1% in 2025E and rising further to 56.1% by 2027E.

Compared to peers, Amgen's EBITDA in 2023 ($14.8B) outpaces Biogen (BIIB, $2.4B), Gilead (GILD, $10.5B), and Regeneron (REGN, $4.7B). On an EV/EBITDA basis, Amgen's 2023 multiple (14.0x) is higher than Gilead (11.4x) but below Biogen (18.4x) and Regeneron (20.0x), indicating a middle-ground valuation relative to large-cap biotech peers. The 2025E EV/EBITDA of 14.0x for Amgen is in line with REGN (13.8x), slightly above BIIB (9.6x), and close to GILD (15.7x).

Amgen’s revenue is projected to grow at a 9.1% CAGR (2023-2027), and EPS is forecast to recover strongly after 2024. The current valuation appears reasonable given Amgen's scale, margin profile, and growth outlook. Relative to peers, Amgen is not trading at a significant premium, with its valuation justified by superior EBITDA generation and improving margins. Fair value appears close to current levels, with modest upside as earnings and margins expand, provided execution on growth drivers continues and integration risks (e.g., from Horizon acquisition) are managed effectively.
