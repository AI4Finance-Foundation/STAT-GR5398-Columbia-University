Company Overview

Applied Materials Inc. (AMAT) is a leading global supplier of equipment, services, and software for the semiconductor, display, and related industries. The company’s business model centers on providing advanced manufacturing solutions that enable the production of semiconductors and display panels—critical components in electronics, computing, communications, and automotive sectors. AMAT’s offerings span wafer fabrication equipment (deposition, etch, ion implantation, and inspection systems), integrated solutions for advanced chipmaking, and comprehensive services including maintenance, upgrades, and process optimization.

Applied Materials serves a diverse, global customer base that includes major semiconductor foundries, logic and memory chip producers, and display manufacturers. Its technological leadership and deep customer partnerships have established it as a core enabler of innovation in the semiconductor value chain. The company’s scale, broad portfolio, and R&D investments allow it to address both leading-edge and mature technology nodes, supporting customers through rapid technology transitions such as the shift to advanced process nodes, 3D architectures, and heterogeneous integration.

In terms of market position, AMAT is one of the largest players in the semiconductor capital equipment industry, competing with companies like ASML, KLA Corporation, and Lam Research (LRCX). In recent years, Applied Materials has demonstrated strong operating performance. Revenue grew from $23.1 billion in fiscal 2021 to $26.5 billion in 2023, with a projected CAGR of approximately 5.3% through 2027. Contribution margin has remained robust, reaching 46.7% in 2023 and forecasted to improve to over 51% by 2027. EBITDA has also increased from $7.4 billion in 2021 to $8.5 billion in 2023, with margins expanding from 32% to nearly 47% by 2027, reflecting strong operational leverage and disciplined cost control.

Compared to peers, AMAT’s EBITDA is competitive, closely matching or exceeding that of KLA and Lam Research, although ASML remains the technology leader in lithography with higher absolute EBITDA. AMAT’s EV/EBITDA multiple has fluctuated but remains in line with the sector’s valuation trends, suggesting the market recognizes its growth prospects and profitability.

In summary, Applied Materials’ strength lies in its broad technology portfolio, strong market position, and ability to capture value from ongoing semiconductor industry growth. The company’s consistent financial performance, expanding margins, and strategic positioning in critical process technologies underpin its leadership and continued relevance in the rapidly evolving semiconductor landscape.

Applied Materials Inc. (AMAT) Investment Update

Recent Financial Performance

Applied Materials has delivered steady financial performance over the last several years. Revenue increased from $23.1 billion in 2021 to $27.2 billion in 2024, representing a CAGR of 5.3%. Growth was particularly strong in 2022 (+11.8%), with more moderate increases in subsequent years. Contribution margin has improved from 47.3% in 2021 to 47.5% in 2024 and is projected to reach 51.7% by 2027, reflecting ongoing operational efficiency and a favorable product mix. EBITDA also rose from $7.4 billion in 2021 to $8.8 billion in 2024, with margins expanding from 32% to 34% and expected to reach 47% by 2027. EPS has steadily improved from $6.47 in 2021 to $8.68 in 2024, with further growth anticipated.

Growth Drivers

AMAT’s performance is supported by robust demand for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, driven by secular trends in AI, advanced computing, and global chip capacity expansions. The company’s focus on R&D and next-generation process technologies has allowed it to capture incremental market share. Margin expansion is aided by cost controls and a shift toward higher-value solutions. Compared to peers, AMAT’s EBITDA remains competitive, and its EV/EBITDA valuation is at a moderate premium to historical levels, but in line with sector standards.

Outlook

Looking ahead, AMAT is expected to continue moderate growth, with revenue projected to exceed $32.8 billion by 2027. Margin expansion and EPS growth remain key themes, underpinned by strong industry fundamentals. However, the pace of revenue growth may moderate as the semiconductor capital equipment market normalizes after recent surges. AMAT’s valuation (2025E EV/EBITDA of 19.3x) is reasonable relative to peers, supporting a positive long-term outlook, though near-term upside may be limited by macro uncertainties and supply chain factors. Overall, AMAT remains well-positioned for sustainable growth and profitability.

Valuation

Applied Materials Inc. (AMAT) is currently trading at a 2024E P/E ratio of approximately 21.4x, with consensus estimates pointing to modest revenue growth (CAGR 5.3%) and improving margins through 2027. EBITDA margins are forecasted to increase from 32% in 2023 to 47% by 2027, reflecting strong operating leverage and disciplined cost control, particularly as SG&A margins decline further.

Comparatively, AMAT’s 2024E EV/EBITDA multiple stands at 17.3x, which is at a discount to ASML (25.4x), and broadly in line with Lam Research (LRCX: 28.2x) and KLA Corp (KLAC: 29.9x). Historically, AMAT’s EV/EBITDA has ranged between 9.8x and 19.3x (2022-2025), currently sitting near the high end of its recent range. Notably, AMAT’s EBITDA generation is competitive, outpacing peers like Lam and KLA, and making strong gains on ASML, whose valuation premium reflects its unique technology leadership.

Looking forward, consensus expects AMAT to grow EBITDA from $8.8 billion in 2024 to over $15 billion by 2027, supporting a healthy EPS CAGR. However, with the current P/E and EV/EBITDA multiples above their five-year averages and closer to the upper end of historic ranges, much of the near-term growth appears priced in.

In sum, while AMAT continues to deliver robust margin expansion and is competitively positioned among semiconductor equipment peers, the current valuation is fair to slightly full relative to historical averages and peer multiples. Upside from here will likely require either further upward estimate revisions or a material re-rating of the broader sector.

Risks

Key Investment Risks for Applied Materials Inc. (AMAT):

  • ▪ Slowing Revenue Growth: Revenue growth has decelerated from 11.8% in 2022 to just 2.5% in 2024, suggesting potential demand headwinds or market saturation in the near term.
  • ▪ Margin Compression Risk: While contribution and EBITDA margins are forecasted to improve, actual 2023-2024 margins have remained stable (~32% EBITDA margin), indicating execution risk in achieving targeted profitability improvements.
  • ▪ Elevated Valuation Relative to Growth: AMAT’s forward PE and EV/EBITDA multiples (PE: ~25x in 2025, EV/EBITDA: ~19x) are relatively high compared to its slowing revenue growth, heightening the risk of multiple contraction if growth does not accelerate.
  • ▪ Intensifying Competition: Peer comparison shows competitors (e.g., ASML) with larger or faster-growing EBITDA, suggesting potential market share loss or price competition that could impact future earnings.
  • ▪ Operating Expense Volatility: SG&A margin has increased from 5.3% in 2021 to 6.6% in 2024, indicating cost pressures that could erode profitability if not well controlled.
