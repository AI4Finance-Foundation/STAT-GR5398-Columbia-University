Applied Materials Inc. (AMAT) is currently trading at a 2024E P/E ratio of approximately 21.4x, with consensus estimates pointing to modest revenue growth (CAGR 5.3%) and improving margins through 2027. EBITDA margins are forecasted to increase from 32% in 2023 to 47% by 2027, reflecting strong operating leverage and disciplined cost control, particularly as SG&A margins decline further.

Comparatively, AMAT’s 2024E EV/EBITDA multiple stands at 17.3x, which is at a discount to ASML (25.4x), and broadly in line with Lam Research (LRCX: 28.2x) and KLA Corp (KLAC: 29.9x). Historically, AMAT’s EV/EBITDA has ranged between 9.8x and 19.3x (2022-2025), currently sitting near the high end of its recent range. Notably, AMAT’s EBITDA generation is competitive, outpacing peers like Lam and KLA, and making strong gains on ASML, whose valuation premium reflects its unique technology leadership.

Looking forward, consensus expects AMAT to grow EBITDA from $8.8 billion in 2024 to over $15 billion by 2027, supporting a healthy EPS CAGR. However, with the current P/E and EV/EBITDA multiples above their five-year averages and closer to the upper end of historic ranges, much of the near-term growth appears priced in.

In sum, while AMAT continues to deliver robust margin expansion and is competitively positioned among semiconductor equipment peers, the current valuation is fair to slightly full relative to historical averages and peer multiples. Upside from here will likely require either further upward estimate revisions or a material re-rating of the broader sector.
