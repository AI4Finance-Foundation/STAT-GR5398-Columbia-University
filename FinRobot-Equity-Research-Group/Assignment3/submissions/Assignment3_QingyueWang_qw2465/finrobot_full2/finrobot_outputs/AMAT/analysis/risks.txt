**Key Investment Risks for Applied Materials Inc. (AMAT):**

- **Slowing Revenue Growth:** Revenue growth has decelerated from 11.8% in 2022 to just 2.5% in 2024, suggesting potential demand headwinds or market saturation in the near term.

- **Margin Compression Risk:** While contribution and EBITDA margins are forecasted to improve, actual 2023-2024 margins have remained stable (~32% EBITDA margin), indicating execution risk in achieving targeted profitability improvements.

- **Elevated Valuation Relative to Growth:** AMAT’s forward PE and EV/EBITDA multiples (PE: ~25x in 2025, EV/EBITDA: ~19x) are relatively high compared to its slowing revenue growth, heightening the risk of multiple contraction if growth does not accelerate.

- **Intensifying Competition:** Peer comparison shows competitors (e.g., ASML) with larger or faster-growing EBITDA, suggesting potential market share loss or price competition that could impact future earnings.

- **Operating Expense Volatility:** SG&A margin has increased from 5.3% in 2021 to 6.6% in 2024, indicating cost pressures that could erode profitability if not well controlled.
