Meta Platforms, Inc. (META) is a global leader in social technology, operating a suite of widely used social media and communication platforms including Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, and Messenger. Its core business model is built around digital advertising, leveraging its massive user base and sophisticated data analytics to deliver targeted ads for businesses of all sizes. In recent years, Meta has also invested heavily in virtual and augmented reality, seeking to pioneer the "metaverse"—a new digital environment for work, play, and social connection.

The company’s products and services span social networking apps, messaging, digital commerce, and increasingly, immersive experiences through Meta Quest VR devices and related software. Its advertising ecosystem, driven by user engagement and advanced targeting capabilities, remains the primary revenue engine, accounting for the vast majority of Meta’s top line.

Meta holds a dominant market position in the global digital advertising space, competing primarily with other tech giants like Alphabet (Google), Snap, and Pinterest. Its scale is evident in its financials: In 2023, Meta reported revenues of $134.9 billion, up 15.7% from the previous year, recovering from a flat 2022. Projected growth remains strong, with revenues forecasted to reach over $200 billion by 2025, representing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 14.3% from 2023 to 2027.

Profitability metrics have also rebounded after a challenging 2022. Contribution margin has remained robust, improving from 78.3% in 2022 to 80.8% in 2023, with further increases expected. EBITDA surged from $37.7 billion in 2022 to $59.1 billion in 2023, and margins are set to climb from 43.8% to over 70% by 2026, reflecting operational efficiencies and disciplined cost control, especially in SG&A expenses.

Meta’s performance, as measured by EBITDA, is second only to Alphabet among peers, and its EV/EBITDA multiples remain in line with industry standards, reflecting healthy investor confidence. EPS growth has also rebounded, with 2023 EPS at 15.19, projected to rise steadily.

In summary, Meta Platforms remains a dominant force in social media and digital advertising, with a strong financial profile, market-leading platforms, and ambitious investments in new technologies positioning it for continued growth and innovation.
