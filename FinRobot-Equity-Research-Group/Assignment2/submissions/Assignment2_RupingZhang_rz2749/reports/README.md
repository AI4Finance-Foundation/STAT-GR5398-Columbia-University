# Reports

This folder contains the generated equity research reports for Assignment 2.

The reports are organized by experiment setting:

- `baseline/`: original or baseline FinRobot-style reports for comparison.
- `config1/`: reports generated with the first upgraded model setting.
- `config2/`: reports generated with the second upgraded model setting.
- `config3_acr/`: final ACR-enhanced reports for the five required companies: AAPL, NVDA, AMD, INTC, and GOOGL.
- `config3_model_experiments/`: model-combination experiments, mainly comparing GPT-only, Claude-only, Gemini-only, and mixed-model ACR settings.

The main final reports are in `config3_acr/`.

The model comparison results are in `config3_model_experiments/`.
