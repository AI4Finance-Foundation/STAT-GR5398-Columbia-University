Microsoft recently delivered strong fiscal third-quarter results, surpassing Wall Street's earnings and revenue estimates. The company reported earnings of $4.27 per share, beating the Zacks Consensus Estimate, and demonstrated robust revenue growth, projected to reach $295.8 billion in 2025. This performance was primarily driven by the continued strength of its Intelligent Cloud segment and significant advancements in Artificial Intelligence. Notably, AI revenue surged by 123% year-over-year, achieving an impressive annual run rate of $37 billion. The company's financial projections indicate consistent revenue and EPS growth, with EBITDA margins expected to expand to 61.6% by 2027, alongside improving operational efficiency.

Despite these positive financial indicators, Microsoft's stock has faced headwinds, underperforming its "Magnificent 7" peers leading into earnings and trading more than 20% below its record high.
