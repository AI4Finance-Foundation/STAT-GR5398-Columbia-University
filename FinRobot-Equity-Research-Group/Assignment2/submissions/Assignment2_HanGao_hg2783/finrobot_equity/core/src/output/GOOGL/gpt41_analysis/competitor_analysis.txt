Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) continues to demonstrate strong financial performance relative to its major technology peers: Apple (AAPL), Amazon (AMZN), Meta Platforms (META), and Microsoft (MSFT). From 2021 to 2027, Alphabet’s revenue exhibits an impressive 11.8% CAGR, outpacing the mature growth rates typically seen in large-cap tech. Its revenue growth remains robust, with double-digit gains projected through 2025, before moderating to mid-single digits.

Alphabet’s EBITDA margin is a key differentiator. Starting at 40.2% in 2021, margins dip in 2022 but rebound sharply, projected to exceed 50% by 2027. This margin expansion is notably higher than Amazon and comparable or superior to Apple and Microsoft, indicating strong operational leverage and efficiency gains. Contribution margins also steadily improve, reaching above 62% by 2027, reflecting cost discipline and effective scaling.

Peer EBITDA data shows Alphabet moving from third position in 2021–2023 to leading the group from 2024 onwards, surpassing both Apple and Microsoft. By 2026, its EBITDA is projected at $210.7 billion, ahead of all listed peers, underscoring its growing cash generation capacity.

On valuation, Alphabet’s EV/EBITDA multiple is generally in line with or slightly below peers, averaging mid-to-high teens, which is lower than Apple and Microsoft, while being above Meta in recent years. This suggests investors see Alphabet as a growth and profitability leader but at a more reasonable premium than the most expensive peers.

In summary, Alphabet stands out for its accelerating margins, industry-leading EBITDA growth, and balanced valuation, positioning it as a top performer among large-cap technology companies. Its financial trajectory indicates continued leadership in scale, profitability, and operational efficiency.
