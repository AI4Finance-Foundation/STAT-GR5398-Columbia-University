- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty**: SEC filings highlight that macroeconomic pressures—such as slower device replacement cycles and weakened discretionary consumer demand—could negatively impact Microsoft’s revenue growth trajectory, particularly in premium-device and consumer segments.

- **Inflationary Pressures**: Persistent inflation could increase costs in components, logistics, and labor, while also eroding consumer purchasing power. This may limit Microsoft’s ability to expand margins, despite current margin discipline and strong EBITDA margin improvements.

- **Supply Chain Disruption**: Ongoing supply chain risks are cited as threats to product availability, cost structure, inventory management, and timely product launches. Disruptions could adversely affect both revenue realization and operating leverage.

- **Competitive Pressure Across Core Segments**: Heightened competition in smartphones, wearables, cloud, AI, and services could challenge Microsoft’s pricing power and market share, impacting both top-line growth and margin expansion despite current strong financial performance.

- **Regulatory and Cybersecurity Risk**: Increased regulatory scrutiny and potential cybersecurity/data-protection incidents are identified in recent SEC disclosures as threats to operational continuity, reputation, and customer trust, which could result in financial or reputational loss.
