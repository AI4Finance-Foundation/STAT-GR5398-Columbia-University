Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) recently reported its first-quarter 2026 results, presenting a mixed but generally positive picture. While some reports indicated the company missed Zacks Consensus EPS estimates, with actual earnings of $1.56 per share against an expected $1.60, other prominent headlines celebrated a "massive double beat," with revenue, profit, and cloud growth all surpassing Wall Street expectations. The official announcement highlighted a robust 17% increase in net sales, reaching $181.5 billion for the quarter.

The strong performance was largely attributed to an "explosion in AI-driven cloud demand" for Amazon Web Services (AWS), which saw its fastest growth in 15 quarters, alongside contributions from high-margin advertising. Looking ahead, Amazon's commitment to AI is evident, with projected 2026 capital expenditures topping $200 billion. This investment is expected to fuel continued growth, with revenue projected to reach $797.9 billion in 2026, up from $716.9 billion in 2025.
