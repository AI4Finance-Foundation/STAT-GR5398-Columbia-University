Meta Platforms Competitor Analysis

Meta Platforms operates in the large-cap technology sector alongside Apple, Amazon, Alphabet, and Microsoft, competing across digital advertising, cloud infrastructure, and AI-driven platforms. The following analysis benchmarks Meta against these peers using available financial data.

Revenue and Growth: Meta reported 2025 revenue of approximately $201 billion, representing a 22.2% year-over-year increase. While this growth rate is strong relative to peers, Meta's absolute revenue base remains smaller than Amazon and Microsoft. Forward estimates project Meta's growth moderating to 6% and 4% in 2026 and 2027, respectively, which is in line with the broader industry maturation trend.

EBITDA and Profitability: Meta's EBITDA of roughly $104.5 billion in 2025 trails Apple, Amazon, Alphabet, and Microsoft in absolute terms. However, Meta's EBITDA growth trajectory from $37.7 billion in 2022 to over $104 billion in 2025 is the most dramatic improvement among peers, reflecting aggressive cost discipline and operating leverage. Forward estimates project Meta reaching $128.7 billion in EBITDA by 2026, narrowing the gap with competitors.

Valuation: Meta's EV/EBITDA multiple of approximately 16.4x in 2025 is the lowest among its peer group, which includes Apple at 27.0x and Microsoft at 23.6x. This relative discount may suggest the market is pricing in execution risk around Meta's heavy capital investments, though it also presents a potential value opportunity compared to peers.

Margin Expansion: Meta's contribution margin is projected to rise from 82% in 2025 to 85% by 2027, and SG&A as a percentage of revenue continues to decline, signaling improving operational efficiency that compares favorably across the peer group.
