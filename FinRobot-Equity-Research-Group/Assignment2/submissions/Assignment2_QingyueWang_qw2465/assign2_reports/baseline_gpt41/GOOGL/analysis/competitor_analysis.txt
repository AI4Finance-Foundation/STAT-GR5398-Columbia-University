Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) remains a dominant force in the technology sector, but faces intensifying competition from Meta Platforms (META) and Microsoft (MSFT). Alphabet’s revenue is projected to grow at an impressive 11.8% CAGR through 2027, outpacing typical large-cap peers, driven by its core advertising business and expansion in cloud and AI services. Its EBITDA margin is also forecast to expand significantly, from 31.9% in 2023 to 51.7% by 2027, indicating strong operating leverage and efficiency improvements.

Comparatively, Alphabet’s EBITDA performance is robust. In 2023, its EBITDA ($97.9B) was higher than Meta ($59.1B) and slightly below Microsoft ($105.1B). However, by 2026, Alphabet is projected to surpass Microsoft, reaching $210.7B vs. Microsoft’s $188.0B and Meta’s $128.7B, underscoring Alphabet’s scale and profitability trajectory.

On valuation, Alphabet’s EV/EBITDA multiples have generally traded between those of Meta and Microsoft. For 2024, its multiple (17.2x) is nearly identical to Meta’s (17.1x) and lower than Microsoft’s (26.1x), suggesting a more attractive risk/reward given its growth and margin expansion. Looking ahead, Alphabet’s forward multiples rise to 21.2x by 2025, reflecting market expectations for continued earnings growth and premium positioning.

Alphabet’s margin profile is improving, with Contribution Margin expected to rise from 56.6% in 2023 to 62.7% in 2027, and SG&A margin trending down, signaling disciplined cost management. While Microsoft maintains a premium valuation due to its diversified enterprise exposure and Meta is aggressive on cost controls, Alphabet’s balance of growth, margin expansion, and reasonable valuation positions it favorably among large tech peers.
