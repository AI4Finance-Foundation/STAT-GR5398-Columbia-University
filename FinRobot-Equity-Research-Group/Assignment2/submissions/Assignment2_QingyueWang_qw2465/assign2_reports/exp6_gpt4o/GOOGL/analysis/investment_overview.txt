Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) has demonstrated a solid financial performance over the past few years, with a consistent upward trajectory in revenue. In 2023, the company reported a revenue of $307.4 billion, marking an 8.7% growth from the previous year. This growth is projected to continue, with estimates for 2024 and 2025 showing revenue increases to $350.0 billion and $402.9 billion respectively, reflecting a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 11.8%.

The company's contribution margin has improved, rising from 55.4% in 2022 to 56.6% in 2023, and is expected to reach 62.7% by 2027. This indicates enhanced operational efficiency. The EBITDA margin has also seen a significant improvement from 30.1% in 2022 to 31.9% in 2023, with future expectations of it reaching 51.7% by 2027. Such improvements suggest strong profitability potential.

Alphabet’s earnings per share (EPS) saw a notable rise from $4.59 in 2022 to $5.84 in 2023, with forecasts indicating further growth to $13.01 by 2027. The PE ratio, while slightly elevated, remains competitive within the industry at 23.9 in 2023, with a gradual decrease expected, indicating potential stock value appreciation.

In the peer comparison, Alphabet's EBITDA is competitive, surpassing Meta but slightly trailing Microsoft. However, Alphabet’s EV/EBITDA ratio positions it favorably against its peers, suggesting that the stock may still offer value.

Overall, Alphabet’s strong revenue growth, improving margins, and competitive position against peers present a positive investment outlook. The company appears well-positioned to capitalize on growth opportunities, making it an attractive option for investors seeking long-term value.
