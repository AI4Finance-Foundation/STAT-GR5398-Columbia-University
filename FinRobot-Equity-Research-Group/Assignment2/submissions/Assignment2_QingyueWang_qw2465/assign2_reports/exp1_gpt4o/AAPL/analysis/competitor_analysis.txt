Apple Inc. is a dominant player in the technology sector, rivaled closely by companies like Google (Alphabet Inc.) and Microsoft. Analyzing Apple against these peers on key metrics reveals some insights into its competitive positioning.

Revenue Growth: Apple's revenue growth appears modest with a Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR) of 3.3%. The company experienced a slight dip of -2.8% in 2023 but is projected to recover and grow steadily. In contrast, peers like Google and Microsoft have shown more robust growth patterns, particularly evident in their expanding EBITDA figures.

EBITDA and Margins: Apple's EBITDA has consistently been strong, showing resilience even with slight fluctuations, such as the dip in 2023. Apple's EBITDA margins are also noteworthy, with an upward trend expected to reach 44.8% by 2027, indicating efficient operations. Compared to Google's and Microsoft's EBITDA, Apple had a head start in 2021 but faces increasing competition, particularly from Google, whose EBITDA is projected to surpass Apple's by 2025.

Valuation Multiples: Apple's PE ratio is high, reflecting strong investor confidence and the premium nature of its brand. However, its EV/EBITDA ratio is generally higher than Google's but comparable to Microsoft's, suggesting Apple's valuation is relatively expensive compared to Google, especially as Google's growth accelerates.

In summary, while Apple maintains a strong financial position and brand appeal, its growth is more measured compared to the aggressive expansion seen in Google and Microsoft. Apple's focus on margin improvement and product innovation will be crucial to maintain its competitive edge, particularly as Google enhances its EBITDA performance and Microsoft continues to diversify its offerings.
