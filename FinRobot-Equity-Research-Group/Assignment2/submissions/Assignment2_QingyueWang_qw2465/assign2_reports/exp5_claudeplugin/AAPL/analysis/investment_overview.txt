Apple Inc. (AAPL) Investment Update

Recent Financial Performance

Apple delivered solid fiscal 2025 results, with revenue growing 6.4% year-over-year to $416.2 billion, accelerating meaningfully from the modest 2.0% growth recorded in fiscal 2024. EBITDA expanded to $144.4 billion, representing a margin of 34.7%, reflecting continued operational discipline. Contribution margins have steadily improved from 41.8% in 2021 to 46.9% in 2025, underscoring Apple's pricing power and favorable services mix shift. EPS reached $7.49 in fiscal 2025, recovering strongly from the relatively flat 2022-2024 period.

Growth Drivers

The primary growth catalyst remains Apple's expanding Services segment, which continues to drive margin accretion and reduce dependence on hardware cycles. The anticipated integration of Apple Intelligence across its device ecosystem represents a meaningful long-term monetization opportunity. SG&A efficiency improvements are also notable, with the margin expected to decline from 6.6% to 5.1% through 2027, freeing capital for reinvestment and shareholder returns.

Outlook and Valuation

Consensus estimates project revenue reaching $481.7 billion by 2027, implying a three-year CAGR of 3.3%. EBITDA margins are forecast to expand significantly toward 44.8% by 2027, well above historical levels, suggesting meaningful operating leverage ahead. The stock currently trades at an EV/EBITDA of approximately 27.0x, at a premium to peers Alphabet and Microsoft. The forward PE of 32.4x reflects investor confidence in the AI-driven earnings acceleration thesis. While near-term macro headwinds and competitive pressures warrant monitoring, Apple's ecosystem stickiness and margin trajectory support a constructive long-term view.
