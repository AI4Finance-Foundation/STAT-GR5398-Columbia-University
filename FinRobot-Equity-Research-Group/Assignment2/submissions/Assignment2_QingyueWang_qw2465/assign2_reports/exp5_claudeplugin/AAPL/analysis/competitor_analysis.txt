Apple Inc. Competitor Analysis

Apple competes directly with Alphabet (GOOGL) and Microsoft (MSFT) across hardware, software, and services ecosystems. Across key financial metrics, each company demonstrates distinct strengths and growth trajectories worth examining closely.

In terms of absolute EBITDA, Apple led the peer group through 2023, generating $129.2 billion compared to Alphabet's $98.0 billion and Microsoft's $105.1 billion. However, the competitive landscape has shifted meaningfully. By 2025, both Alphabet and Microsoft surpassed Apple in EBITDA, posting $180.0 billion and $160.2 billion respectively, against Apple's $144.4 billion. Looking at 2026 estimates, the gap widens further, with Alphabet projected at $210.7 billion and Microsoft at $188.0 billion, while Apple trails at $150.4 billion. This suggests that Alphabet and Microsoft are capturing operating leverage at a faster pace, likely driven by AI-related monetization and cloud growth.

On a valuation basis, Apple trades at a premium EV/EBITDA multiple relative to Alphabet, though Microsoft has historically commanded comparable or higher multiples. Apple's EV/EBITDA expanded from 19.1x in 2022 to approximately 27.0x in 2025, reflecting strong investor confidence in its ecosystem and services margin expansion. Alphabet, by contrast, trades at a more modest 21.2x in 2025 despite superior EBITDA growth, suggesting it may offer better relative value.

Apple's revenue CAGR of 3.3% lags behind its peers' growth rates, and with contribution margins plateauing in the high 40s, sustaining its premium valuation will require meaningful acceleration in services revenue or new product categories to remain competitive.
