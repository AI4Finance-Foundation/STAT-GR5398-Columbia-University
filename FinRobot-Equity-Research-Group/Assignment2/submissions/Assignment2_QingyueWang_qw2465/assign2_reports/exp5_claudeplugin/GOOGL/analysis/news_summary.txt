Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) delivered a standout performance in Q1 2026, emerging as the clear winner among Big Tech on a major earnings day that also included results from Meta, Amazon, and Microsoft. Alphabet posted earnings of $5.11 per share, nearly doubling the consensus estimate of $2.64, compared to $2.81 a year ago. The strong beat drove the stock to outperform peers on the day, with investors rewarding Alphabet's disciplined approach to AI capital deployment over competitors like Meta, which faced scrutiny for its aggressive spending guidance.

Google Cloud was a headline driver, surpassing $20 billion in quarterly revenue for the first time, representing a 63% year-over-year increase. Notably, management flagged that growth was capacity-constrained, suggesting demand meaningfully exceeds current infrastructure, a bullish signal for future quarters as new data center capacity comes online. Search is also undergoing a structural evolution, with Alphabet repositioning it as a transaction engine through AI Mode, and the company signaling openness to introducing advertising within its Gemini AI platform, which could unlock a significant new revenue stream.

From a financial trajectory standpoint, EBITDA margins are projected to expand from 44.7% in 2025 to 51.7% by 2027, reflecting strong operating leverage. Revenue is forecast to grow at an 11.8% CAGR through 2027, with EPS climbing toward $13. The forward EV/EBITDA of roughly 21x remains at a modest premium to Meta but a meaningful discount to Microsoft.

Retail sentiment is broadly positive with a buzz score of 79.5 and 58% bullish positioning on average, though divergence across platforms warrants monitoring. Overall, Alphabet presents a compelling investment case anchored in AI monetization, cloud growth, and expanding margins.
