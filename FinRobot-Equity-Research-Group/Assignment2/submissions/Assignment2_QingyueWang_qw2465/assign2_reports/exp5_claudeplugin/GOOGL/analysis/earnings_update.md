### Analyst commentary

Alphabet's fiscal 2025 results present a mixed picture relative to the prior internal model estimate row, with top-line revenue and earnings per share both tracking inline while EBITDA and EBITDA margin came in below that reference point. The variance at the profitability level suggests that cost or mix dynamics ran somewhat ahead of what the estimate row had anticipated, even as the revenue trajectory held broadly on track—a pattern worth monitoring as the company continues to invest across its cloud, AI, and advertising infrastructure. Looking ahead, the inline revenue and EPS outcomes support the durability of Alphabet's core demand environment, and the EBITDA variance does not, on its own, alter the long-term investment thesis. Analysts will reasonably focus on operating expense cadence and segment-level margin progression in subsequent periods to assess whether the profitability gap relative to the estimate row narrows as investment cycles mature. Overall, the results reflect a company navigating a period of elevated reinvestment, and the headline revenue resilience provides a constructive foundation from which to evaluate forward estimates.
