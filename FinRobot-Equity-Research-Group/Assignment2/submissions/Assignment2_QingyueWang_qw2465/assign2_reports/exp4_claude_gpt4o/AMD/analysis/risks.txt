# AMD Investment Risk Analysis

## Key Investment Risks

- **Severe NVIDIA Competitive Displacement**: NVDA's EBITDA is projected to reach **$382B by 2027 vs. AMD's $8.6B** — a 44x gap — suggesting AMD risks being structurally marginalized in the AI GPU market despite its own growth trajectory

- **Aggressive Margin Expansion Assumptions**: Estimated EBITDA margins jump dramatically from **21.0% (2025A) to 39.0-42.0% (2025E-2027E)**, representing an unrealistic near-doubling that may signal overly optimistic modeling and significant downside earnings risk if execution falters

- **Revenue Growth Deceleration**: Projected revenue growth decelerates sharply from **34.3% (2024) to 5-6% (2026E)**, raising questions about AMD's ability to sustain premium valuation multiples (P/E ~70-76x) as the growth narrative weakens

- **Historical Earnings Volatility**: EPS has been highly inconsistent — **$2.61 → $0.85 → $0.53 → $1.01 → $2.67** — demonstrating AMD's vulnerability to cyclical downturns, integration costs, and competitive pricing pressure, undermining earnings predictability

- **Elevated Valuation vs. Deteriorating Intel Benchmark**: AMD trades at **EV/EBITDA ~47.8x vs. Intel's 14.5x**, and Intel's projected EBITDA recovery to **$47B by 2027** signals a potential competitive resurgence that could compress AMD's market share and force multiple contraction
