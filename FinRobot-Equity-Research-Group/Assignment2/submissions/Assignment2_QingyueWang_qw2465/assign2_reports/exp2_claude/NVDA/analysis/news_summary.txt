NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) Summary - April 29, 2026

NVIDIA continues to dominate the AI infrastructure narrative heading into its Q1 fiscal results, scheduled for a May 20 conference call. Shares touched fresh all-time highs this week before pulling back roughly 2% to approximately $209.40, still reflecting a strong 20% gain in April alone. The broader AI data center buildout, estimated at $7 trillion in cumulative investment, remains the central tailwind driving investor enthusiasm around the stock.

On the supply side, TSMC's production capacity remains a key constraint on Nvidia's ability to fully meet surging end-market demand, a dynamic investors should monitor closely as it could cap near-term upside even as order books remain robust. Competitor Broadcom reported AI revenue growth of 106% year-over-year, signaling that while NVIDIA leads, competition for AI infrastructure spending is intensifying across the semiconductor landscape.

Financially, NVIDIA's trajectory remains exceptional. Actual 2026 revenue reached approximately $216 billion with EBITDA margins expanding to nearly 67%, and forward estimates project continued growth toward $250 billion in revenue by 2027 with EBITDA margins potentially reaching 73.5%. EPS is forecast to grow from $4.93 in 2026 to $5.88 in 2027, with the forward PE compressing to around 32x, suggesting improving valuation as earnings catch up to price.

Retail sentiment shows elevated buzz at 76.5 out of 100, though bullish conviction is mixed at 53% overall, with Reddit notably more cautious at 38% bullish. Trending sentiment across platforms is falling, which may signal near-term consolidation.

Investment implication: NVIDIA remains a high-conviction long-term holding within AI infrastructure, though supply constraints, moderating revenue growth forecasts of 4 to 6% annually, and softening retail momentum suggest a more measured entry approach near current levels.
