Company Overview

Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) is a global technology conglomerate and the parent company of Google, one of the world's most recognized and widely used digital platforms. Founded in 1998 and restructured under the Alphabet umbrella in 2015, the company operates across several high-impact business segments that collectively define its diversified yet interconnected revenue model.

At its core, Alphabet generates the vast majority of its revenue through Google Services, which encompasses Google Search, YouTube, Google Maps, Google Play, and its suite of advertising products. Search advertising remains the dominant revenue driver, leveraging the company's unmatched data infrastructure and user base to deliver highly targeted, performance-driven marketing solutions to businesses worldwide. Google Cloud, the company's rapidly growing enterprise segment, provides infrastructure, data analytics, and artificial intelligence services to corporate clients, representing an increasingly important pillar of future growth. Beyond these, Alphabet's Other Bets segment houses moonshot ventures in areas such as autonomous driving through Waymo, life sciences via Verily, and various early-stage technology initiatives.

Alphabet occupies a commanding position in the global digital advertising market, competing primarily with Meta Platforms and Microsoft. In terms of EBITDA, Alphabet has demonstrated clear leadership over its peers, generating approximately 180 billion dollars in 2025 compared to Meta's 104.5 billion and Microsoft's 160.2 billion. Its EV/EBITDA multiple of 21.2 in 2025 reflects a premium valuation relative to Meta but remains below Microsoft's historically elevated multiple, suggesting a balanced market perception of growth and stability.

Financially, Alphabet has delivered strong and accelerating performance. Revenue grew from approximately 258 billion dollars in 2021 to nearly 403 billion in 2025, representing a compound annual growth rate of 11.8 percent. Perhaps more impressive is the company's expanding profitability profile. EBITDA margins have climbed meaningfully from 40.2 percent in 2021 to 44.7 percent in 2025, with forward estimates projecting margins approaching 52 percent by 2027, driven by operating leverage, disciplined cost management, and the scaling of higher-margin cloud and AI-related revenues. Contribution margins have similarly trended upward, reaching nearly 60 percent in 2025.

Earnings per share have grown from 5.69 dollars in 2021 to 10.91 dollars in 2025, with estimates pointing toward 13.01 dollars by 2027. The company's SG&A margin has also improved, declining from 15 percent in 2022 to a projected 11 percent by 2027, reflecting ongoing efficiency initiatives. Overall, Alphabet presents a compelling profile of scale, profitability expansion, and durable competitive positioning in both advertising and cloud computing.

Investment Update

Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) Investment Update

Alphabet delivered a strong fiscal 2025, reporting revenue of approximately $403 billion, representing 15.1% year-over-year growth and accelerating from the 13.9% recorded in 2024. EBITDA reached $180 billion, translating to an EBITDA margin of 44.7%, a meaningful expansion from 38.7% in the prior year and a significant recovery from the trough of 30.1% seen in 2022. Earnings per share grew to $10.91, up from $8.13 in 2024, reflecting both operational leverage and disciplined cost management. Contribution margins have steadily improved, reaching 59.7% in 2025, underscoring the inherent scalability of Alphabet's core advertising and cloud businesses.

Key growth drivers include continued strength in Google Search and YouTube advertising, rapid expansion of Google Cloud, and increasing monetization of AI-integrated products across the platform. SG&A as a percentage of revenue has declined to 12.5%, signaling improved operational efficiency following the cost restructuring initiatives undertaken in recent years.

Looking ahead, estimates project revenue growing to approximately $466 billion by 2027, with EBITDA margins expanding further toward 51.7%. EPS is forecast to reach $13.01 by 2027, supporting a declining forward PE ratio from 27.3x to 24.6x, suggesting improving valuation attractiveness over the period.

On a peer basis, Alphabet's EBITDA growth trajectory compares favorably against Meta and Microsoft, with its EV/EBITDA multiple of 21.2x in 2025 remaining at a modest premium to Meta but a meaningful discount to Microsoft's 23.6x, reflecting reasonable relative value.

Overall, Alphabet's combination of durable revenue growth, expanding margins, and AI-driven catalysts supports a constructive investment outlook.

Valuation

Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) Valuation Analysis

Current Valuation Metrics

Alphabet trades at a trailing P/E of approximately 28.7x on 2025 actual earnings of $10.91 per share, with forward multiples compressing to 27.3x and 25.9x on 2025E and 2026E estimates, respectively. The current EV/EBITDA stands at 21.2x on 2025 actuals, a notable premium to its 2022 trough of 13.6x, reflecting the market's renewed confidence in Alphabet's AI-driven growth narrative and sustained margin expansion. EBITDA margins have shown remarkable improvement, rising from 30.1% in 2022 to 44.7% in 2025, with estimates projecting further expansion toward 51.7% by 2027, underpinned by operating leverage across Search, Cloud, and YouTube segments.

Peer Comparison

Relative to peers, Alphabet's EV/EBITDA of 21.2x sits above Meta's 16.4x but meaningfully below Microsoft's 23.6x, suggesting a reasonable middle-ground valuation. In absolute EBITDA terms, Alphabet leads the peer group at $180 billion in 2025, surpassing both Meta at $104.5 billion and Microsoft at $160.2 billion. This operational scale advantage, combined with a more attractive multiple than Microsoft, positions Alphabet favorably on a risk-adjusted basis.

Fair Value Assessment

Given projected EPS of $12.39 in 2026 and applying a peer-adjusted forward P/E of approximately 26x to 27x, a fair value range of $322 to $334 per share appears supportable. Revenue growth moderating to 4 to 6 percent annually through 2027 introduces some caution, but continued margin expansion and strong free cash flow generation provide meaningful downside protection. Overall, GOOGL appears fairly valued to modestly undervalued at current levels.

Risks

# Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) — Key Investment Risks

  • ▪ Revenue Growth Deceleration: Projected revenue growth drops sharply from 15.1% (2025A) to 4.0% (2027E), signaling potential saturation in core advertising markets and raising concerns about sustainable long-term top-line expansion
  • ▪ Valuation Premium vs. Peers: GOOGL's forward EV/EBITDA of 21.2x (2025) trades at a meaningful premium to META (16.4x), leaving limited margin of safety if EBITDA margin expansion targets of ~52% by 2027E are missed or delayed
  • ▪ EBITDA Margin Execution Risk: Projected EBITDA margins assume aggressive expansion from 44.7% (2025A) to 51.7% (2027E), requiring disciplined cost control in an environment of heavy AI infrastructure capex that could pressure Cost of Operations, which already totals $162.5B
  • ▪ Competitive Pressure from Microsoft: MSFT's EBITDA surpassed GOOGL in 2022–2023 and remains closely competitive at $160B (2025A), reflecting intensifying rivalry in cloud and AI-driven search that threatens Google's core advertising dominance
  • ▪ Regulatory & Antitrust Exposure: Alphabet's concentrated revenue dependency on Search and advertising (~$350B+ revenue base) makes it acutely vulnerable to ongoing DOJ antitrust actions, which could structurally disrupt its distribution agreements and monetization model, undermining EPS growth projections of $13.01 by 2027E
