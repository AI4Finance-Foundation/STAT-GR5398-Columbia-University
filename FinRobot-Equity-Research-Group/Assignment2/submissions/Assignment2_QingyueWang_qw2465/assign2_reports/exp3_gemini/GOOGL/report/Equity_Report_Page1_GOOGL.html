Company Overview

Alphabet Inc. is a global technology conglomerate and the parent company of Google. It operates primarily through two segments: Google, which includes Search, YouTube, Android, Chrome, and hardware; and Google Cloud. The company's business model is anchored by its dominant position in digital advertising, where it leverages its massive user base to provide targeted marketing solutions. Additionally, Google Cloud has emerged as a significant growth engine, providing infrastructure and platform services to enterprises globally.

The company maintains a formidable market position, holding a near-monopoly in global search and a leading role in online video via YouTube. Alphabet competes directly with Meta in the advertising space and with Microsoft and Amazon in the cloud computing market. Recent financial performance underscores Alphabet's ability to scale efficiently. Between 2021 and 2025, revenue grew from 257.6 billion to 402.9 billion, representing a compound annual growth rate of 11.8%. Notably, the company saw a significant growth re-acceleration in 2024 and 2025, with annual revenue increasing by 13.9% and 15.1%, respectively.

Profitability metrics have shown consistent improvement. Alphabet's contribution margin expanded from 56.9% in 2021 to 59.7% in 2025, driven by optimized cost structures. EBITDA performance has been particularly strong, rising from 103.5 billion in 2021 to 179.9 billion in 2025. This growth resulted in an EBITDA margin expansion from 40.2% to 44.7% over the same period. Efficiency gains are also evident in the reduction of SG&A as a percentage of revenue, which dropped from 14.1% in 2021 to 12.5% in 2025, with further declines projected through 2027.

From a valuation perspective, Alphabet’s 2025 EV/EBITDA of 21.2x places it at a premium compared to Meta (16.4x) but at a slight discount to Microsoft (23.6x). Earnings per share have nearly doubled from 5.69 in 2021 to 10.91 in 2025. While analysts expect revenue growth to moderate to a range of 4% to 6% between 2025 and 2027, the company is projected to maintain its upward trajectory in EPS, reaching an estimated 13.01 by 2027. Overall, Alphabet remains a high-margin leader with a robust balance sheet and a clear path toward continued earnings expansion.

Investment Update

Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) continues to demonstrate exceptional financial strength, characterized by accelerating revenue growth and significant margin expansion through fiscal year 2025. The company reported 2025 revenue of 402.9 billion dollars, representing a 15.1 percent year-over-year increase. This performance underscores a healthy 11.8 percent compound annual growth rate since 2021, driven by the core search business and scaling cloud operations.

A key highlight of recent performance is the dramatic improvement in profitability. Alphabet’s EBITDA margin expanded from 30.1 percent in 2022 to 44.7 percent in 2025, with projections suggesting it could reach 51.7 percent by 2027. This expansion is largely supported by operational discipline; SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue have consistently declined and are expected to hit 11 percent by 2027. Consequently, earnings per share rose to 10.91 dollars in 2025, up from 8.13 dollars the previous year.

In a peer context, Alphabet’s absolute EBITDA of 179.9 billion dollars in 2025 comfortably exceeds both Microsoft and Meta. While its EV/EBITDA multiple of 21.2x sits at a premium compared to Meta, it remains lower than Microsoft’s 23.6x, offering a compelling valuation for a market leader.

Looking ahead, the outlook remains positive despite a projected moderation in top-line growth to the 4 to 6 percent range. Sustained margin improvements are expected to drive EPS to 13.01 dollars by 2027. With the forward P/E ratio projected to compress from 28.7x to 24.6x over the next two years, Alphabet remains a core holding for investors seeking a blend of growth, massive cash flow generation, and improving operational efficiency.

Valuation

Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) exhibits a transition from high-growth to a high-margin maturity phase. Current metrics show a revenue CAGR of 11.8%, but the standout feature is the aggressive expansion of EBITDA margins. Projections indicate EBITDA margins rising from 38.7% in 2024 to 51.7% by 2027, driven by operational efficiencies and scaled contributions from high-margin segments. This trajectory supports a steady EPS increase, reaching an estimated $13.01 by 2027.

In a peer context, Alphabet’s 2025 EV/EBITDA of 21.2x positions it between Meta (16.4x) and Microsoft (23.6x). While Alphabet maintains the highest absolute EBITDA in the peer group, its valuation multiple has expanded significantly from the 13.6x to 18.0x range seen between 2022 and 2024. This premium is justified by its dominant market position and superior margin profile, though it is tempered by a projected slowdown in revenue growth to the 4% to 6% range in 2026-2027.

From a fair value perspective, Alphabet appears reasonably valued at current levels, though upside may be capped by the decelerating top-line growth. Applying a target P/E multiple of 24x to 25x—consistent with its projected 2027 earnings profile—suggests the market has already priced in much of the anticipated margin improvement. The investment case remains strong due to massive cash flow generation and a projected P/E compression to 24.6x by 2027, but further multiple expansion is unlikely without a re-acceleration in revenue. Investors should view Alphabet as a core value-growth hybrid where total return will be driven by earnings compounding rather than valuation re-rating.

Risks

Based on the provided financial data and peer comparisons for Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL), here are five key investment risks:

* Significant Revenue Growth Deceleration: While revenue growth is strong in 2024A (13.9%) and 2025A (15.1%), the projections show a sharp decline to 5% in 2025E and 4% by 2027E. This suggests the company is entering a low-growth phase that may not support its current valuation.

* Valuation Multiple Expansion Risk: The P/E ratio has expanded from 23.3x in 2024A to 28.7x in 2025A. With projected revenue growth slowing to mid-single digits, there is a high risk of multiple compression if the market re-rates the stock to reflect slower growth.

* Aggressive Margin Expansion Assumptions: The model forecasts EBITDA margins to climb from 38.7% (2024A) to 51.7% (2027E). Relying on such substantial margin expansion to drive EPS growth in a decelerating revenue environment leaves little room for operational errors or unexpected cost increases.

* Relative Overvaluation vs. Peers: GOOGL’s 2025 EV/EBITDA of 21.2x is significantly higher than META’s 16.4x. This premium may be difficult to justify if peer companies maintain higher growth rates or more efficient cost structures.

* Dependency on Drastic SG&A Reduction: To meet earnings targets amidst slowing revenue, the projections assume SG&A margins will drop from 12.5% to 11.0%. Failure to achieve these efficiencies could lead to significant misses in both EBITDA and EPS forecasts.
