Alphabet Inc. demonstrates robust financial health and a dominant competitive position when compared to its primary peers, Meta Platforms and Microsoft. With a revenue CAGR of 11.8% and a projected increase to over 466 billion dollars by 2027, Alphabet maintains a steady growth trajectory. A key highlight is the company's aggressive EBITDA recovery. After a dip in 2022, Alphabet's EBITDA is projected to reach nearly 180 billion dollars by 2025, consistently outpacing Microsoft's 160 billion and Meta's 104 billion for the same period. This scale underscores Alphabet's superior ability to generate operational cash flow.

Alphabet's operational efficiency is also improving significantly. The company is expected to expand its EBITDA margin from 30.1% in 2022 to a projected 51.7% by 2027. This expansion is supported by disciplined cost management, as SG&A margins are forecasted to compress from 15% to 11% over the same timeframe. 

From a valuation perspective, Alphabet occupies a middle ground between its peers. Its 2025 EV/EBITDA multiple of 21.2x is higher than Meta's 16.4x, suggesting that the market assigns a premium to Alphabet's diversified ecosystem and search dominance. However, Alphabet remains more attractively priced than Microsoft, which carries a higher premium at 23.6x. As Alphabet's PE ratio is expected to stabilize in the mid-20s through 2027, the company presents a compelling balance of high-volume profitability and growth. While Meta offers a lower entry multiple, Alphabet's massive EBITDA scale and consistent margin expansion position it as a more stable leader in the big-tech landscape.
