NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) continues to trade at premium valuation metrics, justified by its exceptional growth and leading position in AI and accelerated computing. As of 2024E, NVDA is forecast to deliver $130.5 billion in revenue, with EBITDA of $86.1 billion, implying a robust EBITDA margin of 66.0%. Revenue is projected to grow at a 68% CAGR through 2027, with contribution and EBITDA margins expanding, reflecting strong operating leverage and disciplined cost control.

Relative to peers Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) and Intel (INTC), NVDA’s EBITDA generation is now in a different league. For 2025, NVDA’s projected EBITDA ($86.1B) dwarfs AMD ($7.3B) and INTC ($14.3B). On an EV/EBITDA basis, NVDA trades at 33.8x 2025E, declining to 31.4x in 2026E. While this is above INTC (14.5x 2025E) and in line or below AMD (47.8x 2025E), it reflects NVDA’s outsized growth, margins, and market dominance. P/E ratios are also elevated but compressing rapidly, from 51.8x in 2024E to 35.9x in 2025E and 32.4x in 2027E, as earnings scale.

The current valuation embeds high expectations, but given the unprecedented revenue and profit growth—supported by secular AI demand and technological leadership—NVDA’s premium appears warranted. A fair value assessment, applying a 30–35x forward EBITDA multiple (consistent with leading tech growth stories), supports current trading levels, assuming continued execution and industry tailwinds. Upside risk exists if AI adoption accelerates further; downside risk remains if growth decelerates or competition intensifies. In summary, NVDA’s valuation is rich but justified by its unique growth profile and profitability.
