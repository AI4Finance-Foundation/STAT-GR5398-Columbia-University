**Key Investment Risks for NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) Based on Provided Data:**

- **Valuation Risk:** NVIDIA trades at significantly higher PE and EV/EBITDA multiples compared to peers (AMD, INTC), indicating aggressive market expectations and limited margin for error if growth moderates.

- **Revenue Growth Sustainability:** Recent explosive revenue growth (125.9% in 2024, 114.2% in 2025) is unlikely to be sustained long-term; any slowdown could trigger sharp stock price corrections given high embedded expectations.

- **Margin Compression:** While margins have expanded recently, contribution and EBITDA margins could face pressure from intensifying competition, rising costs, or increased R&D and SG&A needs as market matures.

- **Competitive Landscape:** AMD and Intel are investing heavily in AI and GPU technology; technological disruption or market share loss could erode NVIDIA’s earnings power and valuation premium.

- **Cyclical and Market Demand Risk:** NVIDIA’s revenues are highly exposed to volatile end markets (AI, data centers, gaming); any cyclical downturn or demand normalization could materially impact financial performance and investor sentiment.
