Company Overview

NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) is a global leader in visual computing technologies and artificial intelligence (AI) hardware and software. The company’s business model centers on designing and selling high-performance graphics processing units (GPUs), systems-on-a-chip (SoCs), and related software platforms. While NVIDIA initially built its reputation in the gaming market, it has successfully diversified into data center, AI, professional visualization, and automotive markets, with data center and AI now driving the majority of growth.

NVIDIA’s flagship products include the GeForce line for gaming, Quadro for professional visualization, and the market-leading H100 and A100 GPUs for data centers, which are foundational to AI training and inference workloads. The company’s CUDA software platform and ecosystem enable developers to leverage GPU acceleration for a wide range of scientific, industrial, and enterprise applications, further entrenching NVIDIA’s hardware in critical computing infrastructure. NVIDIA also offers networking products (Mellanox), automotive platforms (NVIDIA DRIVE), and edge computing solutions.

NVIDIA holds a dominant position in the AI and data center GPU market, outpacing major peers such as AMD and Intel in both technological leadership and financial performance. The company’s 2024 revenue surged to $60.9 billion, doubling the prior year and continuing a trajectory of rapid expansion. Forecasts for 2025 and 2026 estimate revenues of $130.5 billion and $215.9 billion, respectively, representing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 68.3% over the period. This explosive growth is driven by surging global demand for AI infrastructure, with NVIDIA’s GPUs widely regarded as the gold standard for training large AI models.

NVIDIA’s profitability has scaled with its revenues. The company’s EBITDA margin expanded from 22.2% in 2023 to 58.4% in 2024, with projected margins exceeding 70% by 2026. Contribution margins have also improved sharply, indicating strong operating leverage. NVIDIA’s earnings per share (EPS) jumped from $0.18 in 2023 to $1.21 in 2024, with further strong gains projected. Relative to peers, NVIDIA boasts superior EBITDA and margin profiles, and although its EV/EBITDA multiple is elevated compared to AMD and Intel, this reflects investor confidence in its growth prospects and market leadership.

In summary, NVIDIA is uniquely positioned at the forefront of the AI and accelerated computing revolution. Its robust product portfolio, software ecosystem, and scale advantages underpin its dominance in high-performance computing markets, with financial performance reflecting both strong demand and operational excellence.

Investment Update

NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) - Investment Update

NVIDIA has delivered a remarkable surge in financial performance, driven by accelerating demand for AI and data center solutions. FY2024 revenues more than doubled year-on-year, reaching $60.9 billion (+125.9% y/y), with further explosive growth projected: sales are expected to climb to $130.5 billion in FY2025 and $215.9 billion in FY2026, implying a 68% CAGR through 2027. This growth has translated into exceptional operational leverage, with EBITDA jumping to $35.6 billion in FY2024 (versus $6.0 billion in FY2023) and projected to reach $144.6 billion by FY2026.

Profitability has improved markedly. Contribution margin rose to 72.7% in FY2024 and is expected to remain above 70% through 2027. EBITDA margin expanded to 58.4% in FY2024 and is forecasted to rise further, reaching 73.5% by 2027. SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue are trending down, highlighting robust scale efficiencies.

NVIDIA’s outperformance is further underscored by peer comparisons: in 2024, NVIDIA’s EBITDA ($35.6 billion) dwarfed AMD ($5.3 billion) and Intel ($1.2 billion). This lead is forecast to widen, with NVIDIA’s 2027 EBITDA ($382 billion) eclipsing both rivals. Valuation, while elevated (2024 EV/EBITDA: 43.5x), is normalizing as earnings scale, with multiples expected to decline to 31.4x by 2026.

Growth drivers include unparalleled leadership in AI GPUs, robust data center demand, and early positioning in generative AI infrastructure. Risks include industry cyclicality and intensifying competition, but NVIDIA’s technical and scale advantages remain significant.

Outlook: NVIDIA remains a high-growth, high-margin leader in AI hardware, with a strong outlook for continued outperformance versus peers. While valuation is above historical norms, ongoing rapid earnings growth supports the investment case.

Valuation

NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) continues to trade at premium valuation metrics, justified by its exceptional growth and leading position in AI and accelerated computing. As of 2024E, NVDA is forecast to deliver $130.5 billion in revenue, with EBITDA of $86.1 billion, implying a robust EBITDA margin of 66.0%. Revenue is projected to grow at a 68% CAGR through 2027, with contribution and EBITDA margins expanding, reflecting strong operating leverage and disciplined cost control.

Relative to peers Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) and Intel (INTC), NVDA’s EBITDA generation is now in a different league. For 2025, NVDA’s projected EBITDA ($86.1B) dwarfs AMD ($7.3B) and INTC ($14.3B). On an EV/EBITDA basis, NVDA trades at 33.8x 2025E, declining to 31.4x in 2026E. While this is above INTC (14.5x 2025E) and in line or below AMD (47.8x 2025E), it reflects NVDA’s outsized growth, margins, and market dominance. P/E ratios are also elevated but compressing rapidly, from 51.8x in 2024E to 35.9x in 2025E and 32.4x in 2027E, as earnings scale.

The current valuation embeds high expectations, but given the unprecedented revenue and profit growth—supported by secular AI demand and technological leadership—NVDA’s premium appears warranted. A fair value assessment, applying a 30–35x forward EBITDA multiple (consistent with leading tech growth stories), supports current trading levels, assuming continued execution and industry tailwinds. Upside risk exists if AI adoption accelerates further; downside risk remains if growth decelerates or competition intensifies. In summary, NVDA’s valuation is rich but justified by its unique growth profile and profitability.

Risks

Key Investment Risks for NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) Based on Provided Data:

  • ▪ Valuation Risk: NVIDIA trades at significantly higher PE and EV/EBITDA multiples compared to peers (AMD, INTC), indicating aggressive market expectations and limited margin for error if growth moderates.
  • ▪ Revenue Growth Sustainability: Recent explosive revenue growth (125.9% in 2024, 114.2% in 2025) is unlikely to be sustained long-term; any slowdown could trigger sharp stock price corrections given high embedded expectations.
  • ▪ Margin Compression: While margins have expanded recently, contribution and EBITDA margins could face pressure from intensifying competition, rising costs, or increased R&D and SG&A needs as market matures.
  • ▪ Competitive Landscape: AMD and Intel are investing heavily in AI and GPU technology; technological disruption or market share loss could erode NVIDIA’s earnings power and valuation premium.
  • ▪ Cyclical and Market Demand Risk: NVIDIA’s revenues are highly exposed to volatile end markets (AI, data centers, gaming); any cyclical downturn or demand normalization could materially impact financial performance and investor sentiment.
