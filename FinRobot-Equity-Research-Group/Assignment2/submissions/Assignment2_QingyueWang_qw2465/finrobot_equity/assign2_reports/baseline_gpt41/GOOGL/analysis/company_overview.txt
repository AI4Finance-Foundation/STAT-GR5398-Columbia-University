Alphabet Inc. is a global technology conglomerate best known as the parent company of Google, with a diversified business model anchored in digital advertising, cloud computing, consumer hardware, and a portfolio of “Other Bets” in moonshot technologies. The company generates the majority of its revenue from advertising through its dominant search engine (Google Search), video platform (YouTube), and network businesses, but continues to invest heavily in Google Cloud, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies such as Waymo (autonomous vehicles) and Verily (life sciences).

Alphabet’s core products and services include Google Search, YouTube, Google Maps, Android, Google Play, and Google Cloud. These platforms collectively reach billions of users worldwide, underpinning Alphabet’s unrivaled scale in the digital ecosystem. Its cloud division, Google Cloud, is a fast-growing business segment, competing closely with Amazon AWS and Microsoft Azure. The “Other Bets” segment, while still a small portion of total revenue, positions Alphabet at the forefront of next-generation innovation.

Market position remains robust. Alphabet is the clear leader in global search and online video, and ranks among the top three players in cloud infrastructure. Its continuous investments in AI—integrated across products and services—are expected to sustain its competitive edge, drive user engagement, and open new monetization avenues.

Financially, Alphabet has demonstrated consistent top-line growth, with a 2021-2025 revenue CAGR of 11.8%. Revenue rose from $258 billion in 2021 to $308 billion in 2023 and is projected to reach $403 billion by 2025. Contribution margin has steadily improved, climbing from 56.9% in 2021 to an estimated 59.7% in 2025. EBITDA has shown significant expansion, from $104 billion in 2021 to a projected $180 billion in 2025, with EBITDA margin expected to widen from 40.2% to 44.7% over the same period. Alphabet’s EBITDA performance consistently outpaces Meta (Facebook’s parent) and is competitive with Microsoft, underscoring strong operational efficiency.

Recent performance highlights a rebound in earnings per share (EPS) to $5.84 in 2023, with further growth projected. While Alphabet’s valuation (EV/EBITDA) is somewhat higher than that of Meta and below Microsoft, it reflects investor confidence in its growth prospects and innovation pipeline.

Overall, Alphabet combines market dominance, financial strength, and a culture of innovation, positioning it as a leading force in technology with strong momentum for continued growth.
