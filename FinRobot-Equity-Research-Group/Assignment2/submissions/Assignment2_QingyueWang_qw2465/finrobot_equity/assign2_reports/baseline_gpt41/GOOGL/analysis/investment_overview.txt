Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) delivered strong financial performance in recent years, demonstrating robust topline growth and margin expansion. Revenues grew at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 11.8% from 2021 to 2025E, rising from $257.6 billion in 2021 to a forecasted $423.1 billion in 2025E. Growth accelerated in 2024 (13.9%) and is expected to remain healthy, albeit moderating to mid-single digits by 2026-2027.

Profitability has improved significantly. EBITDA is projected to more than double from $103.5 billion in 2021 to $206.1 billion in 2025E, with margins expanding from 40.2% to 48.7%. Contribution margins are also rising, reflecting operational leverage and disciplined SG&A spending, which is expected to decline as a percentage of revenue. EPS is forecast to nearly double from 2023 to 2027, driven by both revenue growth and margin expansion.

Key growth drivers include ongoing strength in core advertising, rapid cloud adoption, and emerging opportunities in AI-powered services. Alphabet’s investment in AI and cloud infrastructure is expected to support continued market share gains and open new monetization avenues.

Compared to peers, Alphabet maintains a competitive EBITDA profile, outperforming Meta and closely tracking Microsoft’s growth. However, its EV/EBITDA valuation remains below Microsoft’s (21.2x vs. 23.6x in 2025E), suggesting some upside potential given Alphabet’s margin trajectory.

Outlook: Alphabet is well positioned to sustain double-digit profit growth, supported by resilient core businesses and strategic investments in AI. Margin expansion and disciplined cost control provide additional upside. With its peer-leading scale and improving profitability, Alphabet remains an attractive long-term investment, though investors should monitor evolving competition and regulatory risks.
