Apple Inc. (AAPL) Investment Update

Recent Financial Performance

Apple delivered solid fiscal 2025 results, with revenue growing 6.4% year-over-year to $416.2 billion, rebounding meaningfully from the modest 2.0% growth recorded in fiscal 2024. EBITDA expanded to $144.4 billion, representing a margin of 34.7%, continuing a gradual upward trend from the 33.7% level seen in 2021 and 2023. Earnings per share rose sharply to $7.49 in 2025, up from $6.11 in 2024, reflecting both operational improvement and the company's ongoing share repurchase program. Contribution margins have also improved steadily, reaching 46.9% in 2025, underscoring Apple's pricing power and favorable product mix shift toward higher-margin services.

Growth Drivers

The primary growth catalysts include continued Services segment expansion, which carries structurally higher margins than hardware, and the gradual integration of Apple Intelligence across its device ecosystem. SG&A as a percentage of revenue has begun to decline, signaling improving operating leverage. Estimated figures through 2027 project EBITDA margins expanding notably toward 44.8%, suggesting meaningful efficiency gains ahead.

Peer Positioning and Valuation

Apple trades at an EV/EBITDA multiple of approximately 27.0x on 2025 figures, a premium to Alphabet at 21.2x but broadly comparable to historical norms. However, both Alphabet and Microsoft are projected to grow EBITDA at a faster pace through 2026, which may pressure Apple's relative valuation. Revenue is forecast to grow at a three-year CAGR of 3.3% through 2027, reflecting a mature but resilient business.

Outlook

With EPS estimated to reach $8.93 by 2027 and margins trending higher, Apple remains a high-quality compounder. Investors should monitor Services growth sustainability and competitive AI developments as key variables influencing the forward multiple.
