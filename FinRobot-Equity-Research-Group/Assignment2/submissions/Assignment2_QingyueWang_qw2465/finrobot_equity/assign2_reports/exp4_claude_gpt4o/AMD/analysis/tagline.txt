AMD has demonstrated robust top-line momentum, with revenue compounding at a 20.5% CAGR from 2021 to 2025 and accelerating 34.3% in fiscal 2025, driven by sustained share gains in high-performance computing and AI-oriented data center markets. Margin expansion remains a key forward catalyst, as EBITDA margins are projected to inflect materially from the 21% historical range toward 39-42% by 2025-2027, reflecting improving operating leverage and a favorable product mix shift. Trading at a forward EV/EBITDA of approximately 47.8x, AMD commands a premium to Intel while remaining at a meaningful discount to NVIDIA, positioning the stock as a compelling risk-adjusted opportunity for investors seeking differentiated AI semiconductor exposure.
