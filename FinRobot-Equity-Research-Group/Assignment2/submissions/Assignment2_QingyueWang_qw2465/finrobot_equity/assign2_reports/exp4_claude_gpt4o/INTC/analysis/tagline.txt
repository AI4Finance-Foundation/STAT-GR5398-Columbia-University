Intel Corporation has endured a prolonged revenue contraction of 9.6% CAGR since 2021, with EPS turning deeply negative and EBITDA collapsing to near-zero in 2024 before a partial recovery, underscoring the severity of its ongoing operational restructuring. While forward estimates project modest revenue growth of 5-6% and expanding EBITDA margins approaching 30%, persistent negative earnings and an elevated EV/EBITDA of 14.5x reflect continued investor skepticism relative to peers AMD and NVDA. Intel remains a turnaround story in progress, with improving cost discipline and margin recovery offering cautious optimism, but meaningful competitive repositioning against a rapidly accelerating NVIDIA is still far from assured.
