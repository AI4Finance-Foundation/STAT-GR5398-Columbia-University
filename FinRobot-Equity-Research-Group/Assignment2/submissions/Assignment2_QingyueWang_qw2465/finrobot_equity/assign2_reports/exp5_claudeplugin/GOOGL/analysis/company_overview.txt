Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) is a global technology conglomerate and the parent company of Google, one of the most dominant and recognizable brands in the world. Founded in 1998 and restructured under the Alphabet umbrella in 2015, the company operates across several high-impact verticals including digital advertising, cloud computing, hardware, and emerging technologies.

Alphabet's core business model revolves around monetizing its vast digital ecosystem through advertising revenue, which remains the primary driver of top-line performance. Google Search, YouTube, and the Google Network collectively generate the majority of revenues, while Google Cloud has emerged as a rapidly growing and increasingly significant contributor. Beyond advertising and cloud, the company's Other Bets segment houses moonshot ventures in areas such as autonomous vehicles through Waymo, life sciences through Verily, and other frontier technology initiatives.

From a market position standpoint, Alphabet holds an unrivaled leadership position in global search, commanding approximately 90 percent of worldwide search engine market share. YouTube is the world's largest video-sharing platform, and Google Cloud ranks among the top three global cloud providers alongside Amazon Web Services and Microsoft Azure. This diversified yet interconnected portfolio creates powerful network effects and significant competitive moats across each business line.

Financially, Alphabet has demonstrated compelling and accelerating performance. Revenue has grown from approximately 258 billion dollars in 2021 to nearly 403 billion dollars in 2025, representing a four-year compound annual growth rate of approximately 11.8 percent. More impressively, EBITDA margin has expanded dramatically from 40.2 percent in 2021 to 44.7 percent in 2025, reflecting disciplined cost management and operating leverage. SG&A as a percentage of revenue has declined steadily, falling from 14.1 percent in 2021 to 12.5 percent in 2025. Earnings per share have similarly grown from 5.69 dollars in 2021 to 10.91 dollars in 2025.

Looking ahead, forward estimates project continued revenue growth toward 466 billion dollars by 2027, with EBITDA margins expected to expand further to approximately 51.7 percent, suggesting meaningful operating leverage as artificial intelligence integration drives efficiency gains. When benchmarked against peers, Alphabet's EV/EBITDA multiple of approximately 21.2 times in 2025 compares favorably relative to Microsoft at 23.6 times, while trading at a premium to Meta at 16.4 times, reflecting the market's recognition of Alphabet's diversified earnings power and long-term growth optionality.
