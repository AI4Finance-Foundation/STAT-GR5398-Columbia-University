Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) Valuation Analysis

Current Valuation Metrics

Alphabet trades at a trailing EV/EBITDA of approximately 21.2x on 2025 actuals, reflecting meaningful multiple expansion from the 17.2x recorded in 2024. This re-rating is justified by a significant improvement in EBITDA margins, which expanded from 38.7% in 2024 to 44.7% in 2025, driven by disciplined cost management and operating leverage across its core search and cloud businesses. The trailing P/E of approximately 28.7x on 2025 EPS of $10.91 compresses to an estimated 24.6x by 2027 as earnings grow toward $13.01 per share, suggesting a reasonable growth-adjusted valuation for a business of this quality and scale.

Peer Comparison

Relative to peers, Alphabet screens attractively. Microsoft commands a notably higher EV/EBITDA of 23.6x on 2025 figures despite generating lower absolute EBITDA growth momentum. Meta trades at a discount of approximately 16.4x, reflecting its narrower business mix and higher perceived concentration risk. Alphabet's 2026 estimated EBITDA of roughly $211 billion surpasses both peers, and its projected EBITDA margin expansion toward 51.7% by 2027 is among the most compelling in the large-cap technology universe.

Fair Value Assessment

Applying a peer-blended EV/EBITDA of 20x to 22x against 2026 estimated EBITDA of approximately $211 billion implies an enterprise value range of $4.2 trillion to $4.6 trillion. Incorporating net cash positions, this supports a fair value range of roughly $168 to $185 per share, suggesting the stock is approximately fairly valued at current levels with moderate upside contingent on sustained margin execution and cloud acceleration.
