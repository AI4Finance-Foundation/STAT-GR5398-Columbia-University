Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) competes primarily with Meta Platforms and Microsoft across digital advertising, cloud computing, and AI-driven services. Each company has demonstrated strong financial resilience, but notable differences in scale, margin trajectory, and valuation distinguish them.

In terms of EBITDA generation, Alphabet leads the peer group with $180 billion in 2025, surpassing Microsoft at $160 billion and Meta at $105 billion. Alphabet's EBITDA margin has expanded dramatically from 30.1% in 2022 to 44.7% in 2025, reflecting disciplined cost management and operating leverage. Microsoft maintains a consistently high margin profile, while Meta has shown the most aggressive margin recovery after its costly metaverse investment cycle weighed heavily on 2022 results.

On a valuation basis, Alphabet trades at an EV/EBITDA of 21.2x in 2025, sitting between Meta at 16.4x and Microsoft at 23.6x. Meta's lower multiple suggests the market assigns a modest discount, likely reflecting greater concentration risk in social advertising. Microsoft commands a premium multiple, supported by its entrenched enterprise software ecosystem and Azure cloud momentum.

Alphabet's revenue CAGR of 11.8% over the observed period is competitive, though forward estimates project a moderation to 4 to 6 percent annual growth through 2027. Meta and Microsoft are similarly navigating a maturing digital economy while investing heavily in AI infrastructure.

Alphabet's expanding contribution margin, now approaching 63% by 2027 estimates, underscores its durable competitive advantages in search and cloud. However, sustained AI investment and regulatory scrutiny remain key risks that could pressure margins and differentiate performance across the peer group going forward.
