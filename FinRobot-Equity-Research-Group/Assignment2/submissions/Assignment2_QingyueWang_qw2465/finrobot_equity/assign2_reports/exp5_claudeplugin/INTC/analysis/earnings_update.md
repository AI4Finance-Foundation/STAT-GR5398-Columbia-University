The one area of variance was EBITDA, which fell short of the estimate row; given that margin landed inline, the shortfall likely reflects a base-level difference in the absolute revenue or cost mix assumptions embedded in that row rather than a structural shift in underlying profitability dynamics. Looking ahead, the mixed but largely constructive read across the headline metrics keeps the long-term thesis intact. Segment-level trends and forward guidance will remain the natural focus for refining the model row in subsequent periods, as is standard practice when absolute earnings metrics diverge modestly from margin-consistent outcomes. On balance, the print does not materially alter the investment framework, and monitoring execution across Intel's product and manufacturing roadmap continues to be the appropriate lens for assessing the durability of the recovery narrative.
