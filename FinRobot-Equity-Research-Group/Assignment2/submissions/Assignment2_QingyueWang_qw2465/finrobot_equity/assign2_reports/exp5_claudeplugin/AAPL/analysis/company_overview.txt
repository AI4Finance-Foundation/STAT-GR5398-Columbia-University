Apple Inc. (AAPL) is one of the world's most valuable and recognizable technology companies, headquartered in Cupertino, California. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, Apple has evolved from a personal computer manufacturer into a diversified technology ecosystem company with a commanding global presence across consumer electronics, software, and digital services.

Apple's business model is built around a tightly integrated hardware and software ecosystem designed to drive customer loyalty and recurring revenue. The company's core product lines include the iPhone, Mac computers, iPad, Apple Watch, and AirPods. Complementing its hardware offerings, Apple operates a rapidly expanding Services segment encompassing the App Store, Apple Music, Apple TV+, iCloud, Apple Pay, and Apple Arcade. This services division has become an increasingly critical growth driver, commanding higher margins than hardware and providing more predictable, subscription-based revenue streams.

In terms of market position, Apple consistently ranks among the top global technology companies by revenue and market capitalization. The company maintains a premium brand identity that supports strong pricing power and customer retention. When compared to peers such as Alphabet and Microsoft, Apple has historically led in absolute EBITDA, though recent data indicates that both competitors have begun to close or surpass that gap, with Alphabet's EBITDA reaching approximately 180 billion dollars in 2025 versus Apple's 144 billion dollars.

Apple's recent financial performance reflects steady, if measured, growth. Revenue expanded from approximately 391 billion dollars in fiscal 2024 to 416 billion dollars in fiscal 2025, representing 6.4 percent growth. Contribution margins have improved consistently, rising from 41.8 percent in 2021 to 46.9 percent in 2025, signaling effective cost discipline and a favorable product mix shift toward higher-margin services. EBITDA margins have remained relatively stable in the low-to-mid 34 percent range historically, with forward estimates projecting meaningful expansion toward 44.8 percent by 2027, driven by operating leverage and SG&A efficiency improvements.

Earnings per share have grown from 5.67 dollars in 2021 to 7.49 dollars in 2025, with estimates projecting continued growth to 8.93 dollars by 2027. The stock currently trades at a premium EV/EBITDA multiple of approximately 27 times relative to peers, reflecting investor confidence in Apple's brand strength, ecosystem stickiness, and long-term earnings visibility despite a moderating revenue growth outlook of approximately 3.3 percent on a compounded annual basis through 2027.
