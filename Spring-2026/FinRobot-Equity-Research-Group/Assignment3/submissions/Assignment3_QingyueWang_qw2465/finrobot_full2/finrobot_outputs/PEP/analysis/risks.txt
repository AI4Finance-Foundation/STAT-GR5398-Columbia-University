**Key Investment Risks for PepsiCo Inc. (PEP):**

- **Margin Compression Risk:** EBITDA margins have declined from 18.7% (2021) to 16.5% (2025A) despite healthy revenue growth, indicating rising costs and pressure on profitability.

- **Slowing Revenue Growth:** Revenue growth is decelerating—dropping from 8.7% (2022) to 0.4% (2024A) and 2.3% (2025A)—raising concerns about future top-line expansion.

- **Competitive Pressure:** PepsiCo’s EV/EBITDA multiples are at or below key peers like KO, and EBITDA growth lags behind KO, suggesting intensifying competition and potential loss of market share.

- **Earnings Volatility:** EPS is projected to decline from $6.98 (2024A) to $6.03 (2025A), reflecting potential operational challenges or increased costs impacting bottom-line performance.

- **High SG&A Expenses:** SG&A margin remains elevated (near or above 39% through 2027E), which could limit operational flexibility and reduce leverage to improve profitability if sales growth continues to slow.
