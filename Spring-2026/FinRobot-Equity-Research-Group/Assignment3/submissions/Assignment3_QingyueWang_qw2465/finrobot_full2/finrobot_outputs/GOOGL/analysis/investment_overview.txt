Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) Investment Update

Alphabet has demonstrated robust financial performance, with revenue rising from $257.6 billion in 2021 to $350.0 billion in 2024, representing a strong CAGR of 11.8%. The pace of revenue growth accelerated in 2024 (13.9%) after a slower 2022–2023 period, reflecting resilient advertising demand and increasing traction in Google Cloud and YouTube. Looking forward, consensus estimates project continued topline growth, albeit at a moderating pace (5–6% in 2025–2027), as the company matures.

Profitability has significantly improved. EBITDA is forecast to grow from $97.9 billion in 2023 to $179.9 billion in 2025, with margins expanding from 31.9% to 44.7% over the same period. This margin expansion is driven by operating leverage, disciplined cost control (notably, SG&A margin declines from 14.4% in 2023 to 12.5% in 2025), and ongoing efficiency initiatives. Contribution margins are also climbing, reaching over 59% in 2025 and projected to surpass 62% by 2027.

Among peers, Alphabet’s EBITDA is on par with Microsoft and above Amazon and Meta, signaling strong operational scale. While Alphabet’s EV/EBITDA multiple (21.2x for 2025) is higher than Amazon and Meta, it trades at a discount to Microsoft, reflecting a balanced risk/reward profile.

Earnings per share are forecast to almost double from $5.84 in 2023 to $10.91 in 2025, supporting further valuation upside. With ongoing investments in AI, cloud, and digital content, Alphabet is well positioned to capture secular growth trends.

Outlook: Alphabet remains a core holding for growth-oriented investors, supported by strong fundamentals, expanding profitability, and leadership in digital advertising and AI. Valuation is reasonable relative to peers, and near-term catalysts include further AI commercialization and continued cloud momentum.
