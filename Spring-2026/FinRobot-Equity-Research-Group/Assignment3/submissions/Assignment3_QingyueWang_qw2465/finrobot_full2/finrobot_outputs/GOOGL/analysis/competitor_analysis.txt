Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) remains a dominant force among its technology peers—Amazon (AMZN), Meta Platforms (META), and Microsoft (MSFT)—demonstrating robust financial performance and operational efficiency. Alphabet's revenue growth from $258 billion in 2021 to an estimated $466 billion in 2027 (11.8% CAGR) outpaces historical growth rates of most traditional tech companies, although it is comparable to its closest peers.

Alphabet's EBITDA performance is particularly notable. In 2023, its EBITDA of $97.97 billion was ahead of Meta ($59.05B) and Amazon ($89.4B), and closely followed Microsoft ($105.14B). By 2025, Alphabet is projected to lead the group with $179.96 billion in EBITDA, surpassing both Amazon ($165.34B) and Microsoft ($160.17B), and maintaining a significant gap over Meta ($104.55B). Alphabet’s EBITDA margin is also on an upward trajectory, projected to reach 51.7% by 2027, reflecting strong cost discipline and operating leverage relative to Amazon, which traditionally operates at lower margins.

In terms of valuation, Alphabet’s EV/EBITDA multiple is generally competitive, ranging from 13.6x to 21.2x (2022-2025), typically below Microsoft’s but above Meta’s, suggesting a balance between growth expectations and profitability. Amazon’s higher multiples reflect its reinvestment-heavy model and historically lower margins.

Margin-wise, Alphabet boasts industry-leading contribution and EBITDA margins, with contribution margin rising from 56.9% in 2021 to an expected 62.7% by 2027, and a steady reduction in SG&A margin, indicating operational efficiency.

Overall, Alphabet stands out for its sustained revenue growth, expanding margins, and strong profitability compared to peers. Its balanced valuation reflects its leadership in digital advertising, cloud, and AI, while ongoing cost control and margin expansion position it favorably among tech giants.
