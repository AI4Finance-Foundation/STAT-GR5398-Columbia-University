Amgen Inc. (AMGN) is in the spotlight following several notable developments and financial trends. Guggenheim recently lowered its price target on Amgen to $340 (from $351) but maintained a Neutral rating, reflecting some market caution about the biotech giant’s near-term prospects. Meanwhile, retail investor interest is on the rise, particularly among Zacks.com users and on social media platforms, with overall sentiment mixed—bullishness is moderate, but buzz is uneven between platforms like Reddit (rising trend) and X.com (falling trend).

Financially, Amgen’s growth outlook remains robust. The company’s revenue is projected to grow at a 9.1% CAGR through 2027, reaching over $42.5 billion. After a strong 18.6% revenue jump in 2024, annual growth is expected to moderate to the mid-single digits. EBITDA margins are also set to recover, climbing from 40% in 2024 back above 50% by 2025 and reaching 56.1% by 2027, indicating potential for improved profitability. EPS is projected to rebound sharply from $7.62 in 2024 to over $17 by 2027, suggesting normalization following an earnings dip.

Amgen continues to invest aggressively in its manufacturing footprint, recently announcing an additional $300 million U.S. investment—bringing its total to nearly $2 billion in the last year. This signals a focus on supply chain resilience and next-generation technology, which could support future growth and mitigate operational risks.

Valuation concerns persist, however. A discounted cash flow (DCF) analysis pegs intrinsic value at $268, below the current share price near $330, implying downside risk if growth or margins disappoint. Peer comparisons show Amgen’s EV/EBITDA is broadly in line with industry averages, but not at a clear discount.

Investment Implications: Amgen’s financial trajectory and manufacturing investments support a positive long-term growth thesis, but the stock appears fully valued to slightly overvalued based on intrinsic value models. Investors should monitor margin recovery, manufacturing execution, and evolving sentiment, as well as peer performance for confirmation of sustained upside. Caution is warranted given the recent price target cut and mixed retail sentiment.
