Amgen Inc. is a leading biotechnology company focused on the discovery, development, manufacturing, and commercialization of innovative human therapeutics. The company’s business model centers on leveraging advanced science and biotechnology to address serious illnesses, primarily in the areas of oncology, nephrology, bone health, cardiology, inflammation, and neuroscience. Amgen generates revenue through the sale of proprietary biologic medicines and biosimilars, with a global footprint that includes both established and emerging markets.

Amgen’s diverse product portfolio includes some of the world’s best-selling biologic drugs, such as Enbrel (for rheumatoid arthritis and other autoimmune diseases), Prolia and XGEVA (for bone health), and Repatha (for cholesterol management), among others. The company continues to invest heavily in research and development to expand its pipeline, including biologics and biosimilar products that address high unmet medical needs.

Market position remains strong for Amgen, as reflected in its robust financial metrics. In 2023, Amgen reported revenues of $28.2 billion, up 7.1% versus 2022, and expects to see continued growth with a CAGR of 9.1% projected through 2027, reaching over $42.5 billion in revenue. Amgen’s EBITDA in 2023 was $14.8 billion, surpassing key biotech peers such as Biogen, Gilead, and Regeneron, and is forecasted to rise further, highlighting Amgen’s operational scale and efficiency. Contribution margins and EBITDA margins are consistently strong, with the latter rebounding to over 53% by 2025 and projected to exceed 56% by 2027. This reflects effective cost management and a high-value product mix. Amgen’s SG&A margin is also trending lower, indicative of improving operational leverage.

Recent performance has been marked by accelerating top-line growth and a resilient bottom line. While 2024 EPS is temporarily lower due to acquisition-related and integration costs, projections show a strong rebound to above $14 in 2025 and further growth thereafter. Amgen’s valuation, with a PE ratio in the low 20s and an EV/EBITDA multiple in line with or slightly above peers, reflects investor confidence in its future earnings potential and robust cash flow generation.

Overall, Amgen’s strong market position, diversified product portfolio, and consistent financial performance underpin its status as a global biotechnology leader, well-positioned for sustained growth and value creation in the coming years.
