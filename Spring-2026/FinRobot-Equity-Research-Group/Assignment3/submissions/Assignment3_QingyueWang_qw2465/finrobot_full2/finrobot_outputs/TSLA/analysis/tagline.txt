Tesla maintains robust revenue growth with a 15.2% CAGR from 2021 to 2027, although recent topline expansion has slowed and margins have compressed, reflecting increased cost pressures. EBITDA margins have declined from 21.7% in 2022 to 12.4% in 2025E, underperforming key peers in profitability, while the company’s valuation remains elevated, with a 2025E EV/EBITDA multiple of 122.6x compared to peers in the 10–23x range. Despite industry-leading scale, Tesla faces headwinds to earnings and margin recovery, leaving its premium valuation exposed to execution risk.
