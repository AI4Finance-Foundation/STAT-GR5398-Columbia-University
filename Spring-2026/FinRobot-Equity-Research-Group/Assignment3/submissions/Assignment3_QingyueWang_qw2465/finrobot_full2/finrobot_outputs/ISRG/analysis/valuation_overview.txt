Intuitive Surgical Inc. (ISRG) continues to trade at a premium valuation, reflecting its market leadership in robotic surgery and strong growth outlook. As of 2023, ISRG posted revenue of $7.12 billion (up 14.5% YoY) and EBITDA of $2.17 billion. Forward revenue growth is robust, with a 2021-2027 CAGR of 15.2%, and EBITDA margins are projected to expand from 30.4% in 2023 to nearly 47% by 2027—well above most medtech peers.

ISRG’s current 2023 EV/EBITDA multiple stands at 53.4x, far higher than Stryker (SYK) at 24.6x, Medtronic (MDT) at 16.5x, and Edwards Lifesciences (EW) at 29.8x. Its forward (2025E) EV/EBITDA of 55x remains more than double that of MDT (14.6x) and SYK (23x). The stock’s 2023 PE ratio is 65.9x, dropping to a still-elevated 60.7x by 2027E, compared to large-cap medtech averages in the 20–30x range.

This valuation premium is justified by ISRG’s superior growth, high contribution margins (66–69%), and dominant position in a rapidly expanding, high-barrier market. However, the gap to peers is historically wide and leaves little room for execution missteps or regulatory risk.

On a fair value basis, if ISRG were to trade in line with high-growth peers like SYK (mid-20x EV/EBITDA), fair value would be meaningfully lower than current levels. However, given its unique market position and growth profile, a justified premium of 35–40x EV/EBITDA is reasonable, implying the stock is currently overvalued by 20–30%. Cautious investors may wish to await a pullback, while long-term growth-focused holders may see continued upside as ISRG delivers on its growth trajectory.
