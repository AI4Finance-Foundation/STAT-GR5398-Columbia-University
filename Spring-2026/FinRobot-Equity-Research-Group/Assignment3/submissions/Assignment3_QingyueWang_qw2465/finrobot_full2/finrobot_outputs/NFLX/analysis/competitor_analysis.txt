Netflix Inc. (NFLX) continues to lead the streaming industry in both financial performance and operational efficiency compared to its key peers: Disney (DIS), Paramount Global (PARA), and Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD). In 2023, Netflix reported an EBITDA of $21.5 billion, outpacing Disney ($12.1 billion), Paramount ($85 million), and Warner Bros. Discovery ($22.4 billion). This strong EBITDA performance reflects Netflix’s high contribution margins (41.5% in 2023, trending to over 50% by 2027) and disciplined cost structure, with SG&A margins projected to decline below 10% by 2027.

Revenue growth for Netflix is robust, with a CAGR of 11.1% through 2027, and double-digit annual growth expected in 2024 and 2025. This contrasts with peers, many of whom face slower growth or, in Paramount’s case, significant volatility and even negative EBITDA in projected years. Netflix has managed to scale its operations globally, translating subscriber growth into higher revenues and improved profitability.

On valuation, Netflix’s EV/EBITDA multiple (10.5x in 2023, stabilizing around 13x-15x through 2025) is higher than Warner Bros. Discovery (3x-10.5x) but well below Disney’s elevated ratios (15x-40x historically). This reflects the market’s recognition of Netflix’s superior financial performance, growth prospects, and margin profile, while still offering a premium relative to less profitable or more volatile peers.

In summary, Netflix remains the clear financial and operational leader among streaming peers, combining high growth, expanding margins, and consistent profitability. While Disney commands a strategic brand premium and Warner Bros. Discovery trades at lower multiples, neither matches Netflix’s combination of scale, efficiency, and forward momentum.
