Netflix Inc. (NASDAQ: NFLX) is the world’s leading subscription-based streaming entertainment service, operating a direct-to-consumer model that delivers a vast array of TV series, documentaries, feature films, and original content across virtually all internet-connected screens globally. The company’s business model is centered on subscription revenue, with users paying a recurring monthly fee for unlimited access to its content library, which includes both licensed programming and an increasing slate of original productions under the “Netflix Originals” banner.

Netflix’s core product is its streaming platform, which now serves over 230 million subscribers worldwide. The company has invested heavily in content creation, allowing it to differentiate itself from competitors through exclusive, high-quality original content like “Stranger Things,” “The Crown,” and “Squid Game.” Netflix’s global reach, user-friendly interface, and sophisticated recommendation algorithms have enabled it to maintain a leading market position, despite intensifying competition from peers such as Disney+ (DIS), Paramount+ (PARA), and Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD).

Financially, Netflix has demonstrated robust growth and improving profitability. Revenue increased at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 11.1% from $29.7 billion in 2021 to $33.7 billion in 2023, with forecasts projecting continued momentum to over $52 billion by 2027. Contribution margin improved from 41.6% in 2021 to 46.1% in 2024, and is projected to exceed 51% by 2027, reflecting both operational leverage and disciplined cost management. EBITDA has also grown substantially, reaching $26.3 billion in 2024, outpacing major peers (e.g., Disney’s $14.6 billion in 2024) and highlighting Netflix’s superior scalability and margin profile.

On the capital markets side, Netflix trades at a premium relative to traditional media peers, with a 2023 EV/EBITDA multiple of 10.5x, indicating investor confidence in its growth trajectory and profitability versus legacy studios. Despite a historically high PE ratio (39.8x in 2023), strong EPS growth (from $1.16 in 2021 to a projected $3.08 in 2027) underpins future value creation.

Recent performance reflects Netflix’s ability to navigate industry pressures, with accelerating top-line growth (15.6% in 2024), expanding margins, and strong free cash flow generation. Strategic initiatives—such as password-sharing crackdowns, ad-supported tiers, and international content investments—have further cemented its position as the dominant force in the global streaming market.
