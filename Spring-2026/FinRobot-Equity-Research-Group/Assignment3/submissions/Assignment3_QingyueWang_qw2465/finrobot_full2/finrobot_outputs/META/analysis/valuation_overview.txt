Meta Platforms, Inc. (META) is currently trading at a 2024E PE ratio of 23.8x and a 2024E EV/EBITDA multiple of 17.1x. The company has demonstrated robust revenue growth, with a projected CAGR of 14.3% from 2023 to 2027, and has significantly expanded its EBITDA margin from 32.3% in 2022 to an estimated 52.8% in 2024. EPS is forecasted to grow from $15.19 in 2023 to $24.61 in 2024, indicating strong earnings momentum.

When compared to its major peer Alphabet (GOOGL), Meta’s 2024E EV/EBITDA of 17.1x is roughly in line with GOOGL’s 17.2x, while its 2025E multiple of 16.4x remains below GOOGL’s 21.2x. Both companies show strong EBITDA generation, but Meta’s faster margin expansion and higher projected EBITDA growth (from $59.1B in 2023 to $104.5B in 2025) suggest it is closing the gap with Alphabet in terms of profitability. In contrast, smaller peers like Pinterest (PINS) and Snap (SNAP) report far lower or negative EBITDA and highly volatile multiples, reinforcing Meta’s position as a best-in-class operator.

Given Meta’s rapid earnings growth, expanding margins, and valuation in line with or below its closest peer, the stock appears reasonably valued. Applying a peer-average 2025E EV/EBITDA of ~18x to Meta’s projected 2025 EBITDA ($104.5B) suggests upside to the current price, supporting a fair value estimate above current trading levels. Risks include a slowdown in digital ad growth and regulatory pressures, but overall, Meta’s fundamentals justify a premium valuation within the sector.
