Meta Platforms, Inc. (META) – Investment Update

Meta Platforms has demonstrated a strong rebound in financial performance since 2022. After a slight revenue decline in 2022 (-1.1%), Meta posted impressive growth in 2023 (+15.7%) and is expected to accelerate further in 2024 and 2025 with projected revenue increases of 21.9% and 22.2% respectively. This growth is supported by solid execution in core advertising, increased monetization of platforms like Instagram and WhatsApp, and ongoing investments in AI-driven features and Reels.

Profitability has markedly improved. After EBITDA margins dipped to 32.3% in 2022 due to elevated costs, margins recovered to 43.8% in 2023 and are forecast to reach over 70% by 2025-2027. Contribution margins are also expanding, driven by disciplined cost management—particularly in SG&A, which is projected to fall below 12% of revenue by 2025. EPS is set to nearly double from 2022 to 2024, reflecting both revenue growth and operational leverage.

Relative to peers, Meta’s EBITDA is second only to Alphabet (GOOGL), and its forward EV/EBITDA multiple (16.4x for 2025) is at a discount to Alphabet (21.2x), suggesting attractive relative value. Peers such as Pinterest and Snap continue to lag considerably in both profitability and scale.

Looking ahead, Meta’s outlook remains positive, with a projected revenue CAGR of 14.3% through 2027. The company is well-positioned to benefit from digital advertising tailwinds, new product initiatives, and potential monetization of its growing ecosystem, including the metaverse. Risks include regulatory headwinds and intensifying competition, but current trends indicate Meta remains a leader in digital engagement and monetization.
