Investment Update: Texas Instruments (TXN)

Texas Instruments (TXN) has experienced a challenging period, with revenue declining from a peak of $20.0 billion in 2022 to $15.6 billion projected for 2024. This decline reflects ongoing softness in end markets, particularly in the industrial and automotive sectors, which together represent a majority of TXN’s revenue mix. EBITDA and contribution margins have compressed accordingly, with EBITDA margin falling from 56.0% in 2022 to 48.2% in 2024, and contribution margin from 68.8% to 58.1% over the same period.

Despite recent headwinds, the company is expected to return to growth in 2025, with consensus projecting a 13.0% revenue rebound and sequential margin recovery. Longer-term projections suggest a 4–6% annual revenue growth from 2026 onwards, supporting gradual margin expansion and earnings per share (EPS) improvement. SG&A costs remain well controlled, and management’s focus on operational efficiency and capital allocation supports a positive outlook as demand stabilizes.

Compared to peers (ADI, MCHP, QCOM), TXN’s EBITDA performance remains robust, but its near-term EV/EBITDA valuation has expanded to 24x (2024E), above its historical average and above most peers, reflecting market confidence in TXN’s long-term competitive positioning and eventual earnings recovery.

Key growth drivers include the secular demand for analog and embedded chips in automotive and industrial applications, TXN’s broad customer base, and its strategic investments in 300mm manufacturing capacity. Risks include slower-than-expected recovery in cyclical markets and elevated capital expenditures.

Overall, while near-term results remain pressured, TXN’s strong market position, prudent cost management, and exposure to long-term growth trends underpin a constructive medium- to long-term outlook. Investors should watch for signs of demand stabilization and margin improvement as signals for renewed momentum.
