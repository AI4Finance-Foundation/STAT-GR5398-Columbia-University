NVIDIA (NVDA) remains at the forefront of the AI hardware sector, recently making headlines with a sweeping long-term partnership with Corning (GLW) to expand U.S. manufacturing for AI infrastructure. This strategic alliance, announced on May 6, 2026, drove Corning shares up nearly 19% and signals NVIDIA’s commitment to securing its supply chain and supporting domestic production of advanced fiber optics and glass crucial for next-gen data centers.

Financially, NVIDIA’s performance is remarkable. Revenues have soared from $26.9 billion in 2023 to $60.9 billion in 2024, with projections of $130.5 billion and $215.9 billion for 2025 and 2026, respectively—a CAGR of 68.3%. Margins have expanded sharply: EBITDA margin is expected to reach 66.9% in 2026, up from 22.2% in 2023, reflecting strong operating leverage as AI demand accelerates. Contribution margins and EPS are also rising, with EPS forecast to more than quadruple from 2024 to 2026.

However, some analysts note that NVIDIA’s valuation has compressed noticeably. The PE ratio is projected to fall from over 100x in 2023 to below 38x by 2026, and its EV/EBITDA multiple is converging toward industry peers. This suggests the market is pricing in a more mature growth phase or anticipating increased competition, particularly as Google begins directly selling TPUs, potentially challenging NVIDIA’s AI compute dominance.

Peer comparisons show NVIDIA dramatically outpacing rivals like AMD, Intel, and Broadcom in EBITDA growth, underscoring its leading position in the AI hardware space. Yet, the stock’s price action has stalled recently, up less than 2% over six months despite a 93% one-year run. Retail sentiment is mixed; while buzz is high, bullishness is muted, and prediction markets lean slightly bearish for late May 2026.

Investment implications: NVIDIA’s fundamentals and strategic positioning remain robust, but valuation concerns and emerging competition could cap near-term upside. Long-term investors may still find the risk/reward attractive, especially if NVIDIA maintains its technological edge in AI infrastructure.
