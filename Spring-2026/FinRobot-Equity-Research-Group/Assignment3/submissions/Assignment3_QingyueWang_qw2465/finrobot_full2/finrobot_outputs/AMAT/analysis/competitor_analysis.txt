Applied Materials Inc. (AMAT) is a leading player in the semiconductor equipment industry, competing closely with peers such as ASML, KLA Corporation (KLAC), and Lam Research (LRCX). 

From 2021 to 2023, AMAT demonstrated steady revenue growth (CAGR 5.3%), with revenues rising from $23.1B to $26.5B. While its growth rate is moderate compared to some peers, AMAT maintains robust profitability, with contribution margins improving from 47.3% to 46.7% and expected to reach over 51% by 2027. Its EBITDA margin is relatively stable around 32%, and projections suggest an expansion to 47% by 2027, indicating improving operational efficiency and scale.

Comparing EBITDA, AMAT is competitive, trailing only ASML in recent years. In 2023, AMAT posted $8.46B in EBITDA, below ASML’s $9.98B but significantly ahead of KLAC ($4.5B) and LRCX ($5.6B). This positioning reflects AMAT's broad product portfolio and global customer base. 

On valuation, AMAT’s EV/EBITDA ratios have generally been lower than ASML’s, suggesting a discount despite similar or stronger profit metrics. For instance, AMAT’s 2023 EV/EBITDA was 13.0x versus ASML’s 25.9x, indicating a potential undervaluation or lower growth expectations. KLAC and LRCX have ratios that are more closely aligned with AMAT, though KLAC’s 2024 multiple spikes significantly.

In summary, AMAT is a strong performer, balancing growth, profitability, and operational efficiency. While ASML leads in margins and valuation due to its lithography dominance, AMAT’s diversified offerings and improving margins keep it well-positioned among peers. Investors may view AMAT as a value opportunity given its lower valuation multiples, solid financials, and steady growth outlook.
