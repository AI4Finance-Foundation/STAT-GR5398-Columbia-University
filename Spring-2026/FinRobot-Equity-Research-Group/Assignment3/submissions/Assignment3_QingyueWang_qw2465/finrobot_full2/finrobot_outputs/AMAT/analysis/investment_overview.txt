Applied Materials Inc. (AMAT) Investment Update

Recent Financial Performance  
Applied Materials has delivered steady financial performance over the last several years. Revenue increased from $23.1 billion in 2021 to $27.2 billion in 2024, representing a CAGR of 5.3%. Growth was particularly strong in 2022 (+11.8%), with more moderate increases in subsequent years. Contribution margin has improved from 47.3% in 2021 to 47.5% in 2024 and is projected to reach 51.7% by 2027, reflecting ongoing operational efficiency and a favorable product mix. EBITDA also rose from $7.4 billion in 2021 to $8.8 billion in 2024, with margins expanding from 32% to 34% and expected to reach 47% by 2027. EPS has steadily improved from $6.47 in 2021 to $8.68 in 2024, with further growth anticipated.

Growth Drivers  
AMAT’s performance is supported by robust demand for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, driven by secular trends in AI, advanced computing, and global chip capacity expansions. The company’s focus on R&D and next-generation process technologies has allowed it to capture incremental market share. Margin expansion is aided by cost controls and a shift toward higher-value solutions. Compared to peers, AMAT’s EBITDA remains competitive, and its EV/EBITDA valuation is at a moderate premium to historical levels, but in line with sector standards.

Outlook  
Looking ahead, AMAT is expected to continue moderate growth, with revenue projected to exceed $32.8 billion by 2027. Margin expansion and EPS growth remain key themes, underpinned by strong industry fundamentals. However, the pace of revenue growth may moderate as the semiconductor capital equipment market normalizes after recent surges. AMAT’s valuation (2025E EV/EBITDA of 19.3x) is reasonable relative to peers, supporting a positive long-term outlook, though near-term upside may be limited by macro uncertainties and supply chain factors. Overall, AMAT remains well-positioned for sustainable growth and profitability.
