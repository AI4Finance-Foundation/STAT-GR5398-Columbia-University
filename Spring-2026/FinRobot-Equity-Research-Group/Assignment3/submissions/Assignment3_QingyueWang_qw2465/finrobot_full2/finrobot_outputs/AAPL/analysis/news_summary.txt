Apple Inc. (AAPL) reported standout Q2 2026 results, with revenue up 16.6% year-over-year to $111.2 billion and gross margins reaching a record 49.3%. The company’s iPhone sales and Services division continue to drive growth, with Greater China revenue surging 28.1% due to effective iPhone pricing strategies. Apple’s margin expansion is notable, and its contribution margin and EBITDA margin are projected to rise steadily through 2027, reflecting improving operational efficiency.

Apple is taking a unique approach to the AI race, opting not to match rivals’ aggressive $725 billion AI infrastructure spending. Instead, the company is focusing on integrating AI features into its ecosystem and leveraging its existing hardware and services strengths. This strategy is being well-received, as it avoids margin compression and capex risks that competitors face while still positioning Apple to benefit from AI-driven upgrades.

Supply chain risk management remains in focus. Apple’s exploratory talks with Intel and Samsung for chip supply signal a structural shift toward reducing reliance on any single supplier, an important move after previous disruptions impacted iPhone revenues. This diversification is seen as a proactive step to ensure resilience and maintain product availability.

Financially, Apple’s revenue is expected to grow at a 3.3% CAGR through 2027, with robust EBITDA growth outpacing peers like Amazon and Google. The company retains premium valuation multiples (PE and EV/EBITDA), reflecting confidence in its earnings power and cash flow. Apple’s next dividend payment is scheduled for May 14, reinforcing its status as a shareholder-friendly blue-chip.

Retail sentiment is broadly positive but mixed across platforms: X.com (formerly Twitter) shows the highest bullishness (51%), while Reddit is more cautious (29%). Overall, Apple’s strong execution, prudent capital allocation, and defensive supply chain moves make it a resilient long-term investment, though high valuations may limit near-term upside.
