Apple Inc. (AAPL) – Investment Update

Apple delivered steady financial performance in recent years, with revenue reaching $391 billion in 2024, up 2% year-over-year, following a slight decline in 2023. The company’s revenue compound annual growth rate (CAGR) for 2021-2027 is projected at 3.3%, with management forecasting mid-single-digit growth through 2027. Cost discipline remains strong: Cost of operations as a percentage of revenue is stable, and SG&A margin continues to trend lower, falling from 6.7% in 2024 to an estimated 5.1% by 2027, supporting profitability.

Profitability metrics are robust. Contribution margins expanded from 41.8% in 2021 to 46.2% in 2024 and are expected to approach 50% by 2027. EBITDA margin is also set to increase, rising from 33.7% in 2021 to nearly 45% by 2027 on higher-margin services growth and operating leverage. EPS is forecast to grow from $6.11 in 2024 to $8.93 by 2027, reflecting consistent earnings expansion.

Growth drivers include the continued diversification toward high-margin services, ongoing strength in hardware upgrades, and ecosystem expansion. Apple maintains a premium positioning and strong brand loyalty, ensuring resilience even in uneven macro environments. Peer comparisons show Apple’s EBITDA is competitive with tech giants like Microsoft and Alphabet, though Amazon is expected to surpass Apple in EBITDA by 2026. Apple’s EV/EBITDA multiple (26.6x 2024E) remains at a premium to most peers, reflecting its stable cash generation and defensible business model.

Outlook: With healthy margins, increasing service revenue, and a track record of innovation, Apple remains well-positioned for long-term growth. While valuation is elevated, the company’s strong fundamentals and shareholder returns support a favorable outlook for long-term investors.
