Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) stands out among its semiconductor peers—Marvell Technology (MRVL), Qualcomm (QCOM), and Texas Instruments (TXN)—with robust financial performance and strong growth projections. Broadcom’s revenue is expected to grow at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 23.5% from 2021 to 2027, outpacing traditional analog peers like TXN and digital competitors such as QCOM and MRVL. The company’s 2024 revenue surge (44% YoY) and continued expansion position it as a growth leader.

On profitability, Broadcom’s EBITDA has consistently surpassed those of its peers. In 2023, Broadcom posted an EBITDA of $20.6B, compared to QCOM’s $9.95B and TXN’s $9B. By 2027, Broadcom’s EBITDA is forecasted to reach $43.3B, more than double that of QCOM ($17B) and significantly ahead of MRVL and TXN. Contribution and EBITDA margins are also strong, with 2023 margins of 68.9% and 57.4%, respectively, and expected to climb above 70% and 65% by 2027, indicating superior operational efficiency.

However, Broadcom’s valuation is elevated. Its EV/EBITDA multiple for 2023 (18.2x) and projected for 2025 (50.6x) are notably higher than QCOM (12.7x) and TXN (20.6x), reflecting premium growth expectations but also potential risk if growth cools. MRVL’s multiples are even higher, but Broadcom’s scale and profitability are far superior.

In summary, Broadcom leads its peer group in growth, scale, and profitability, justifying its higher valuation. Its strong margins and market expansion highlight its competitive advantages, though investors should weigh these against its premium multiple and the cyclical risks inherent in semiconductors.
