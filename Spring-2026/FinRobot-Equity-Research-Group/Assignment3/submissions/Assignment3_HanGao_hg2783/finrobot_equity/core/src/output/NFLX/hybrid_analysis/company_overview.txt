Netflix Inc. (NFLX) is a leading global provider of streaming entertainment services. The company’s business model is built around a subscription-based platform, offering a vast library of movies, TV series, documentaries, and original content to a worldwide audience. Netflix generates revenue primarily through monthly subscription fees, with no reliance on advertising or physical distribution. Its core value proposition lies in its ability to deliver high-quality, on-demand entertainment across a variety of devices, leveraging its proprietary recommendation algorithms and extensive content catalog.

Netflix’s product offering has evolved from licensing third-party content to developing a significant slate of original programming, which differentiates it from competitors. This focus on exclusive originals and global content has enabled Netflix to attract and retain subscribers in over 190 countries. The company continues to invest heavily in content creation and acquisition, aiming to cater to diverse tastes and cultural preferences.

The company maintains a dominant position in the streaming market, facing competition from other major players but consistently expanding its subscriber base and content reach. Its scale, technological infrastructure, and commitment to original programming underpin its competitive advantage.

Financially, Netflix has demonstrated robust performance and healthy growth. Revenue increased from $29.7 billion in 2021 to $33.7 billion in 2023, and is projected to reach $45.2 billion by 2025, representing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 11.1%. Contribution margin has improved from 41.6% in 2021 to 46.1% in 2024, with further expansion expected. The company’s EBITDA margin has remained strong, averaging above 64% from 2021 to 2024, though it is forecasted to normalize to approximately 40% by 2026-2027. Operating leverage is evident, as SG&A margin is gradually declining from 13.1% in 2021 to a projected 10% by 2027.

Netflix’s earnings per share (EPS) have grown significantly, more than doubling from 1.16 in 2021 to 2.58 in 2025, with continued growth projected. Revenue growth rates have been robust, peaking at 15.9% in 2025, and are expected to moderate to mid-single digits in the following years.

Overall, Netflix’s strong market position, continued investment in original content, and disciplined cost management support its ongoing growth and profitability in the increasingly competitive streaming industry.
