- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** Ongoing macroeconomic pressures could slow enterprise software procurement cycles, reduce government and commercial IT spending, and weaken discretionary demand, directly impacting Palantir's forecasted high revenue growth and margin expansion (SEC 10-K/10-Q).

- **Inflationary Pressures:** Persistent inflation may increase costs for components, logistics, and labor, which could compress margins and limit the company’s ability to fully realize forecasted EBITDA and EPS improvements despite targeted margin discipline (SEC 10-K/10-Q).

- **Supply Chain Disruptions:** Any disruption in the supply chain could impair Palantir’s ability to deliver products and services on time, affecting cost structure, inventory management, and launch execution, thereby risking revenue and profitability targets (SEC 10-K/10-Q).

- **Competitive Pressure:** Intensifying competition in AI, cloud, data analytics, and adjacent technology sectors could erode pricing power and market share, challenging Palantir’s ability to sustain its projected revenue growth and operating leverage (SEC 10-K/10-Q).

- **Regulatory and Cybersecurity Risks:** Heightened regulatory scrutiny and potential cybersecurity or data-protection incidents could disrupt operations, damage reputation, and erode customer trust, adversely affecting financial performance and growth prospects (SEC 10-K/10-Q).
