Amazon.com, Inc. (AMZN) demonstrates robust revenue growth and profitability improvements relative to its technology mega-cap peers—Apple (AAPL), Alphabet (GOOGL), Meta (META), and Microsoft (MSFT). From 2021 to 2027, Amazon’s revenue is projected to grow at an 11.1% CAGR, outpacing the typical industry growth rates for mature tech giants.

Amazon’s EBITDA shows significant recovery and expansion after a dip in 2022, rising from $74.4B in 2021 to an estimated $165.3B in 2025 and over $230B by 2026. In 2023, Amazon’s EBITDA ($89.4B) trailed Apple and Microsoft but was ahead of Meta and comparable to Alphabet. By 2025 and 2026, Amazon is forecasted to surpass Apple and Microsoft in EBITDA, indicating strong operating leverage, particularly as EBITDA margins expand sharply to 43.7% in 2025 and nearly 47% in 2027—well above historical levels.

Amazon’s contribution margin steadily increases from 42.0% in 2021 to over 53% by 2027, reflecting ongoing efficiency in operations and likely continued growth in higher-margin businesses such as AWS and advertising. SG&A margin declines, signaling cost discipline.

From a valuation perspective, Amazon’s EV/EBITDA falls from 23.8x in 2021 to 15.3x in 2025, becoming more attractive relative to peers like Apple and Microsoft (both above 23x in 2025). Alphabet and Meta generally trade at lower EV/EBITDA multiples, reflecting different growth and risk profiles.

In summary, Amazon is positioned as a high-growth, increasingly profitable leader among tech mega-caps, with improving operating leverage and a valuation that is trending toward peer averages as profitability scales. Its ability to grow EBITDA and margins faster than most competitors suggests Amazon is closing the gap with more mature tech firms while retaining its growth premium.
