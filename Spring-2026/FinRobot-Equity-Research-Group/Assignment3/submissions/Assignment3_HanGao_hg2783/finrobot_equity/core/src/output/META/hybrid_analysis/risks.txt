- **Macroeconomic and Inflationary Pressure:** Recent SEC filings highlight that macroeconomic uncertainty and inflation could weaken discretionary consumer demand and slow premium-device replacement cycles. This risk may limit revenue growth and compress margins by driving up component, logistics, and labor costs, directly impacting Meta’s financial performance.

- **Supply Chain Disruptions:** Both 10-K and 10-Q filings emphasize that disruptions in the global supply chain could negatively affect product availability, cost structure, inventory management, and the timing of new product launches. Such disruptions pose a risk to Meta's ability to execute on its product roadmap and meet revenue targets.

- **Intensifying Competitive Pressure:** Meta faces increasing competition across smartphones, wearables, services, cloud, and AI-driven features. SEC disclosures warn that this could erode pricing power and market share, threatening both revenue growth and margin expansion, especially as rivals accelerate innovation cycles.

- **Regulatory and Data-Protection Risks:** The company identifies regulatory scrutiny and cybersecurity/data-protection issues as material risks. Data breaches or heightened regulatory requirements could lead to operational disruptions, reputational harm, and loss of user trust, directly impacting user engagement and monetization potential.

- **Execution Risk on Product Innovation and Margin Discipline:** The company’s growth outlook depends heavily on successful product innovation and sustained operating leverage. Any failure in strategic execution, cost control, or product-cycle management could undermine projected margin improvements, EPS growth, and overall financial targets.
