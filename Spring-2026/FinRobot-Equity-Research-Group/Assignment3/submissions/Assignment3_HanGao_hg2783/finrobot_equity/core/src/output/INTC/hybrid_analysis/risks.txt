- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty & Demand Weakness:** Recent SEC filings highlight that ongoing macroeconomic pressures could slow premium device replacement cycles and weaken discretionary consumer demand, posing a risk to Intel’s already declining revenue trajectory (CAGR -9.6%, 2021-2027).

- **Margin Compression from Inflation:** Inflationary trends are pressuring Intel’s component, logistics, and labor costs, while also constraining consumer purchasing power. This limits margin recovery potential, as seen in the sharp EBITDA margin drop from 42.9% (2021) to 2.3% (2024), with only gradual improvement forecast.

- **Supply Chain Disruption:** Persistent supply chain challenges could disrupt product availability, inflate costs, and delay product launches. SEC disclosures flag inventory timing and operational execution as at-risk, jeopardizing revenue stability and margin discipline.

- **Intensifying Competitive Pressure:** Intel faces heightened competition across smartphones, wearables, services, cloud, and AI features. SEC filings note that competitive dynamics could erode pricing power and market share, impeding the effectiveness of product-cycle execution and innovation.

- **Execution and Regulatory Risks:** Strategic execution risk remains elevated, given the need for disciplined operating leverage and cost control amid transformation. Regulatory scrutiny, including data protection and cybersecurity issues, could create operational disruption, reputational harm, and undermine customer trust.
