**Key Investment Risks for ServiceNow Inc. (NOW):**

- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** SEC filings highlight that global macroeconomic pressures, including potential slowdowns in enterprise IT spending and premium-device replacement cycles, could weaken demand for ServiceNow’s products and services, directly impacting revenue growth and forecast reliability.

- **Margin Pressure from Inflation:** Persistent inflation may increase costs for components, logistics, and labor, thereby constraining operating leverage and limiting margin upside—even as the company prioritizes margin discipline. This risk is material given the company’s reliance on sustained margin improvement to support EPS growth.

- **Supply Chain Disruption:** Ongoing risks in the supply chain—impacting product availability, cost structure, inventory timing, and launch execution—could hinder ServiceNow’s ability to deliver new products and services on schedule, affecting both revenue realization and customer satisfaction.

- **Intensifying Competitive Pressure:** The company faces growing competition across cloud, AI, and enterprise IT service markets. Competitive challenges could erode pricing power, compress margins, and threaten ServiceNow’s market share if it fails to sustain product innovation and differentiation.

- **Regulatory and Cybersecurity Risks:** Increased regulatory scrutiny and the risk of cybersecurity or data-protection incidents could lead to operational disruptions, reputational harm, and loss of customer trust—potentially resulting in fines, legal costs, and lost business.
