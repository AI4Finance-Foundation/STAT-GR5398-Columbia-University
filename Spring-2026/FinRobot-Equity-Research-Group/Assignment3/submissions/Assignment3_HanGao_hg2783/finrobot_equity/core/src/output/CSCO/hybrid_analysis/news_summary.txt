Cisco Systems Inc. (CSCO) remains in focus as the tech sector rides the tailwinds of a strong overall market and surging interest in artificial intelligence (AI) and quantum computing. Cisco’s stock has outperformed recently, closing at $91.5 after a 2.15% gain and marking it as one of the notable winners in a month that saw the best stock market performance since 2020.

Financially, Cisco’s revenues experienced a dip in 2024 (-5.6%) but are projected to rebound with steady growth through 2027 (CAGR 3.3%). Contribution margins have improved, reaching 64.7% in 2024 and forecast to climb further, while EBITDA margins are set to rise sharply from 29.3% in 2024 to an estimated 44.8% by 2027, signaling improving operational efficiency. EPS is expected to steadily recover from $2.55 in 2024 to $3.05 by 2027, supported by both revenue growth and margin expansion.

On the news front, Cisco is drawing attention not just for its core networking business but as a beneficiary of broader technology trends. The advancement of the National Quantum Policy Bill highlights Cisco’s role alongside IBM and IonQ in commercializing quantum technology, opening up new growth avenues. Meanwhile, multiple reports suggest that the real winners of the ongoing AI boom may be companies like Cisco that apply AI to boost efficiency and profitability, rather than the builders of AI itself.

Investor sentiment is mixed but improving, with rising buzz and bullishness on platforms like X.com and Reddit. Cisco’s strong history of earnings surprises and its positioning at the intersection of AI and quantum computing make it a compelling watch in the near term. The upcoming Q3 FY2026 earnings call will be a key event for investors.

Investment implications: Cisco stands to benefit from the next wave of tech innovation, with improving fundamentals, positive sentiment, and exposure to AI and quantum trends. Continued margin expansion and potential earnings beats could support further upside, though investors should monitor execution and sector dynamics closely.
