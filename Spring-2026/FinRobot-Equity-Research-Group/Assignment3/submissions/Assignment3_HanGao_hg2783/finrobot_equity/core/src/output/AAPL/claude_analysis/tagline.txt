Apple Inc. delivers steady top-line growth with a projected revenue CAGR of 3.3% through 2027, supported by expanding contribution margins approaching 50% and EBITDA margins forecast to reach 44.8% by 2027. Despite commanding a premium EV/EBITDA multiple of approximately 27x relative to peers such as Amazon and Meta, the company demonstrates consistent earnings per share growth from $5.67 in 2021 to an estimated $8.93 by 2027. Apple's operational discipline, reflected in declining SG&A margins and robust profitability, underpins its position as a high-quality, margin-expanding franchise within the large-cap technology sector.
