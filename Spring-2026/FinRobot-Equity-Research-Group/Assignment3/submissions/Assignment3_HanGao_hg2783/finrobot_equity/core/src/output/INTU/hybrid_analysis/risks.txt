- **Inflationary Pressures**: Rising costs related to components, logistics, and labor could compress margins by increasing operating expenses and reducing consumer purchasing power, limiting upside from margin discipline.

- **Supply Chain Disruption**: Ongoing supply chain risks may affect product availability, cost structure, inventory timing, and execution of new product launches, posing a threat to both top-line growth and operational efficiency.

- **Competitive Pressure**: Intensifying competition in cloud, AI, and digital services could erode Intuit’s pricing power and market share, making it harder to maintain revenue growth and margin expansion.

- **Regulatory and Cybersecurity Risks**: Heightened regulatory scrutiny and potential cybersecurity or data-protection failures could result in operational disruption, reputational damage, and loss of customer trust, directly affecting business performance.
