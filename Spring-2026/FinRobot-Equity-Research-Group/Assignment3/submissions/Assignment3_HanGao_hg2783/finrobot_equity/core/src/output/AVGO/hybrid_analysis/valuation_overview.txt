Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) currently demonstrates robust financial performance, underpinned by strong historical revenue growth (24.2% CAGR from 2021 to 2025) and improving margins. Revenue has more than doubled from $27.4 billion in 2021 to $63.9 billion in 2025A, with a projected trajectory up to $73.9 billion by 2027E. Contribution margin and EBITDA margin have both strengthened over time, reaching 67.8% and 54.3% respectively in 2025A, with further expansion expected (up to 70.8% and 65.7% by 2027E). This margin expansion reflects the company’s focus on operating discipline and favorable product mix, themes consistently highlighted in recent SEC filings.

In terms of valuation metrics, the lack of a disclosed PE ratio limits direct price-based multiple analysis; however, sustained EPS growth (from $1.57 in 2021 to an estimated $5.86 by 2027E) and margin improvement signal strong underlying value creation. Compared to peers in the semiconductor and technology sectors, Broadcom’s double-digit revenue growth and best-in-class margins position it favorably on both growth and profitability axes.

The revenue growth assumptions for 2025–2027 (5.0%, 6.0%, 4.0%) are notably more conservative than historical averages, reflecting management’s acknowledgment of macroeconomic, competitive, and execution risks identified in the filings. These forecasts are supported by identified drivers: ongoing product launches, portfolio expansion, and resilient demand—aligned with management’s focus on innovation and strategic execution.

Overall, Broadcom’s valuation context appears supported by both historical performance and prudent forward assumptions. While macro and competitive headwinds pose risks, the company’s disciplined execution, margin resilience, and product innovation provide a credible foundation for its projected fair value.
