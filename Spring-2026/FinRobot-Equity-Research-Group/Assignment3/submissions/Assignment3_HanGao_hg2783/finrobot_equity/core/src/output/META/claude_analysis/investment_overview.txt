Meta Platforms, Inc. (META) Investment Update

Meta delivered another strong fiscal year in 2025, with revenue reaching approximately $201.0 billion, representing 22.2% year-over-year growth and continuing a recovery trajectory from the 2022 trough when revenue declined 1.1%. Over the 2021 to 2025 period, Meta compounded revenue at an average annual rate of 14.7%, underpinned by advertising demand resilience, product innovation across its family of apps, and strategic expansion into AI-driven monetization. EBITDA grew to approximately $104.5 billion in 2025, with EBITDA margin holding at 52.0%, a meaningful improvement from the 32.3% reported in 2022 and reflective of sustained operating leverage and cost discipline. SG&A as a percentage of revenue has declined steadily, falling from 23.2% in 2022 to 12.0% in 2025, signaling meaningful structural efficiency gains.

Key revenue drivers identified in recent SEC filings include product and service innovation, AI integration, geographic expansion particularly in Europe, and pricing and mix optimization. Management commentary emphasizes demand resilience and operating discipline as central priorities, with new product cycles expected to support continued ecosystem engagement and monetization.

Looking ahead, estimates project revenue of approximately $211.0 billion in 2025E, growing to $223.7 billion in 2026E and $232.6 billion in 2027E, reflecting a more moderate growth cadence of 5% to 6% annually. EBITDA margins are forecast to expand materially, reaching 71.5% in 2025E and 74.5% by 2027E, driven by operating leverage and continued SG&A efficiency. EPS is estimated to grow from $25.43 in 2025E to $28.60 in 2027E.

Key risks flagged in recent 10-K and 10-Q filings include macroeconomic uncertainty, inflationary cost pressures, competitive intensity across digital advertising and AI, regulatory scrutiny, and cybersecurity exposure. Investors should weigh these constraints against Meta's demonstrated margin discipline and strategic positioning in AI-driven advertising and platform expansion.
