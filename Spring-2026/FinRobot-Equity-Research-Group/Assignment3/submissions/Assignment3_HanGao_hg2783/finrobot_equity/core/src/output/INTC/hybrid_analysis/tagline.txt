Intel Corporation is experiencing declining revenues and profitability, with revenue contracting at a -9.6% CAGR since 2021 and EBITDA margins falling to a low of 2.3% in 2024. While a modest recovery in both revenue growth and margins is projected from 2025 onward, contribution and EBITDA margins are expected to remain below historical levels. Persistent negative EPS forecasts and compressed margins highlight ongoing operational and financial headwinds for the company.
