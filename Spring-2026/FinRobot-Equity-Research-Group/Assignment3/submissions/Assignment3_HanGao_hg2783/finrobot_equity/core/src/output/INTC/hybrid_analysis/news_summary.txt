Intel Corporation (INTC) has experienced a notable turnaround in recent months following a prolonged period of declining financial performance. Revenue has contracted at a -9.6% CAGR from 2021’s $79B to an estimated $53B in 2024, with 2023 showing a -14% drop and 2024 expected to be relatively flat. Margins have also compressed, with contribution margin falling from 55.4% in 2021 to 32.7% in 2024, and EBITDA margin plummeting from 42.9% to just 2.3% in the same period. EPS turned negative in 2024, reflecting the challenges Intel faces in profitability.

Despite these headwinds, recent news points to a dramatic rally in Intel’s stock, which surged from under $40 to about $94, driven by a strong Q1 2026 earnings report and a 22% YoY jump in DCAI (Data Center and AI) revenue. This suggests that demand is now outpacing supply, particularly as the industry shifts from GPU training to CPU-driven inference and agentic workloads. Some analysts are bullish, predicting that Intel’s recent 122% rally could continue, while others remain cautious, with JPMorgan’s Harlan Sur recommending selling Intel in favor of Nvidia, citing ongoing competitive pressures.

Intel’s recent investment in chip startup SambaNova received U.S. antitrust clearance, strengthening its AI and data center ambitions. Additionally, Intel stands to benefit from broader trends such as the buildout of infrastructure for anticipated large IPOs like SpaceX, positioning it as a key enabler in the AI and aerospace sectors.

Retail sentiment is mixed, with X.com users showing moderate bullishness (54%), while Reddit sentiment is less optimistic (29%), and overall buzz remains high but trending lower. The wide divergence in source alignment reflects ongoing debate about Intel’s future prospects.

Investment implications: While Intel’s financials remain challenged, recent operational momentum, strategic investments, and sectoral tailwinds have fueled a strong stock rally. However, competitive risks and margin pressures persist, making Intel a higher-risk, potentially higher-reward proposition for investors.
