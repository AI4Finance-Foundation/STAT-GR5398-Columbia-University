**Key Investment Risks for Salesforce Inc. (CRM):**

- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** SEC filings highlight that global economic volatility may weaken discretionary enterprise software spending, potentially slowing Salesforce’s revenue growth, which has already decelerated from 18.3% (2023A) to an estimated 4–6% CAGR (2025E–2027E).

- **Inflationary Pressure:** Prolonged inflation could increase costs for components, logistics, and labor, compressing operating margins and offsetting recent gains in EBITDA margin (26.5% in 2024A, projected to 40.4% by 2027E).

- **Supply Chain Disruption:** Ongoing exposure to supply chain risks could impact Salesforce’s ability to deliver products and services on schedule, affecting customer satisfaction and financial performance, as flagged in recent SEC filings.

- **Intensifying Competition:** Heightened competition in cloud, AI, and enterprise software markets could pressure pricing power and market share, challenging Salesforce’s ability to sustain revenue growth and margin expansion.

- **Regulatory and Cybersecurity Risks:** Increased regulatory scrutiny and the threat of cybersecurity or data-protection breaches could result in operational disruptions, reputational harm, and loss of customer trust, as noted in the latest 10-K and 10-Q disclosures.
