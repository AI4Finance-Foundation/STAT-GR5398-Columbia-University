Earnings analysis for International Business Machines Corporation is constructive but execution-sensitive. The latest actual period shows revenue growth and earnings resilience, while the forecast depends on continued margin expansion, cost discipline, and demand durability. The key debate is whether International Business Machines Corporation can sustain EPS growth and justify its valuation premium through product-cycle execution, recurring revenue growth, and operating leverage.

Key Earnings Read-Through
- International Business Machines Corporation generated $67.5B of revenue in 2025A; latest observed revenue growth was 7.6%, providing the starting point for the earnings forecast.
- Year-over-year revenue growth was 7.6%, indicating a constructive recovery in top-line momentum.
- EPS increased to $11.36 in 2025A and is forecast to reach $13.55 by 2027E; the implied earnings upside depends on sustaining revenue growth while converting margin expansion into per-share profit growth.
- EBITDA is forecast to increase from $17.3B in 2025A to $25.1B in 2027E; this makes operating leverage and margin execution the central earnings debate.
- EBITDA margin is projected to expand from 25.6% in 2025A to 32.1% in 2027E; the magnitude of this step-up should be treated as an important execution assumption rather than a mechanical extrapolation.
- Contribution margin is expected to rise from 59.5% to 62.5%, supporting the earnings growth outlook through pricing discipline, services mix, and cost control.
- SG&A margin is expected to move from 31.9% to 30.4%, indicating continued cost discipline.

Key Watch Items
- Revenue growth versus forecast assumptions
- Margin guidance and operating leverage
- Product or segment demand trends
- Competitive pressure and pricing power
- Capital allocation and shareholder returns
