- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** SEC filings highlight that global macroeconomic pressure could slow premium-device replacement cycles and weaken discretionary consumer demand, posing a direct risk to Micron's top-line growth and recovery trajectory after the sharp 2023 revenue decline.

- **Inflationary Pressures:** Rising costs for components, logistics, and labor—along with reduced consumer purchasing power—may limit margin upside, despite management’s focus on margin discipline and operating leverage. Elevated inflation could undermine the forecasted margin recovery and EPS growth.

- **Supply Chain Disruptions:** The company faces risk of supply chain interruptions that could impact product availability, cost structure, inventory timing, and the execution of new product launches, potentially jeopardizing both revenue and margin targets.

- **Intense Competitive Pressure:** SEC filings and company disclosures note heightened competition in smartphones, wearables, cloud, and AI markets, which could pressure pricing power, limit market share gains, and constrain Micron’s ability to capitalize on anticipated demand resilience and product-cycle upsides.

- **Regulatory and Cybersecurity Risks:** Increased regulatory scrutiny and the potential for cybersecurity or data-protection breaches could result in operational disruption, reputational damage, and erosion of customer trust, impacting both short-term performance and long-term strategic expansion.
