Qualcomm (QCOM) shares have surged in recent days, jumping as much as 34% in the past week and over 42% in the past month, closing April at $179.58. The rally was triggered by the company’s Q2 FY26 earnings report, which, while only modestly beating expectations, signaled a significant strategic shift and renewed investor optimism.

A key driver behind the stock’s outperformance is Qualcomm’s push into the data center market. CEO Cristiano Amon revealed progress with a large hyperscaler customer, fueling speculation about new high-value partnerships and diversifying Qualcomm’s traditionally mobile-focused revenue streams. The news of an imminent deal in this lucrative sector boosted sentiment, even as the broader tech sector experienced volatility.

Despite recent slumps in some fundamentals—such as a 19% revenue decline in 2023 and a dip in EBITDA margin—the company is showing signs of recovery. Revenues are projected to rebound with a 7.2% CAGR through 2027, and margins are expected to improve, with EBITDA margin forecast to rise from 32.7% in 2024 to over 52% by 2027. Contribution margins are also stabilizing above 55%. While EPS guidance for 2025 is below current levels, the market appears focused on long-term growth and Qualcomm’s expanding role in AI and data infrastructure.

Technical indicators point to a breakout, and Wall Street analysts have raised price targets, with several firms citing the AI ramp and data center initiatives as key catalysts. Retail sentiment is mixed but leans bullish overall, with particularly strong momentum on social platforms like X.com.

In summary, Qualcomm is gaining traction on its diversification strategy, with investors rewarding its progress in new markets and improved outlook, despite near-term headwinds. The company’s ability to capitalize on AI and data center trends could drive further upside for long-term investors.
