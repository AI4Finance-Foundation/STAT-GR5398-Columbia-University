Applied Materials Inc. (AMAT) has been in the spotlight recently, with its stock price experiencing significant volatility. Shares surged 3.1% on April 30, 2026, reaching $394.49, capping off a remarkable 150% rally over the past year. This run-up has sent the trailing P/E ratio soaring from 19.1x to 42.2x, far outpacing the company’s revenue growth, which expanded by only 2.8% in 2023 and is expected to grow at a 5.3% CAGR through 2027. The increasing valuation raises questions about sustainability, especially as broader AI-linked semiconductor stocks face pressure amid concerns over data-center spending and growth expectations.

Financially, AMAT continues to deliver steady operational performance. Revenue and EBITDA margins are expected to expand, with EBITDA margin rising from 32% in 2021 to a projected 47% by 2027. Contribution margins are also increasing, indicating improved efficiency, while SG&A as a percentage of revenue is trending lower. Earnings per share (EPS) are forecast to grow from $8.16 in 2023 to $10.39 by 2027.

Notably, the competitive landscape is shifting. Despite U.S. sanctions, Chinese semiconductor equipment companies are gaining market share, which could impact AMAT’s future growth in a key market. Meanwhile, product innovation and investor demand remain strong, as evidenced by the launch of the Defiance Daily Target 2X Long AMAT ETF—a leveraged product catering to active traders.

Retail sentiment is mixed, with social media buzz declining on both Reddit and X.com, though sentiment remains more bullish on X.com (62% bullish). Institutional activity, like Exencial Wealth Advisors trimming its stake by 34%, suggests some profit-taking.

Investment implications: While AMAT’s strong financials and innovation position it well in the semiconductor cycle, the elevated valuation, competitive threats from China, and emerging volatility in AI-linked stocks warrant a cautious, selective approach for new investors.
