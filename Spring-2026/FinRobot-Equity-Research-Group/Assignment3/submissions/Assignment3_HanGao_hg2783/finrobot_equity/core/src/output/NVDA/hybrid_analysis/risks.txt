- **Macroeconomic & Inflation Risk:** Macroeconomic uncertainty and persistent inflation (as highlighted in recent 10-K and 10-Q filings) could slow premium-device replacement cycles, weaken discretionary consumer demand, and pressure component, logistics, and labor costs, thereby limiting margin upside and constraining revenue growth.

- **Supply Chain Disruption:** Ongoing supply chain exposure presents risks to product availability, cost structure, inventory timing, and launch execution, potentially disrupting NVIDIA’s ability to meet demand and maintain forecasted revenue and margin growth.

- **Competitive Pressure:** Intensifying competition across AI, cloud, smartphones, wearables, and services threatens NVIDIA’s pricing power and market share. The risk is amplified by rapid product innovation cycles and new entrants, directly impacting revenue trajectory and margin discipline.

- **Regulatory & Geopolitical Scrutiny:** Heightened regulatory scrutiny and potential geopolitical restrictions (noted in SEC filings) may affect market access, product approvals, and overall strategic execution, posing risks to expansion plans and revenue forecasts.

- **Execution & Cybersecurity Risk:** Execution risk around product-cycle launches and strategic initiatives, combined with potential cybersecurity and data-protection issues, could result in operational disruptions, reputational damage, and weakened customer trust, ultimately impacting financial performance.
