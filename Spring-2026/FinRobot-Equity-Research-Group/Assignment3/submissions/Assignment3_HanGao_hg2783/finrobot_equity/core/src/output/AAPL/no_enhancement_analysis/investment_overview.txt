Apple Inc. (AAPL) Investment Update

Apple’s recent financial performance demonstrates stable growth with improving profitability. Fiscal 2023 revenue was $383.3 billion, reflecting a modest -2.8% decline year-over-year after a strong 7.8% gain in 2022. However, Apple rebounded in 2024, posting a 2.0% revenue increase to $391.0 billion, with consensus estimates projecting further acceleration to $416.2 billion in 2025. Over the period from 2021 to 2027, revenue is expected to grow at a 3.3% CAGR, reflecting Apple’s resilience amid macroeconomic headwinds.

Key revenue drivers remain Apple’s diverse hardware portfolio, continued strength in iPhone and wearables, and robust growth in services. Notably, contribution margin improved from 41.8% in 2021 to 46.2% in 2024, and is forecast to reach 49.9% by 2027. This margin expansion is likely driven by operating leverage in high-margin services and disciplined cost control, as outlined in recent SEC filings. SG&A expenses have been managed effectively, with SG&A margin projected to decline from 6.7% in 2024 to 5.1% by 2027, supporting profitability gains.

EBITDA margin has improved from 33.7% in 2021 to 34.5% in 2024, with a significant step-up to 44.8% forecast by 2027—reflecting the growing share of higher-margin businesses and scale efficiencies. Earnings per share (EPS) growth is also strong: after stabilizing at $6.16 in 2023, EPS is projected to reach $7.94 in 2025 and $8.93 by 2027.

Looking forward, Apple’s financial outlook is underpinned by ongoing innovation, expanding services, and operational efficiency initiatives disclosed in recent SEC filings. With improving margins, controlled expenses, and consistent revenue growth, Apple remains positioned for sustained shareholder returns.
