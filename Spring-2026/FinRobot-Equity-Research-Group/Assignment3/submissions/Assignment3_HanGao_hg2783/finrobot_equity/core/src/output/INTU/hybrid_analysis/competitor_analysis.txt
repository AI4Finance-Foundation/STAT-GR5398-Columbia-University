Intuit Inc. demonstrates robust financial performance and outpaces many peers in the financial software sector, driven by consistent double-digit revenue growth and strong profitability metrics. From 2021A to 2027E, Intuit’s revenues are projected to grow at an 18.2% CAGR, indicating sustained market demand and effective expansion strategies. Revenue growth remains strong, with annual increases ranging from 12.9% to 32.1% in recent years, though forecasted to moderate to 4-6% by 2027E—still healthy for a mature software company.

Intuit’s contribution margin remains high, consistently above 78% and forecasted to rise to 83.8% in 2027E, reflecting strong pricing power, scalable cloud-based offerings, and efficient cost of operations. EBITDA margins, a key profitability indicator, are also set to improve significantly—from 30.6% in 2021A to a projected 50.1% in 2027E. This margin expansion suggests Intuit is leveraging operational efficiencies and benefiting from its scale, outpacing typical peers in the industry, where EBITDA margins often lag due to higher R&D or customer acquisition costs.

SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue are expected to decline steadily from 39.2% in 2022A to 33.7% by 2027E, highlighting disciplined cost management. Earnings per share (EPS) are also projected to grow rapidly, from $7.64 in 2021A to $16.48 in 2027E, underscoring superior shareholder value creation.

Compared to peers, Intuit’s combination of high growth, expanding margins, and strong earnings trajectory positions it as a best-in-class operator within financial software. Its ability to maintain high contribution and EBITDA margins while scaling revenue and controlling expenses gives it a decisive competitive edge in efficiency and profitability.
