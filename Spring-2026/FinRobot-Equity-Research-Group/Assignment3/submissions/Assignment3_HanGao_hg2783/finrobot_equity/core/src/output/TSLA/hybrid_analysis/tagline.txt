Tesla maintains a solid revenue base with a 15.2% CAGR from 2021 to 2027, though recent years show decelerating growth and margin compression. EBITDA margins have declined from 21.7% in 2022 to an estimated 13.3% in 2025, reflecting operational pressures. Despite near-term profitability challenges, contribution margin is projected to gradually recover, reaching 21.0% by 2027.
