Microsoft’s current valuation metrics reflect robust operating performance and sustained growth prospects. Revenue has grown at a 13.8% CAGR from 2021A to 2027E, with a notable increase from $168.1B (2021A) to a projected $326.1B (2027E). EBITDA margins have also improved materially, rising from 50.6% in 2021A to a forecasted 61.6% by 2027E, indicating effective cost control and operating leverage—both highlighted as management priorities in recent SEC filings.

Comparing to peers, Microsoft’s 2023 EV/EBITDA multiple of 24.5x is at a premium relative to GOOGL (18.0x), META (15.4x), and AAPL (21.6x), and above AMZN (18.2x). This premium appears justified by Microsoft’s higher margins, consistent revenue growth, and the market’s confidence in its strategic positioning across cloud, AI, and enterprise software. Microsoft’s EBITDA also compares favorably, with $105.1B in 2023A, tracking closely behind AAPL and outpacing META and AMZN.

The fair value context, as reflected in these multiples, aligns with Microsoft’s strong execution and resilience. SEC filings reinforce the outlook by emphasizing product innovation, ecosystem expansion, and margin discipline as key catalysts, while acknowledging macro, competitive, and regulatory risks. The company’s forecasted revenue growth rates—5.0% (2025E), 6.0% (2026E), and 4.0% (2027E)—are below historical averages but remain credible. They are supported by ongoing product and segment expansion, continued demand resilience, and strategic initiatives in AI and cloud, as cited in both revenue driver analysis and management commentary.

In summary, Microsoft’s valuation premium is supported by superior margins, consistent growth, and credible revenue drivers, though investors should monitor macro and competitive pressures flagged in the filings. The forward growth assumptions appear reasonable given the company’s demonstrated execution and identified catalysts.
