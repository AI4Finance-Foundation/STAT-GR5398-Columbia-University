Amazon demonstrates robust revenue growth with an 11.1% CAGR projected through 2027, supported by steadily expanding contribution and EBITDA margins. Operational efficiencies are driving margin improvement, with contribution margin rising from 42.0% in 2021 to an estimated 53.3% in 2027 and EBITDA margin expected to reach 46.7%. Earnings per share are recovering strongly after 2022, reflecting increasing profitability and scale across Amazon’s core businesses.
