- **Macroeconomic Uncertainty:** Recent SEC filings highlight that broader economic pressures—such as slowing premium-device replacement cycles and weaker discretionary consumer demand—could negatively impact Alphabet’s revenue growth, especially given forecasts that rely on continued demand resilience.

- **Inflationary Cost Pressures:** The company explicitly identifies inflation as a risk to its component, logistics, and labor costs. Elevated inflation could limit operating margin expansion and constrain Alphabet’s ability to deliver forecasted EBITDA margin improvements.

- **Supply Chain Disruption:** SEC filings note that supply chain issues may affect product availability, cost structure, inventory timing, and the execution of new product launches, posing a direct threat to both revenue growth and operating leverage targets.

- **Intensifying Competitive Pressure:** Alphabet faces heightened competition in smartphones, wearables, cloud, services, and AI, which could erode pricing power and market share, challenging the sustainability of its revenue and margin growth as projected.

- **Regulatory and Cybersecurity Risks:** Ongoing regulatory scrutiny and potential cybersecurity/data protection incidents are flagged in filings as threats that could create operational disruption, reputational damage, and undermine customer trust, with potential impacts on both top-line and bottom-line performance.
