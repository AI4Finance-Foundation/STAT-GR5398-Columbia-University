Apple Inc. (AAPL) continues to be a leading player among large-cap tech peers, including Amazon (AMZN), Alphabet/Google (GOOGL), Meta Platforms (META), and Microsoft (MSFT). Apple’s revenue growth has moderated in recent years, with a CAGR of 3.3% projected through 2027, and annual revenue growth rates between -2.8% and 6.4%. This is relatively modest compared to the high-growth periods seen earlier in the decade, suggesting a mature business but with steady expansion.

Apple’s EBITDA performance stands out for its consistency and scale. In 2023, Apple’s EBITDA reached $129B, second only to Microsoft ($105B) among peers and well ahead of Amazon, Google, and Meta. While Amazon and Google are expected to surpass Apple in EBITDA by 2026, Apple’s EBITDA margins are robust—rising from 33.7% in 2021 to a projected 44.8% in 2027, indicating strong operational efficiency and cost control. Contribution margins also improve year-over-year, approaching 50% by 2027, further highlighting Apple’s profitability leadership.

On valuation, Apple trades at a premium. Its EV/EBITDA multiple for 2023 is 21.6x, higher than Google (18.0x), Amazon (18.2x), and Meta (15.4x), and closely aligned with Microsoft (24.5x). This premium reflects Apple’s brand strength, recurring revenues, and stable margins, but also suggests that investors expect continued operational outperformance.

In summary, Apple is a mature, highly profitable tech leader with industry-leading margins and stable growth. While it faces increasing competition in EBITDA scale from Amazon and Google, its superior margins and premium valuation underscore enduring investor confidence in its business model and financial discipline.
