Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) is one of the world's largest technology conglomerates, operating primarily through its flagship subsidiary Google. The company's business model is built around a diversified portfolio of digital products and services, with advertising revenue serving as its core revenue engine. Google's search platform, YouTube, and its broader advertising network collectively represent the dominant share of Alphabet's top-line results, while the company also derives meaningful and growing contributions from Google Cloud, hardware products, and a collection of other ventures housed under the Other Bets segment.

Alphabet holds a commanding position in the global digital advertising market, competing directly with peers such as Meta, Microsoft, Amazon, and Apple across various segments of the technology landscape. Its scale, data infrastructure, and brand recognition provide durable competitive advantages that have supported consistent revenue growth over the past several years.

From a financial performance standpoint, Alphabet has demonstrated strong and accelerating growth. Revenue expanded from approximately $257.6 billion in 2021 to $403.0 billion in 2025, representing a compound annual growth rate of 11.8 percent. Revenue growth notably accelerated to 15.1 percent in 2025 after a period of moderation in 2022 and 2023. More impressively, profitability has expanded significantly over this period, with EBITDA margins improving from 40.2 percent in 2021 to 44.7 percent in 2025, and contribution margins rising steadily from 56.9 percent to 59.7 percent over the same period. Earnings per share have grown from $5.69 in 2021 to $10.91 in 2025, reflecting both operational leverage and active capital returns.

In the context of its peer group, Alphabet's EBITDA of approximately $180 billion in 2025 positions it competitively alongside Microsoft and ahead of Apple, Meta, and Amazon on an absolute basis. Its EV/EBITDA multiple of 21.2 times in 2025 reflects a premium relative to recent historical levels, suggesting the market is pricing in continued margin expansion and growth, consistent with forward estimates projecting EBITDA margins approaching 50 percent by 2026 and revenue reaching approximately $466 billion by 2027.
