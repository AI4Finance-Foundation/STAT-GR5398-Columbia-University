Apple Inc. (AAPL) continues to deliver solid financial performance, anchored by its robust product ecosystem and increasing services monetization. Over the past few years, Apple’s revenue has grown at a moderate 3.4% CAGR, rising from $365.8 billion in 2021 to $416.2 billion in 2025, with a forecast of $436.97 billion in 2025E. The latest revenue growth was 6.4%, reflecting ongoing demand resilience and effective product-cycle execution. Key revenue drivers include product refreshes, pricing and mix discipline, expanding services, and strong installed-base retention.

Profitability remains a core strength. Apple’s contribution margin has steadily improved from 41.8% in 2021 to 46.9% in 2025A, with further expansion forecasted. EBITDA margins have also improved (33.7% in 2021 to 34.7% in 2025A), and are expected to rise sharply to 41.8% in 2025E and beyond, indicating operating leverage and cost control. EPS is projected to rise from $6.16 in 2023 to $7.94 in 2025E, supported by margin improvement and share repurchases.

Recent SEC filings reinforce Apple’s focus on ecosystem-led growth, services monetization, and margin discipline, while highlighting risks such as macroeconomic uncertainty, inflation, supply chain challenges, competitive intensity, and regulatory scrutiny. Management emphasizes that sustained innovation, installed-base monetization, and customer retention will underpin long-term value creation. However, downside risks—such as slowing replacement cycles, inflationary pressures, and supply chain disruptions—remain relevant.

In peer comparison, Apple maintains a strong EBITDA profile, though its EV/EBITDA multiple stands at the higher end among peers, reflecting market confidence in its business model and margin outlook.

Looking forward, Apple is well-positioned for continued moderate revenue growth and margin expansion, provided it navigates macro and competitive risks while capitalizing on ecosystem and services opportunities.
