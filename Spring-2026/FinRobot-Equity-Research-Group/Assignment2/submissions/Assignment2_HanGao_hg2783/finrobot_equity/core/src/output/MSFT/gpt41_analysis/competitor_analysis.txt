Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) continues to demonstrate robust financial performance relative to its major technology peers: Apple (AAPL), Amazon (AMZN), Alphabet/Google (GOOGL), and Meta Platforms (META). With a compound annual revenue growth rate (CAGR) of 13.8% (2021-2027E), Microsoft is posting healthy top-line growth, outpacing the typical mature tech company profile and aligning with peers’ expansion trajectories.

On profitability, Microsoft’s EBITDA margin is notably strong, rising from 50.6% in 2021 to a projected 61.6% by 2027E. This outperforms most peers, as evidenced by its 2023 EBITDA ($105.1B) being near the top of the group, and projected to reach $200.9B in 2027. While Apple frequently leads in absolute EBITDA, Microsoft is closing the gap and is forecasted to surpass several peers (notably Amazon, Meta, and Alphabet) in the coming years.

Microsoft’s cost discipline is evident in its declining SG&A margin, dropping from 15.0% in 2021 to an estimated 10.2% by 2027E, reflecting increasing operational efficiency. Its contribution margin, climbing from 68.9% to 71.8% over the same period, reinforces its strong profitability profile.

In terms of valuation, Microsoft trades at a premium EV/EBITDA multiple compared to peers, with a 2023 ratio of 24.5x versus Apple’s 21.6x, Alphabet’s 18.0x, and Meta’s 15.4x. This reflects investor confidence in Microsoft’s sustained earnings growth and margin strength. However, this premium also suggests higher expectations and potentially less room for error.

Overall, Microsoft stands out for its consistent growth, expanding margins, and operational efficiency, positioning itself as a top-tier performer among large-cap tech companies, albeit at a higher valuation multiple than most peers.
