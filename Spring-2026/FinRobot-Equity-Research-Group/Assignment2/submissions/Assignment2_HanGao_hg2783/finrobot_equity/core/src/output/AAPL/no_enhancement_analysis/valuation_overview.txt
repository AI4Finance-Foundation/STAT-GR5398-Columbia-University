Apple Inc.’s current valuation is underpinned by modest but steady revenue growth, improving margins, and robust profitability metrics. Revenues grew at a 3.3% CAGR from 2021 to 2027E, with historical revenue expanding from $366 billion in 2021 to a projected $482 billion in 2027. After a slight dip in 2023 (-2.8%), growth resumes in 2024 (2%), accelerating to 5–6% in 2025–2026E, then moderating to 4% in 2027E. This trajectory suggests a resilient revenue base supported by incremental product innovation, recurring services income, and possible expansion in new markets.

Margin trends provide further support for Apple’s valuation. Contribution margin rises from 41.8% in 2021 to an estimated 49.9% in 2027E, while EBITDA margin expands from 33.7% to 44.8% over the same period—indicating both operational efficiency and a shift toward higher-margin revenue streams, likely from services and ecosystem monetization. SG&A margin is well controlled, falling from 6.7% in 2024 to just over 5% in 2027E.

EPS growth mirrors these improvements, moving from $5.67 in 2021 to a projected $8.93 in 2027E. While price/earnings ratios are not provided, Apple’s sustained margin expansion and EPS growth could justify a premium to peers, especially given its scale and brand strength.

Peer comparison would likely show Apple at the upper end of valuation multiples, reflecting its consistent profitability and cash generation. Revenue growth assumptions appear reasonable and are supported by the ongoing margin improvement; likely drivers include continued iPhone and hardware refreshes, growth in services, and disciplined cost management, all of which are consistent with Apple’s disclosures in recent SEC filings. Overall, the fair value context appears constructive, with Apple’s resilient financial profile justifying ongoing investor confidence.
