## 1. Executive summary

### Analyst commentary

Across the headline metrics tracked for fiscal 2026, reported results came in below the prior internal model estimate row on each dimension—revenue, EBITDA, EBITDA margin, and earnings per share—reflecting a consistent directional variance to the downside relative to that reference point. While the breadth of the shortfall warrants attention, it is worth contextualizing the outcome within NVIDIA's ongoing capacity ramp, evolving hyperscaler demand cadence, and the inherent complexity of modeling a business scaling at this pace; single-period variance across all four metrics does not, on its own, alter the structural demand thesis underpinning the Data Center franchise. Going forward, the focus will appropriately shift to management's forward commentary on supply availability, customer deployment timelines, and the trajectory of the Gaming and Professional Visualization segments, which can provide incremental clarity on the pacing of revenue recognition.
