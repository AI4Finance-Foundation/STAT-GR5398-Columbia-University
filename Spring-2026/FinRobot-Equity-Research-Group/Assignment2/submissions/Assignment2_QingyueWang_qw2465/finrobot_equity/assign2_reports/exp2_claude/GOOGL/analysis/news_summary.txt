Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) Summary - April 29, 2026

Alphabet delivered a standout earnings performance in Q1 2026, emerging as the clear winner among Big Tech on a monster earnings day. The company reported EPS of $5.11, nearly doubling the consensus estimate of $2.64, and significantly ahead of the $2.81 reported in the prior year period. Revenue and earnings both beat expectations, driving GOOGL shares higher while peers like Meta faced selling pressure despite their own beats.

The most significant development was Google Cloud surpassing $20 billion in quarterly revenue for the first time, representing 63% year-over-year growth. Management noted the growth was actually capacity-constrained, suggesting even stronger demand exists than reported results reflect. This is a critical signal for investors, as it implies continued capital expenditure is directly tied to revenue opportunity rather than speculative spending.

Alphabet is also accelerating its transformation of core Search into a transaction engine, with AI Mode shifting from experimental to operational. The company confirmed openness to introducing advertising within its Gemini AI app, which could unlock a significant new monetization layer. Investors clearly differentiated Alphabet's AI spending narrative from Meta's, rewarding Alphabet's capex guidance increase while punishing Meta's similar announcement.

Financially, the trajectory remains compelling. EBITDA margins are projected to expand from 44.7% in 2025 to 51.7% by 2027, while EPS is expected to grow steadily toward $13.01. The stock trades at roughly 25.9x 2026 estimated earnings, a reasonable multiple given the growth profile.

Retail sentiment is broadly elevated with buzz scores above 80 across Reddit and X, though Reddit shows notably lower bullish conviction at 38%, suggesting some skepticism among retail investors despite institutional enthusiasm. Overall, Alphabet presents a strong near-term investment case driven by cloud momentum and AI monetization.
