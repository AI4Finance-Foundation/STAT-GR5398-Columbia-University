# NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) - Key Investment Risks

- **Revenue Growth Deceleration Risk:** Revenue growth is projected to collapse from 65.5% (2026A) to just 4-6% (2025E-2027E), signaling potential market saturation in AI/data center demand; any shortfall from even these modest estimates could trigger significant multiple compression given NVDA's still-elevated EV/EBITDA of ~31-34x versus AMD's historical range of 18-57x

- **Margin Sustainability Concern:** Despite projected EBITDA margin expansion to 73.5% by 2027E, Cost of Operations has grown disproportionately from $9.4B (2022A) to $62.5B (2026A) — a 562% increase — suggesting rising supply chain and manufacturing costs that could compress margins if pricing power weakens amid increased competition

- **Valuation Premium Vulnerability:** NVDA trades at EV/EBITDA of ~31-34x (2026E-2027E) versus Intel's 14.5x and AMD's historical ~38-57x range; with EPS growth slowing from 66% (2025A) to ~6% (2027E), the current P/E of ~32-36x leaves limited margin of safety if AI capital expenditure cycles turn

- **Customer Concentration & Demand Cyclicality Risk:** The explosive revenue surge from $27B (2023A) to $216B (2026A) is heavily dependent on hyperscaler AI infrastructure spending; any pullback in cloud capex budgets or delays in AI monetization by Microsoft, Google, or Meta could cause severe demand shocks

- **Competitive & Geopolitical Displacement Risk:** AMD's projected EBITDA growth to $8.6B (2027E) and Intel's recovery to $47.4B signal intensifying competition; simultaneously, U.S. export restrictions on advanced chips to China — historically a significant revenue contributor — represent a structural headwind that could impair the 2027E revenue trajectory of ~$250B
