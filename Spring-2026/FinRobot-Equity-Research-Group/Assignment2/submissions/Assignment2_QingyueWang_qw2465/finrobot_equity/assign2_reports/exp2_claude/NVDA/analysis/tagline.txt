NVIDIA has delivered extraordinary revenue growth of 68.3% CAGR from 2022 to 2026, driven by surging AI infrastructure demand that propelled EBITDA margins from 22.2% in 2023 to nearly 67% in 2026, far outpacing semiconductor peers AMD and Intel. The company's expanding contribution margins, disciplined SG&A leverage declining to just 2.1% of revenue, and EPS growth from $0.18 to $4.93 over the same period underscore a highly scalable and increasingly profitable business model. Trading at a forward EV/EBITDA of approximately 31x on 2026 estimates, NVIDIA commands a justified premium valuation reflective of its dominant positioning in accelerated computing and continued earnings momentum through 2027.
