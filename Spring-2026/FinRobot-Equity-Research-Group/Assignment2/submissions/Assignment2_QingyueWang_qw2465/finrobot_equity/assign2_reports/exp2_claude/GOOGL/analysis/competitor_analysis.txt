Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) Competitor Analysis vs. Meta Platforms (META) and Microsoft (MSFT)

Alphabet has demonstrated strong and accelerating financial performance, with revenue growing at an 11.8% CAGR from 2021 to 2025, reaching approximately $403 billion in 2025. Its EBITDA margin has expanded meaningfully from 40.2% in 2021 to 44.7% in 2025, reflecting disciplined cost management and operating leverage. Projections suggest further margin expansion toward 51.7% by 2027, which would place Alphabet ahead of most large-cap technology peers on a profitability basis.

In absolute EBITDA terms, Alphabet leads its peer group. Its 2025 EBITDA of approximately $180 billion surpasses Microsoft's $160 billion and is nearly double Meta's $105 billion. However, Microsoft has historically commanded a premium valuation, with EV/EBITDA multiples consistently ranging between 20x and 26x, compared to Alphabet's 13x to 21x range. This valuation gap likely reflects Microsoft's strong enterprise software positioning, Azure cloud growth, and perceived AI integration through its OpenAI partnership. Meta, while trading at a discount to both peers during 2022, has recovered meaningfully and now trades closer to Alphabet's multiples, supported by its aggressive AI-driven advertising efficiency gains.

Alphabet's SG&A margin has declined from 15.0% in 2022 to 12.5% in 2025, signaling improving operational efficiency. EPS has grown from $4.59 in 2022 to $10.91 in 2025, reflecting both earnings growth and share buybacks. Overall, Alphabet presents a compelling combination of scale, profitability, and reasonable valuation relative to peers, though closing the valuation gap with Microsoft remains a key challenge tied to demonstrating sustained AI monetization.
