Apple Inc. Competitor Analysis

Apple competes directly with Alphabet (GOOGL) and Microsoft (MSFT) across hardware, software, and increasingly cloud and AI-driven services. Each company commands significant scale, but meaningful differences in growth trajectory, profitability, and valuation have emerged in recent years.

On an absolute EBITDA basis, Apple led the peer group through 2023, generating $129.2 billion versus Alphabet's $98.0 billion and Microsoft's $105.1 billion. However, this leadership has eroded. By 2025, both Alphabet and Microsoft surpassed Apple, posting $180.0 billion and $160.2 billion respectively against Apple's $144.4 billion. Projections for 2026 reinforce this divergence, with Alphabet expected to reach $210.7 billion and Microsoft $188.0 billion, while Apple trails at $150.4 billion. This suggests Apple's core hardware-driven business model is generating slower earnings momentum relative to peers with greater exposure to cloud infrastructure and AI workloads.

From a valuation standpoint, Apple trades at an EV/EBITDA multiple of approximately 27.0x in 2025, a meaningful premium to Alphabet at 21.2x and slightly above Microsoft at 23.6x. This premium appears increasingly difficult to justify given Apple's slower EBITDA growth. Alphabet in particular offers a compelling combination of accelerating earnings growth and a discounted multiple, making it arguably the more attractive risk-reward proposition among the three.

Apple's revenue CAGR of 3.3% through the forecast period further underscores a maturing growth profile. While expanding contribution margins and disciplined SG&A management reflect strong operational execution, Apple must accelerate its services segment and demonstrate credible AI monetization to defend its premium valuation against faster-growing peers.
