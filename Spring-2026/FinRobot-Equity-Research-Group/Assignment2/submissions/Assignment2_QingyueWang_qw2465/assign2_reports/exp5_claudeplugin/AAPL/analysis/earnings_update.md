## 1. Executive summary

On the selected operating lines, reported results **trailed** our internal model estimates in **2** area(s) and **exceeded** in **0** (see table below).

### Analyst commentary

Apple's fiscal 2025 results came in broadly in line with the prior internal model estimate at the revenue and earnings-per-share level, suggesting the top-line and bottom-line trajectory was reasonably well anticipated. The variance of note emerged at the EBITDA and EBITDA margin lines, where outcomes fell short of the prior estimate row, pointing to cost or mix dynamics that ran modestly ahead of what the model had embedded for the period. While the margin variance warrants monitoring in the context of ongoing investment cycles and segment mix, the inline revenue and EPS outcomes preserve the core elements of the long-term thesis around ecosystem monetization and services growth. Management's forward commentary on cost structure and segment contribution will be a natural focus as the estimate framework is refreshed, and the overall picture remains one of measured variance rather than a directional shift in the fundamental story.
