Apple Inc. (AAPL) Valuation Analysis

Current Valuation Metrics

Apple currently trades at a trailing P/E of approximately 34.1x based on 2025 actual EPS of $7.49, which steps down to an estimated 32.4x on 2025E EPS of $7.94 and further to 29.2x on 2027E EPS of $8.93. The EV/EBITDA multiple stands at roughly 27.0x on 2025 actuals, reflecting a premium valuation consistent with Apple's brand strength, ecosystem stickiness, and expanding Services segment. Notably, forward EBITDA margins are projected to expand meaningfully from 34.7% in 2025A to 44.8% by 2027E, driven by SG&A leverage and mix shift toward higher-margin revenue streams, which partially justifies the elevated multiple.

Peer Comparison

Relative to peers, Apple trades at a premium EV/EBITDA versus Alphabet (GOOGL) at 21.2x in 2025, but broadly in line with Microsoft (MSFT) at 23.6x. However, Alphabet's EBITDA is growing at a materially faster pace, reaching an estimated $210.7 billion by 2026 compared to Apple's $150.4 billion, raising questions about whether Apple's premium is fully warranted on a growth-adjusted basis. Apple's revenue CAGR of 3.3% is modest relative to both peers, suggesting the valuation leans heavily on margin expansion and capital return programs rather than top-line acceleration.

Fair Value Assessment

Applying a blended EV/EBITDA of 26x to 2026E EBITDA of approximately $200.6 billion implies an enterprise value near $5.2 trillion. Adjusting for net cash, this suggests a fair value range of roughly $195 to $215 per share. At current market prices, AAPL appears fairly valued to modestly stretched, with upside contingent on accelerating Services growth and AI-driven product cycles materializing ahead of consensus expectations.
