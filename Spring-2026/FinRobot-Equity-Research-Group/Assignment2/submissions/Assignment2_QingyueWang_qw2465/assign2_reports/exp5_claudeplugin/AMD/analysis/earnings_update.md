### Analyst commentary

AMD's fiscal 2025 results presented a broadly mixed picture relative to the prior internal model estimate row, with revenue and EPS tracking in line while EBITDA and EBITDA margin came in below that reference point. The variance on the profitability metrics suggests that cost or mix dynamics during the period were somewhat less favorable than the estimate row had anticipated, a pattern worth monitoring as the company continues to scale its data center and client segments. Looking ahead, the in-line revenue and EPS outcomes provide a reasonable foundation for the longer-term thesis, and the margin variance does not, in isolation, alter the structural view on AMD's competitive positioning. Segment-level cost trends and management's forward commentary on operating leverage will be the natural focal points for refining the estimate framework in subsequent periods, and the overall read remains constructive pending further disclosure.
