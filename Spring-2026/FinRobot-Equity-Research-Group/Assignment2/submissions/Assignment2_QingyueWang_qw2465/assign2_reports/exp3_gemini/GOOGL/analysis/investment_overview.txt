Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) continues to demonstrate exceptional financial strength, characterized by accelerating revenue growth and significant margin expansion through fiscal year 2025. The company reported 2025 revenue of 402.9 billion dollars, representing a 15.1 percent year-over-year increase. This performance underscores a healthy 11.8 percent compound annual growth rate since 2021, driven by the core search business and scaling cloud operations.

A key highlight of recent performance is the dramatic improvement in profitability. Alphabet’s EBITDA margin expanded from 30.1 percent in 2022 to 44.7 percent in 2025, with projections suggesting it could reach 51.7 percent by 2027. This expansion is largely supported by operational discipline; SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue have consistently declined and are expected to hit 11 percent by 2027. Consequently, earnings per share rose to 10.91 dollars in 2025, up from 8.13 dollars the previous year.

In a peer context, Alphabet’s absolute EBITDA of 179.9 billion dollars in 2025 comfortably exceeds both Microsoft and Meta. While its EV/EBITDA multiple of 21.2x sits at a premium compared to Meta, it remains lower than Microsoft’s 23.6x, offering a compelling valuation for a market leader.

Looking ahead, the outlook remains positive despite a projected moderation in top-line growth to the 4 to 6 percent range. Sustained margin improvements are expected to drive EPS to 13.01 dollars by 2027. With the forward P/E ratio projected to compress from 28.7x to 24.6x over the next two years, Alphabet remains a core holding for investors seeking a blend of growth, massive cash flow generation, and improving operational efficiency.
