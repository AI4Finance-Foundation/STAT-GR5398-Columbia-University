Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) exhibits a robust financial performance with significant revenue growth projected at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 11.8% from 2021 to 2027. The company’s EBITDA margin showcases a steady increase, climbing from 40.2% in 2021 to an estimated 51.7% in 2027, indicating improved operational efficiency and profitability.

When comparing Alphabet to its peers, Meta (META) and Microsoft (MSFT), Alphabet's EBITDA performance is superior in absolute terms, especially by 2025, where it is projected to reach $179.96 billion, compared to Meta's $104.55 billion and Microsoft's $160.17 billion. Despite the strong EBITDA figures, Alphabet's EV/EBITDA ratios fluctuate, reflecting market sentiment and valuation adjustments over time. For instance, the EV/EBITDA ratio dips to 13.63 in 2022 but rises to 21.23 by 2025, suggesting an increasing valuation multiple over the years.

In terms of price-to-earnings (PE) ratio, Alphabet's PE stands at 23.91 in 2023, which is higher than typical market averages but justified by its growth prospects and profitability. This metric is projected to decline slightly by 2027, indicating potential for share price growth relative to earnings.

Overall, Alphabet appears to be fairly valued given its strong growth trajectory, superior EBITDA performance relative to peers, and improving margins. The company's strategic investments and operational efficiencies are likely to enhance shareholder value, making it an attractive proposition for long-term investors seeking growth in the technology sector. However, investors should remain mindful of market conditions and competitive pressures that could impact future valuations.
