Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL), with its impressive revenue growth projected at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 11.8% from 2021 to 2027, is a formidable force in the technology sector. Its robust financial performance is evidenced by its increasing contribution and EBITDA margins, reaching 62.7% and 51.7% respectively by 2027.

Comparatively, Microsoft (MSFT) and Meta Platforms (META) are Alphabet’s key competitors. In terms of EBITDA, Microsoft consistently leads, surpassing Alphabet in 2022 and maintaining its advantage through 2026. This suggests Microsoft’s operational efficiency and effective cost management strategies. Meta, while trailing both in EBITDA, shows significant growth potential, closing the gap with Alphabet by 2026.

The EV/EBITDA ratio provides insight into valuation. Alphabet’s EV/EBITDA fluctuates between 13.6 in 2022 and 21.2 in 2025, indicating varying market sentiments. Microsoft's higher ratios, peaking at 26.1 in 2024, reflect strong investor confidence, likely due to its diversified product portfolio and enterprise solutions. Meta’s lower ratios may suggest a more cautious market outlook, potentially due to its heavy reliance on advertising revenue and recent strategic pivots.

Alphabet's strengths lie in its strong revenue growth and improving margins, driven by its diverse offerings beyond search advertisements, including cloud computing and hardware. However, the competition is fierce. Microsoft’s enterprise dominance and Meta’s social media ecosystem present formidable challenges. Alphabet must continue innovating and diversifying to maintain its competitive edge and meet evolving market demands.
