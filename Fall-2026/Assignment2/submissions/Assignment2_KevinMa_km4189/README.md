### Main findings

The portfolio timing overlay reduced volatility and maximum drawdown in DEV, VAL and TEST. Sharpe improved in DEV and VAL but declined in TEST. In the held-out period, selection alone (A) returned 167.33%, versus 132.70% with timing (C); annualized volatility fell from 31.14% to 28.05%, maximum drawdown from -30.53% to -25.29%, Sharpe from 1.301 to 1.233, and Calmar rose from 1.403 to 1.417. The final strategy trades some upside participation for downside protection; it does not dominate all baselines. A=B and C=D because the unchanged default valuation baseline removes no stocks in these runs.
