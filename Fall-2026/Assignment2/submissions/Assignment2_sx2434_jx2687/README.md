## Report and implementation

[Research report (PDF)](research_report.pdf) · [Reproduction instructions](REPRODUCE.md) · [Result files and provenance](output/SOURCES.md)

The submitted module acts after the course growth-stock selector. A candidate is removed when its prior close exceeds the upper estimates of at least two of DCF, same-sector trailing P/E and the stock’s own historical EV/EBITDA. The selector ranks the survivors. Portfolio exposure stays at the course full-timing setting.
