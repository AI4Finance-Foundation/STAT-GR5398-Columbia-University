Research question: does lowering the weight on DCF when its price is particularly sensitive to the frozen growth and discount-rate scenarios improve weekly growth_tech selection?
Period whitelist: development and validation only. Historical outcomes from other specifications on both splits were previously observed. No parameter search or rule selection between splits. Fees, scores, cash buffer, fills, membership and weekly rebalancing unchanged. Primary metric: net CAGR difference to selection A and to the equal-weight valuation control.
Use the frozen weekly estimates known by the prior close. For the weighted arms require three finite, strictly positive central estimates (DCF, peer P/E, historical EV/EBITDA), a positive current close and valid finite DCF interval endpoints. Missing data => keep under both rules. Stress proxy = (DCF high - DCF low) / central DCF; the frozen envelope combines initial growth +/-5pp, WACC +/-1pp and terminal growth scenarios. This is joint sensitivity, not an error variance or two separately measured derivatives.
On each decision date compute the 75th percentile of the proxy among eligible growth candidates with complete estimates; strictly above => sensitive. All ties at cutoff stay equal-weight. Equal control: 1/3 each. Adaptive: ordinary 1/3 each; sensitive DCF 1/6, peer and history 5/12 each. Drop only if blended fair value < 0.85 * current close (strict). Remaining names follow official ranking and weighting. Reproduce A and frozen team NAV for both splits. Count altered veto decisions and weeks with different held names.
