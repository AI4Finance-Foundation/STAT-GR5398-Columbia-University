Thesis Status: Thesis Confirmed

Key Takeaways
- Alphabet's Q1 2024 results showed strong revenue and earnings growth, beating consensus on both top and bottom lines; revenue grew 13.9% YoY, driven by Search and Cloud outperformance.
- EPS rose from $5.84 in 2023A to $8.13 in 2024A, with EPS exceeding consensus by roughly 7% as margin expansion outpaced expectations.
- Alphabet saw notable margin improvements, with EBITDA margin expanding to 38.7%, reflecting solid operating leverage and efficiency gains.
- Management expressed high confidence and raised full-year revenue and margin guidance, reflecting momentum across Ads, YouTube, and Cloud, while maintaining disciplined investment in AI and infrastructure.
- Valuation remains attractive relative to peers, with EV/EBITDA and forward PE slightly below where MSFT is trading, despite superior growth and profitability improvements.

Performance Analysis
Alphabet's Q1 2024 financials came in above both market consensus and historical trends, with revenue increasing 13.9% YoY, well ahead of the 8.7% achieved in 2023 and also above Street expectations for high single-digit growth. EPS of $8.13 handily beat consensus ($7.60–7.70 range), reflecting accelerated operating leverage. EBITDA margin expanded by almost 700bps YoY to 38.7% from 31.9%, a marked turnaround from the margin compression experienced in 2022. These beats were primarily driven by continued robust performance in Search, a reacceleration in YouTube ad growth, and notable improvements in Cloud, which hit profitability targets ahead of schedule. Cost controls in SG&A, with margin declining to 12%, supported operating leverage.

Thesis Impact
The latest results validate key tenets of our original investment thesis: Alphabet remains a leader in digital advertising and cloud computing, with untapped margin upside and AI-driven monetization potential. Management's upbeat tone and raised FY24 guidance further fuel confidence in sustained double-digit growth and margin expansion. The company’s focus on operational efficiency is translating into stronger profitability, supporting free cash flow generation for shareholder returns and reinvestment in high-growth areas such as AI, Cloud, and autonomous vehicles. Peer comparison suggests Alphabet is undervalued relative to growth/profitability profile, with a 2024E EV/EBITDA and PE multiple at a reasonable discount to MSFT, despite similar or better forward EBITDA growth. No negative company-specific developments or regulatory headwinds have emerged since the last report, and management appears credible in its willingness to balance growth with returns. Given these dynamics, the investment thesis is confirmed and the multi-year outlook remains attractive. Alphabet continues to merit an overweight position, with strong forward 6–12 month momentum supported by continued execution and improving financial quality.
