**Revenue Growth**  
NVIDIA achieved extraordinary revenue growth from $26.9 billion in 2022A to $215.9 billion in 2026A, representing a historical CAGR of 68.3% over this period. However, revenue growth is forecast to moderate significantly, with estimated increases of 5.0% in 2027E, 6.0% in 2028E, and 4.0% in 2029E.

**Gross Profit Margin**  
The company’s contribution margin (a proxy for gross profit margin) improved from 56.9% in 2023A to 75.0% in 2025A, before settling at 71.1% in 2026A. Forecasts indicate continued strength, with contribution margins expected to remain above 72% through 2029E.

**SG&A Expense Margin**  
NVIDIA’s SG&A margin declined steadily from 9.0% in 2023A to 2.1% in 2026A, reflecting strong operating leverage. This trend is projected to continue, with SG&A margin expected to fall further to 1.6% in 2027E, 1.1% in 2028E, and 0.6% in 2029E.

**EBITDA Margin**  
EBITDA margin expanded sharply from 22.2% in 2023A to 66.9% in 2026A, highlighting significant profitability gains. Looking ahead, EBITDA margin is forecast to rise further, reaching 70.5% in 2027E, 72.0% in 2028E, and 73.5% in 2029E.
