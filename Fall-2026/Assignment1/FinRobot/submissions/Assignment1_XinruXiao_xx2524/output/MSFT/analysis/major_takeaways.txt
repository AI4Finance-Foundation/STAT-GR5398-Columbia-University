**Revenue Growth**  
Microsoft achieved strong historical revenue growth with a CAGR of 13.7% from 2022A to 2026A, culminating in $331.8 billion in 2026A. Forecasts indicate a moderation, with revenue growth rates of 5.0% in 2027E, 6.0% in 2028E, and 4.0% in 2029E.

**Gross Profit Margin**  
Contribution margin (a proxy for gross profit margin) remained robust, ranging from 68.4% in 2022A to 67.9% in 2026A, and is projected to improve further to 70.9% by 2029E.

**SG&A Expense Margin**  
SG&A margin has steadily declined from 14.0% in 2022A to 10.4% in 2026A, with forecasts showing continued improvement to 8.9% by 2029E, reflecting increasing operational efficiency.

**EBITDA Margin**  
EBITDA margin increased from 50.6% in 2022A to 62.5% in 2026A, and is expected to remain strong, reaching 62.0% in 2029E, indicating sustained profitability improvements.
