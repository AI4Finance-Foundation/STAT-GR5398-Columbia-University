Microsoft Corporation stands out as a global technology leader with a diversified business model spanning productivity software, cloud infrastructure, and personal computing. Its three core segments—Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing—enable it to serve both enterprise and consumer markets with a broad product portfolio, including Office, Azure, Windows, Surface devices, Xbox, and LinkedIn.

Comparing Microsoft to key peers Apple (AAPL) and Cisco (CSCO), Microsoft demonstrates robust financial performance and growth. From 2022A to 2026A, Microsoft achieved a historical revenue CAGR of 13.7%, reflecting strong momentum across its business lines. In 2026A, Microsoft reported EBITDA of $207.5 billion, significantly outpacing Cisco’s $20.0 billion and exceeding Apple’s last available EBITDA of $144.4 billion in 2025. This positions Microsoft as a scale leader among its peers.

Microsoft’s EBITDA margin has also shown notable improvement, rising from 50.6% in 2022A to 62.5% in 2026A. This margin is higher than Cisco’s, and Microsoft’s 2024A EBITDA of $133.0 billion is close to Apple’s $134.9 billion, despite Apple’s greater focus on hardware and consumer electronics. Furthermore, Microsoft’s EV/EBITDA multiple in 2024A was 26.1, comparable to Apple’s 26.6 and higher than Cisco’s 13.8, indicating strong market confidence in Microsoft’s future earnings potential.

Looking ahead, Microsoft’s forecasts for 2027E-2029E show continued revenue and EBITDA growth, with EBITDA projected to reach $238.1 billion by 2029E. This sustained growth, combined with leading margins and a diversified revenue base, positions Microsoft as a top performer in the technology sector, maintaining a competitive edge over both Apple and Cisco in scale, profitability, and market valuation.
