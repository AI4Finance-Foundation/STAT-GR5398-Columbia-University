Microsoft Corporation delivered robust financial performance through the latest reported period, 2026A. Revenue grew from $198.3 billion in 2022A to $331.8 billion in 2026A, representing a historical CAGR of 13.7% over this period. The company’s EBITDA also expanded significantly, reaching $207.5 billion in 2026A, up from $100.2 billion in 2022A. EBITDA margin improved from 50.6% in 2022A to 62.5% in 2026A, reflecting strong operational leverage and disciplined cost management. Contribution margin remained consistently high, ranging from 68.4% to 69.8% between 2022A and 2024A, and was 67.9% in 2026A.

Key growth drivers included continued momentum across Microsoft’s three core segments: Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing. The Intelligent Cloud division, anchored by Azure, benefited from rising demand for cloud infrastructure and AI-driven solutions. Productivity and Business Processes, with offerings like Office 365 and Dynamics 365, maintained relevance for enterprise and consumer customers. The More Personal Computing segment contributed through Windows licensing, Surface hardware, and gaming, including Xbox and related services.

Looking ahead, Microsoft’s Financial Metrics forecast steady revenue growth, with revenue expected to reach $384.1 billion by 2029E. EBITDA is projected to rise to $238.1 billion in 2029E, with margins remaining strong. EPS is forecast to increase from $19.09 in 2027E to $21.47 in 2029E. Revenue growth rates are expected to moderate, with annual increases of 5.0% in 2027E, 6.0% in 2028E, and 4.0% in 2029E.

Overall, Microsoft’s historical performance demonstrates strong execution and margin expansion, while forecasts indicate continued, albeit moderating, growth supported by its diversified technology portfolio and leadership in cloud and productivity solutions.
