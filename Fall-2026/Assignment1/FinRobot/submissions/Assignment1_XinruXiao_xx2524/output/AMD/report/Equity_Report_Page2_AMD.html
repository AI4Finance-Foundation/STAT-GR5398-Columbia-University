Competitor Analysis

Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD) operates as a major player in the semiconductor industry, competing directly with Intel (INTC) and NVIDIA (NVDA) across computing, graphics, and data center markets. AMD’s business is diversified across two main segments: Computing and Graphics, and Enterprise, Embedded and Semi-Custom, with a broad product portfolio spanning CPUs, GPUs, and custom SoCs for PCs, servers, and gaming consoles.

From 2021A to 2025A, AMD achieved a historical revenue CAGR of 20.5%, reflecting strong growth momentum. In 2025A, AMD reported revenue of $34.6 billion and EBITDA of $7.28 billion. While this EBITDA is significantly lower than NVIDIA’s $86.1 billion and Intel’s $14.4 billion for the same year, AMD’s revenue growth and margin expansion are notable. AMD’s contribution margin improved from 48.2% in 2021A to 49.5% in 2025A, and its EBITDA margin increased from 18.3% in 2023A to 21.0% in 2025A, with further margin expansion forecasted through 2028E.

In terms of valuation, AMD’s EV/EBITDA ratio has fluctuated, reaching 47.8x in 2025, higher than Intel’s 14.5x and NVIDIA’s 33.8x. This suggests a premium valuation relative to Intel, though still below NVIDIA’s peak multiples in earlier years. AMD’s historical EBITDA has trailed both peers, but its margin trajectory and product diversification position it well for continued competitive pressure, especially as NVIDIA’s dominance in data center accelerators and Intel’s scale in CPUs persist.

Overall, AMD stands out for its rapid historical growth and improving profitability, though it remains smaller in absolute scale compared to NVIDIA and Intel. Its ongoing margin improvements and broadening product reach underscore its role as a formidable challenger in the semiconductor landscape.

Major takeaways from AMD and peers:

**Revenue Growth** AMD achieved strong historical revenue growth with a CAGR of 20.5% from 2021A to 2025A, highlighted by a 34.3% increase in 2025A. Forecasts indicate more moderate revenue growth rates of 5.0% in 2026E, 6.0% in 2027E, and 4.0% in 2028E. **Gross Profit Margin** The company’s contribution margin (a proxy for gross profit margin) improved from 48.2% in 2021A to 49.5% in 2025A, and is forecast to rise further to 50.5% in 2026E, 51.5% in 2027E, and 52.5% in 2028E, reflecting ongoing margin expansion. **SG&A Expense Margin** AMD’s SG&A margin increased from 8.8% in 2021A to 12.0% in 2025A, but is expected to decline gradually to 11.5% in 2026E, 11.0% in 2027E, and 10.5% in 2028E, indicating improved operating leverage in the forecast period. **EBITDA Margin** EBITDA margin decreased from 25.3% in 2021A to 18.3% in 2023A, then recovered to 21.0% in 2025A. Substantial improvement is forecast, with margins expected to reach 39.0% in 2026E, 40.5% in 2027E, and 42.0% in 2028E.
