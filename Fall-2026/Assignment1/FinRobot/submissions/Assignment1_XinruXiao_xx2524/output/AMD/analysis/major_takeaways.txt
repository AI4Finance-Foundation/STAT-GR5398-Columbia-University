**Revenue Growth**  
AMD achieved strong historical revenue growth with a CAGR of 20.5% from 2021A to 2025A, highlighted by a 34.3% increase in 2025A. Forecasts indicate more moderate revenue growth rates of 5.0% in 2026E, 6.0% in 2027E, and 4.0% in 2028E.

**Gross Profit Margin**  
The company’s contribution margin (a proxy for gross profit margin) improved from 48.2% in 2021A to 49.5% in 2025A, and is forecast to rise further to 50.5% in 2026E, 51.5% in 2027E, and 52.5% in 2028E, reflecting ongoing margin expansion.

**SG&A Expense Margin**  
AMD’s SG&A margin increased from 8.8% in 2021A to 12.0% in 2025A, but is expected to decline gradually to 11.5% in 2026E, 11.0% in 2027E, and 10.5% in 2028E, indicating improved operating leverage in the forecast period.

**EBITDA Margin**  
EBITDA margin decreased from 25.3% in 2021A to 18.3% in 2023A, then recovered to 21.0% in 2025A. Substantial improvement is forecast, with margins expected to reach 39.0% in 2026E, 40.5% in 2027E, and 42.0% in 2028E.
