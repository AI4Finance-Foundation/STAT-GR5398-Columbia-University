- **Volatile Revenue Growth:** AMD’s historical revenue growth has fluctuated significantly, with a decline of -3.9% in 2023A followed by a sharp rebound of 34.3% in 2025A, indicating exposure to cyclical or unpredictable demand in its markets.

- **Margin Pressure:** EBITDA margin decreased from 25.3% in 2021A to 18.3% in 2023A before partially recovering to 21.0% in 2025A, reflecting vulnerability to cost increases or pricing pressures.

- **Elevated Operating Expenses:** SG&A expenses have risen steadily, reaching $4,144 million in 2025A with a corresponding SG&A margin increase to 12.0%, which could impact profitability if not controlled.

- **Competitive Industry Dynamics:** Peer comparison shows AMD’s EV/EBITDA multiples are consistently higher than Intel’s and, at times, Nvidia’s, suggesting heightened valuation risk and potential sensitivity to competitive shifts.

- **Rising Debt Levels:** Total debt increased from $661 million in 2021A to $4,472 million in 2025A, which may elevate financial risk if cash flows do not keep pace with debt servicing requirements.
