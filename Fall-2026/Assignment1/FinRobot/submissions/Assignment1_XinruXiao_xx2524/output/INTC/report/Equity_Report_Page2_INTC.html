Competitor Analysis

Intel Corporation operates in the highly competitive semiconductor industry, with key peers including Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) and NVIDIA (NVDA). Over the historical period from 2021A to 2025A, Intel experienced significant revenue contraction, with a historical revenue CAGR of -9.6%. Revenue declined from $79.0 billion in 2021A to $52.9 billion in 2025A, reflecting ongoing challenges in market share and product competitiveness.

In terms of profitability, Intel’s EBITDA dropped sharply from $33.9 billion in 2021A to $1.2 billion in 2024A, before recovering to $14.4 billion in 2025A. This volatility contrasts with AMD, whose EBITDA ranged from $4.2 billion to $7.3 billion over the same period, and NVIDIA, which saw a dramatic increase from $5.99 billion in 2023A to $86.1 billion in 2025A. NVIDIA’s rapid EBITDA growth highlights its strong position in high-growth segments such as AI and data centers.

Intel’s EBITDA margin also suffered, falling from 42.9% in 2021A to just 2.3% in 2024A, before rebounding to 27.2% in 2025A. In comparison, NVIDIA’s superior EBITDA performance is reflected in its higher EV/EBITDA multiples, peaking at 81.0 in 2023A and remaining elevated relative to Intel and AMD. Intel’s EV/EBITDA ratio spiked to 106.9 in 2024A, indicating market concerns about its earnings power, but normalized to 14.5 in 2025A.

Looking ahead, Intel’s forecasts show modest revenue growth and gradual margin recovery, but its EPS remains negative through 2028E. In contrast, NVIDIA and AMD are positioned for stronger profitability and growth. Overall, Intel faces significant competitive pressure, with recent financial performance lagging behind its key peers, particularly in profitability and growth momentum.

Major takeaways from INTC and peers:

**Revenue Growth** Intel experienced a significant decline in revenue from 2021A to 2025A, with a historical CAGR of -9.6%. However, forecasts indicate a return to growth, with revenue expected to increase by 5.0% in 2026E, 6.0% in 2027E, and 4.0% in 2028E. **Gross Profit Margin** Contribution margin (a proxy for gross profit margin) declined from 55.4% in 2021A to 34.8% in 2025A, but is forecast to gradually recover to 35.8% in 2026E, 36.8% in 2027E, and 37.8% in 2028E. **SG&A Expense Margin** SG&A margin peaked at 11.1% in 2022A and declined to 8.7% in 2025A. It is forecast to decrease further, reaching 8.2% in 2026E, 7.7% in 2027E, and 7.2% in 2028E, indicating improved operating efficiency. **EBITDA Margin** EBITDA margin dropped sharply from 42.9% in 2021A to 2.3% in 2024A, before rebounding to 27.2% in 2025A. Further improvement is forecast, with margins rising to 27.6% in 2026E, 29.1% in 2027E, and 30.6% in 2028E.
