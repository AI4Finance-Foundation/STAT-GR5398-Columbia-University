Intel Corporation is a leading global technology company specializing in the design, development, manufacturing, marketing, and servicing of computing and related products. Headquartered in Santa Clara, California, and incorporated in 1968, Intel operates internationally, with significant activities in the United States, Ireland, and Israel. The company is organized into three main segments: Client Computing Group (CCG), Data Center and AI (DCAI), and Intel Foundry.

Intel’s CCG segment provides products such as client and commercial CPUs, discrete client GPUs, edge computing, and connectivity solutions. The DCAI segment focuses on data center and AI products, including server CPUs, discrete GPUs, and networking products. The Intel Foundry segment encompasses semiconductor manufacturing, including wafer fabrication, substrates, and related services. Additionally, Intel develops driving assistance and self-driving solutions and manufactures multi-beam mask writing tools. Its customer base includes original equipment manufacturers, original design manufacturers, cloud service providers, and other manufacturers and service providers. Intel distributes its products through direct sales, distributors, resellers, retailers, and OEM partners. The company has also entered into strategic collaborations, such as its partnership with Infosys Limited to develop a multi-layer AI fabric.

In terms of market position, Intel is a major player in the semiconductor industry, competing with companies like AMD and NVIDIA. The company’s historical financial performance from 2021A to 2025A shows a period of revenue contraction, with revenue declining from $79.02 billion in 2021A to $52.85 billion in 2025A, reflecting a historical CAGR of -9.6%. Contribution margin and EBITDA margin also decreased over this period, with contribution margin falling from 55.4% in 2021A to 34.8% in 2025A, and EBITDA margin dropping from 42.9% to 27.2%. Intel’s EBITDA declined from $33.87 billion in 2021A to $1.20 billion in 2024A, before recovering to $14.35 billion in 2025A. Earnings per share (EPS) also turned negative in 2024A and 2025A.

Looking ahead, Intel’s forecasts indicate a return to revenue growth, with revenues expected to reach $61.18 billion by 2028E. Margins are projected to gradually improve, with contribution margin rising to 37.8% and EBITDA margin to 30.6% in 2028E. However, EPS is forecast to remain slightly negative through 2028E. Intel’s financial trajectory reflects both the challenges of recent years and its ongoing efforts to regain momentum through innovation and strategic partnerships in a highly competitive industry.
