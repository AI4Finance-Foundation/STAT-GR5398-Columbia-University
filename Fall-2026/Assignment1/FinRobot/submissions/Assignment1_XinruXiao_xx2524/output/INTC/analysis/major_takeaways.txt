**Revenue Growth**  
Intel experienced a significant decline in revenue from 2021A to 2025A, with a historical CAGR of -9.6%. However, forecasts indicate a return to growth, with revenue expected to increase by 5.0% in 2026E, 6.0% in 2027E, and 4.0% in 2028E.

**Gross Profit Margin**  
Contribution margin (a proxy for gross profit margin) declined from 55.4% in 2021A to 34.8% in 2025A, but is forecast to gradually recover to 35.8% in 2026E, 36.8% in 2027E, and 37.8% in 2028E.

**SG&A Expense Margin**  
SG&A margin peaked at 11.1% in 2022A and declined to 8.7% in 2025A. It is forecast to decrease further, reaching 8.2% in 2026E, 7.7% in 2027E, and 7.2% in 2028E, indicating improved operating efficiency.

**EBITDA Margin**  
EBITDA margin dropped sharply from 42.9% in 2021A to 2.3% in 2024A, before rebounding to 27.2% in 2025A. Further improvement is forecast, with margins rising to 27.6% in 2026E, 29.1% in 2027E, and 30.6% in 2028E.
