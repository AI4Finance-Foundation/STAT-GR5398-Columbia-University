Intel Corporation experienced a significant revenue decline from 2021A to 2025A, with a historical CAGR of -9.6%, and reported negative EPS in both 2024A and 2025A. EBITDA margin dropped sharply to 2.3% in 2024A before partially recovering to 27.2% in 2025A. Forecasts indicate modest revenue growth and gradual margin improvement through 2028E, but EPS is expected to remain negative.
