Apple Inc. delivered solid financial performance in recent years, with revenue rising from $365.8 billion in 2021A to $416.2 billion in 2025A. This represents a historical revenue CAGR of 3.3% from 2021A to 2025A. After a slight revenue decline in 2023A, Apple rebounded with growth of 2.0% in 2024A and 6.4% in 2025A. Contribution margin improved steadily, reaching 46.9% in 2025A, up from 41.8% in 2021A, reflecting ongoing operational efficiencies and a favorable shift toward higher-margin services and products.

EBITDA also grew consistently, from $123.1 billion in 2021A to $144.4 billion in 2025A, with EBITDA margin expanding from 33.7% to 34.7% over the same period. Earnings per share (EPS) increased from $5.67 in 2021A to $7.49 in 2025A, demonstrating Apple’s ability to translate top-line growth and margin expansion into shareholder value. The company’s net debt position improved, declining from $101.6 billion in 2021A to $76.4 billion in 2025A, supported by robust cash generation.

Looking ahead, Apple’s own forecasts anticipate continued momentum. Revenue is expected to reach $481.7 billion by 2028E, with contribution margin rising to 49.9%. EBITDA is projected to grow to $215.8 billion in 2028E, and EBITDA margin is forecast to expand to 44.8%. EPS is estimated to increase to $8.93 in 2028E. These forecasts reflect Apple’s ongoing focus on expanding its ecosystem, growing its services and subscription businesses, and maintaining innovation across hardware and software.

Overall, Apple’s recent results highlight resilient demand, operational discipline, and a strong outlook driven by both product and services growth. The company’s improving margins and earnings, alongside a strengthening balance sheet, position it well for continued value creation.
