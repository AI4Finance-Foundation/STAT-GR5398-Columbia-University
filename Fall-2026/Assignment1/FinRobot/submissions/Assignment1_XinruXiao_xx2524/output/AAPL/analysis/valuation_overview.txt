Apple Inc. (AAPL) currently demonstrates robust financial performance, with 2025A revenue reaching $416.2 billion and EBITDA of $144.4 billion. The company’s EBITDA margin has steadily improved, rising from 33.7% in 2021A to 34.7% in 2025A, reflecting operational efficiency and strong cost management. Over the historical period from 2021A to 2025A, Apple achieved a revenue CAGR of 3.3%. EPS has also grown, reaching 7.49 in 2025A.

In terms of valuation, Apple’s EV/EBITDA multiple has expanded in recent years. For 2025A, Apple trades at an EV/EBITDA of 26.97, which is higher than both Cisco (CSCO) at 19.11 and Microsoft (MSFT) at 23.60 for the same year. This premium suggests that investors are willing to pay more for Apple’s earnings relative to these peers, likely due to Apple’s strong brand, diversified revenue streams, and consistent profitability.

Looking ahead, Apple’s financial forecasts indicate continued growth. Revenue is expected to rise to $436.97 billion in 2026E, $463.19 billion in 2027E, and $481.71 billion in 2028E. EBITDA is forecast to increase significantly, reaching $182.65 billion in 2026E and $215.81 billion in 2028E, with margins expanding to 44.8% by 2028E. EPS is also projected to grow, reaching 8.93 in 2028E.

In summary, Apple’s current valuation reflects its superior profitability and growth prospects compared to peers. The company’s premium EV/EBITDA multiple is supported by its strong historical performance and positive outlook. Based on the supplied data, Apple appears fairly valued relative to its sector peers, with its premium justified by its scale, operational excellence, and ongoing earnings growth.
