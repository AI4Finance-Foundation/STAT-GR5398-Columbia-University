Apple Inc. stands out in the consumer electronics and technology sector with a broad product and service portfolio, including flagship devices like the iPhone, Mac, and iPad, as well as a robust ecosystem of wearables, digital content, and subscription-based services. Its multi-channel distribution strategy and integration of hardware, software, and services have enabled Apple to maintain strong financial performance and brand loyalty.

Comparing Apple to key technology peers Cisco Systems (CSCO) and Microsoft (MSFT), Apple consistently delivers higher EBITDA in the historical period. In 2022A, Apple reported EBITDA of $133.1 billion, significantly outpacing Cisco’s $16.8 billion and Microsoft’s $100.2 billion. This trend continues through 2025A, with Apple reaching $144.4 billion in EBITDA, compared to Cisco’s $15.3 billion and Microsoft’s $160.2 billion. While Microsoft surpasses Apple in EBITDA by 2025A, Apple maintains a substantial lead over Cisco.

Apple’s historical revenue CAGR from 2021A to 2025A is 3.3%, reflecting steady growth in a mature market. The company’s EBITDA margin has remained robust, increasing from 33.7% in 2021A to 34.7% in 2025A, and is forecasted to rise further in subsequent years. Apple’s EV/EBITDA multiple has also trended higher than Cisco’s, reaching 26.9699 in 2025A versus Cisco’s 19.1074, and is comparable to Microsoft’s 23.5956 in the same year, indicating strong investor confidence and premium valuation.

In summary, Apple’s scale, profitability, and integrated ecosystem position it as a leader among technology peers. While Microsoft is closing the EBITDA gap, Apple’s diversified revenue streams and premium market positioning continue to differentiate it from both Cisco and Microsoft.
