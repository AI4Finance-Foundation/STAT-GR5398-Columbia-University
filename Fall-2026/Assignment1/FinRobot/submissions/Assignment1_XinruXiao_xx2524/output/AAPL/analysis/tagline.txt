Apple Inc. demonstrated steady financial growth with revenue rising from $365.8 billion in 2021A to $416.2 billion in 2025A, reflecting a historical CAGR of 3.3% over this period. Profitability remained robust, as evidenced by an increasing EBITDA margin from 33.7% in 2021A to 34.7% in 2025A and a contribution margin reaching 46.9% in 2025A. The company also strengthened its balance sheet, reducing net debt from $101.6 billion in 2021A to $76.4 billion in 2025A.
