**Revenue Growth**  
Apple’s revenue grew from $365.8 billion in 2021A to $416.2 billion in 2025A, representing a historical CAGR of 3.3% from 2021A to 2025A. Forecasts indicate continued growth, with revenue expected to reach $481.7 billion by 2028E.

**Gross Profit Margin**  
Apple’s contribution margin (gross profit margin) improved from 41.8% in 2021A to 46.9% in 2025A, and is forecast to rise further to 49.9% by 2028E, reflecting ongoing gains in profitability.

**SG&A Expense Margin**  
SG&A margin increased from 6.0% in 2021A to 6.7% in 2024A, then slightly declined to 6.6% in 2025A. Looking ahead, SG&A margin is forecast to decrease steadily to 5.1% by 2028E, indicating improving operating efficiency.

**EBITDA Margin**  
EBITDA margin remained stable at around 33.7%–34.7% from 2021A to 2025A, but is projected to expand significantly to 44.8% by 2028E, highlighting expectations for enhanced operational leverage and profitability.
