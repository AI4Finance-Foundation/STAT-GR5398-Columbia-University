- **Product Concentration Risk:** Apple’s revenue is heavily dependent on a limited number of flagship products, such as the iPhone, Mac, and iPad, making the company vulnerable to shifts in consumer preferences or technological disruption affecting these categories.

- **Margin Pressure:** Although Apple’s EBITDA margin increased from 33.7% in 2021A to 34.7% in 2025A, any rise in cost of operations or SG&A expenses could negatively impact profitability, especially if revenue growth slows or competition intensifies.

- **Revenue Growth Volatility:** Apple experienced negative revenue growth in 2023A (-2.8%) and relatively modest growth in 2024A (2.0%), highlighting exposure to cyclical demand, macroeconomic factors, or market saturation.

- **High Net Debt Levels:** Despite a reduction from $101.6 billion in 2021A to $76.4 billion in 2025A, Apple maintains significant net debt, which could constrain financial flexibility in adverse market conditions or during periods of increased investment needs.

- **Regulatory and Platform Risks:** Apple’s reliance on the App Store and digital services exposes it to potential regulatory scrutiny and changes in digital platform policies, which could impact service revenues and profitability.
