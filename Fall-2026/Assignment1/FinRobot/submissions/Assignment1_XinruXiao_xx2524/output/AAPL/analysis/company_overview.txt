Apple Inc. is a leading global technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California, operating within the consumer electronics sector. Established in 1976, Apple has built a robust business model centered on the design, manufacture, and sale of a wide array of electronic devices and digital services. Its core hardware products include the iPhone smartphone, Mac computers, and iPad tablets, complemented by a diverse portfolio of wearables and accessories such as AirPods, Apple Watch, Apple TV, Beats products, and HomePod speakers.

Beyond hardware, Apple has developed a comprehensive ecosystem of services that enhance customer engagement and drive recurring revenue. The company offers support and cloud services, as well as a suite of digital platforms, most notably the App Store. The App Store serves as a central marketplace for applications and digital content, including music, e-books, games, videos, and podcasts. Apple also generates revenue from advertising through its own platforms and third-party licensing agreements.

Subscription-based services are a significant part of Apple’s strategy, with offerings such as Apple Music, Apple TV+, Apple Arcade, Apple Fitness+, and Apple News+ catering to a broad range of consumer interests. Financial services, including the Apple Card and Apple Pay, further diversify the company’s revenue streams. Apple also licenses its intellectual property and serves a wide customer base, ranging from individual consumers to businesses and institutions in education, corporate, and government sectors.

Apple’s products reach customers through a multi-channel distribution network, which includes its own retail stores and online platforms, direct sales teams, and partnerships with mobile carriers, wholesalers, retailers, and authorized resellers.

In terms of recent financial performance, Apple reported revenue of $416.2 billion in 2025A, up from $391.0 billion in 2024A and $383.3 billion in 2023A. The company’s historical revenue CAGR from 2021A to 2025A was 3.3%. Contribution margin improved from 41.8% in 2021A to 46.9% in 2025A, while EBITDA rose from $123.1 billion in 2021A to $144.4 billion in 2025A, with EBITDA margin increasing from 33.7% to 34.7% over the same period. Apple’s EPS reached 7.49 in 2025A. The company also maintained a strong balance sheet, with cash and cash equivalents of $35.9 billion and net debt of $76.4 billion in 2025A.

Apple’s market position is reinforced by its integrated ecosystem, strong brand loyalty, and consistent financial performance, making it a dominant force in the global technology and consumer electronics industry.
