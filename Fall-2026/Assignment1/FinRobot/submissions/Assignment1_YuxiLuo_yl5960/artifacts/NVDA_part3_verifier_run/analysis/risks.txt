**Key Investment Risks for NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) (as of 2026-10-10):**

- **Sustainability of Hyper-Growth:** NVIDIA’s reported revenue grew at a 68.3% CAGR from 2022A ($26.9B) to 2026A ($215.9B), but this pipeline’s forecast assumes a sharp deceleration to single-digit growth from 2027E (5.0%) to 2029E (4.0%). There is significant risk that the recent hyper-growth is not sustainable, and future growth rates could materially underperform even these conservative forecasts.

- **Margin Volatility and Compression:** While reported EBITDA margin reached 66.9% in 2026A (up from 22.2% in 2023A), historical margins were volatile, and forecast margins (2027E: 70.5%, 2029E: 73.5%) may be difficult to maintain amid rising competition, potential pricing pressures, or supply chain disruptions.

- **Valuation Risk:** NVIDIA’s reported PE ratio declined from 106.4x in 2023A to 37.8x in 2026A, and the pipeline’s forecast still implies high multiples (2027E: 35.9x, 2029E: 32.4x). Any disappointment in earnings or broader market de-rating could result in sharp valuation contractions.

- **Competitive Threats:** Peer EBITDA projections (e.g., AMD 2027E: $8.6B, INTC 2027E: $47.4B) suggest rivals are also growing, and historical peer EV/EBITDA multiples are volatile. Increased R&D or pricing competition from AMD, Intel, or new entrants could erode NVIDIA’s market share and profitability.

- **Operating Leverage and SG&A Risk:** SG&A as a percentage of revenue is forecast to decline sharply (2026A: 2.1% to 2029E: 0.6%), which may not be realistic as NVIDIA may need to increase spending to support growth, innovation, and regulatory compliance. Underestimating future cost requirements could pressure earnings and margins.

**Summary:** The main risks are the sustainability of recent explosive growth, maintaining very high margins, elevated valuation multiples, competitive dynamics, and uncertainty around operating expense assumptions. All figures and trends are based on reported results through 2026A and this pipeline’s forecasts for 2027E–2029E.
