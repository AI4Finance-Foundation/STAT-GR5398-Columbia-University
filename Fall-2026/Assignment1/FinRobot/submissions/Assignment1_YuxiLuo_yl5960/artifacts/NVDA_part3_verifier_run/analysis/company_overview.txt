NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) is a global leader in visual computing technologies and artificial intelligence (AI), best known for designing graphics processing units (GPUs) that are critical in gaming, professional visualization, data centers, and automotive markets. NVIDIA’s business model revolves around developing and selling high-performance GPUs, systems-on-a-chip (SoCs), and related software platforms, serving both consumer and enterprise markets. The company’s products underpin a wide range of applications, from gaming and digital content creation to AI training and inference in cloud and edge environments.

NVIDIA’s primary product lines include the GeForce GPU series for gaming, the Quadro and RTX series for professional visualization, and the data center-focused A100 and H100 GPUs, which have become core infrastructure for AI workloads. The company also offers automotive solutions for infotainment and autonomous driving (NVIDIA DRIVE) and an expanding portfolio of AI software and platform solutions, such as CUDA, to foster a developer ecosystem and drive adoption of its hardware.

Over the past several years, NVIDIA has rapidly scaled its business, capitalizing on the rise of AI and accelerated computing. From FY2022 to FY2026, NVIDIA reported exceptional financial growth, with revenue soaring from $26.9 billion (2022A) to $215.9 billion (2026A), representing a remarkable 68.3% compound annual growth rate. The company’s contribution margin remained robust, averaging above 70% in recent years, reflecting strong pricing power and operational efficiency. EBITDA also grew substantially, reaching $144.6 billion in 2026A, with EBITDA margins expanding to 66.9%. Earnings per share (EPS) increased from $0.39 (2022A) to $4.93 (2026A).

NVIDIA’s market position is dominant in both the gaming GPU sector and, increasingly, the enterprise and data center markets, where its solutions are foundational for AI and machine learning infrastructure. Compared to peers such as AMD and Intel, NVIDIA has higher EBITDA and profitability, as seen in peer comparison data. Its EV/EBITDA multiples have remained elevated, indicating strong investor confidence in future growth.

Looking forward, pipeline forecasts (not company guidance) suggest more moderate growth with revenues reaching $249.9 billion by 2029E, and continued margin expansion. NVIDIA’s strategy of combining cutting-edge hardware with a robust software ecosystem positions it at the forefront of the AI and accelerated computing revolution, underpinning its strong market leadership and continued financial outperformance.
