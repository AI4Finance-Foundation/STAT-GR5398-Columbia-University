NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) delivered outstanding financial results in FY2026, continuing a multi-year surge driven by robust demand for its AI and data center products. Reported revenue for FY2026 reached $215.9 billion, reflecting a remarkable 68.3% compound annual growth rate (CAGR) from FY2022 to FY2026. This was propelled by back-to-back years of triple-digit revenue growth: 125.9% in FY2024 and 114.2% in FY2025, followed by a strong 65.5% in FY2026.

Profitability improved significantly as well. EBITDA expanded from $11.4 billion in FY2022 to $144.6 billion in FY2026, with reported EBITDA margin increasing to 66.9% in FY2026. Contribution margin (gross margin) reached 71.1% in FY2026, up from 56.9% in FY2023, indicating effective cost control amid rapid growth. EPS rose from $0.39 in FY2022 to $4.93 in FY2026.

NVIDIA’s operating leverage is evident, with SG&A margin falling steadily to just 2.1% in FY2026. Relative to peers, NVIDIA’s historical EV/EBITDA multiple (31.4x in 2026) remains elevated, reflecting high market confidence.

Looking ahead, this pipeline’s forecast anticipates a normalization of growth: revenue is expected to increase by 5.0% in 2027E, 6.0% in 2028E, and 4.0% in 2029E. Margins are projected to remain strong, with EBITDA margin forecast to rise toward 73.5% by 2029E. EPS is expected to grow steadily to $5.88 by 2029E, with the PE ratio gradually compressing to 32.4x.

In summary, NVIDIA’s recent performance demonstrates exceptional scale and profitability, driven by AI and data center tailwinds. While growth rates are forecast to moderate, the outlook remains robust, underpinned by industry leadership and strong operating efficiency.
