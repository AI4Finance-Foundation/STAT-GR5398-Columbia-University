- **Slowing Revenue Growth:** Apple's revenue growth has decelerated, with negative growth in 2023 (-2.8%) and only modest projected growth through 2027 (CAGR of 3.3%), indicating maturing markets and potential challenges in driving top-line expansion.

- **Margin Compression Risk:** While contribution and EBITDA margins are rising, continued inflationary pressures or increased competition could impact Apple's ability to maintain or expand these margins, especially as SG&A margins are projected to decrease.

- **Premium Valuation Relative to Peers:** Apple's EV/EBITDA and PE ratios are higher than those of key peers (GOOGL, MSFT), suggesting elevated market expectations and limited room for multiple expansion, increasing downside risk if growth disappoints.

- **Earnings Stagnation:** EPS growth stalled in 2023 (6.16 vs. 6.15 in 2022), highlighting pressure on earnings momentum, which could impact investor sentiment if not reversed.

- **Competitive and Innovation Pressures:** Peer EBITDA projections (notably for GOOGL and MSFT) are rising faster than Apple’s, indicating intensifying competition in key technology sectors that may threaten Apple's market share and growth prospects.
