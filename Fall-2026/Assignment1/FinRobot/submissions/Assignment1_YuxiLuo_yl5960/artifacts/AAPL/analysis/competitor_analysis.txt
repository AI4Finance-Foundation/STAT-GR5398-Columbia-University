Apple Inc. (AAPL) maintains its leadership in the technology sector with robust financials, but faces intensifying competition from peers such as Alphabet (GOOGL) and Microsoft (MSFT). Over the 2021–2025 period, Apple’s revenue grows at a modest 3.3% CAGR, reflecting its maturity and the scale of its operations. While revenue growth is slower than some peers, Apple’s operational efficiency stands out: its contribution margin improves from 41.8% in 2021 to an estimated 47.9% in 2025, far exceeding most hardware-centric competitors and rivaling software-heavy peers.

Apple’s EBITDA performance remains strong, consistently outpacing Alphabet and often surpassing Microsoft through 2024, though both competitors are projected to close the gap or overtake Apple by 2025–2027. For example, Apple’s 2025 EBITDA is projected at $144.4B versus $180.7B for Alphabet and $160.2B for Microsoft, suggesting intensifying competition as peers scale their high-margin businesses. However, Apple’s EBITDA margins are on an upward trajectory, reaching a projected 41.8% in 2025, which is particularly impressive for a company with significant hardware exposure.

In terms of valuation, Apple trades at a premium, with a 2025E EV/EBITDA of 27.0x compared to Alphabet’s 21.2x and Microsoft’s 23.6x. This premium likely reflects Apple’s brand strength, customer loyalty, and consistent profitability. The company’s EPS and margins are also steady, but the relatively high valuation means Apple has less room for error compared to its peers.

In summary, Apple’s financials demonstrate operational excellence and margin strength, but slower revenue growth and premium valuations suggest that investors are paying for stability and brand, while Alphabet and Microsoft may offer more upside as their earnings catch up. Apple’s continued focus on efficiency and ecosystem differentiation will be crucial as the competitive landscape evolves.
