Apple Inc. (AAPL) continues to deliver robust financial performance, maintaining its position as a global technology leader. For fiscal 2023, Apple reported revenues of $383.3 billion, a 2.8% year-over-year decline, primarily reflecting softer hardware demand amid a challenging macroeconomic environment. However, revenue is expected to rebound, with consensus estimates pointing to a CAGR of 3.3% through 2027 and projected revenue of $481.7 billion in 2027.

Despite modest topline growth, Apple has demonstrated strong operational execution. Contribution margins expanded steadily from 41.8% in 2021 to 46.2% in 2024, and are forecasted to approach 50% by 2027, reflecting Apple's continued focus on premium products and services, as well as operational efficiency. The company’s EBITDA margin also improved, reaching 34.5% in 2024, with further expansion expected as high-margin services and subscription revenues grow.

EPS has remained resilient, with 2023 EPS of $6.16 and projections rising to $8.93 by 2027. Apple’s valuation, as measured by its PE ratio (37.3x for 2024E), remains above historical averages and peers, reflecting investor confidence in Apple’s brand, ecosystem, and ability to generate cash flow. Compared to peers, Apple’s 2024 EV/EBITDA of 26.6x is higher than both Alphabet (17.3x) and Microsoft (26.1x), suggesting a premium for its stability and shareholder returns.

Looking ahead, growth drivers include continued expansion of high-margin services, ongoing innovation in hardware, and potential new product categories (e.g., spatial computing, AI integration). While near-term growth may be moderate, Apple’s strong balance sheet, loyal customer base, and diversified ecosystem provide a solid foundation for long-term value creation.
