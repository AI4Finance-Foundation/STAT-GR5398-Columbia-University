Investment Update: Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD)

Recent Financial Performance  
AMD’s financial performance has shown resilience and strong growth, despite a challenging 2023. Revenue reached $22.7 billion in 2023 (-3.9% YoY), following a robust 43.6% surge in 2022. However, 2024 guidance points to a solid rebound, with revenue expected to climb 13.7% to $25.8 billion, and further acceleration forecasted for 2025 (+34.3%). Contribution margin improved to 49.4% in 2024, and is projected to increase steadily to 52.5% by 2027, demonstrating AMD’s ability to scale profitably.

EBITDA dipped in 2023 but is set to recover to $5.3 billion in 2024 and more than double to $14.2 billion by 2025, with margins expected to expand sharply from 20.4% (2024) to over 42% (2027). EPS is also forecasted to rebound, reaching $2.67 in 2025 and $3.18 by 2027.

Growth Drivers  
Key growth catalysts include AMD’s rising presence in high-performance computing, AI, and data center markets, where demand for advanced chips is accelerating. The ramp-up of new product lines and increased market share in the data center segment are expected to drive significant top-line and margin expansion. AMD’s focus on R&D and operational efficiency is also supporting sustained contribution margin gains.

Peer Comparison  
Relative to Intel (INTC) and Nvidia (NVDA), AMD’s EBITDA growth is strong, though it trades at a premium EV/EBITDA multiple (38.3x for 2024E vs. NVDA’s 43.5x and INTC’s 106.9x). While Nvidia remains the AI leader, AMD’s improving profitability and competitive positioning in AI and data centers underpin its investment case.

Outlook  
With a projected revenue CAGR of 20.5% through 2027, margin expansion, and earnings recovery, AMD is well positioned to benefit from secular tech trends. While valuation remains elevated, continued execution and market share gains in critical growth areas support a positive long-term outlook.
