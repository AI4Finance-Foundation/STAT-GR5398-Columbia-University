**Key Investment Risks for Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. (AMD):**

- **Profitability Concerns:** Despite strong revenue growth projections (20.5% CAGR), AMD’s EBITDA margin dropped from 25.3% in 2021 to 18.3% in 2023, and EPS remains low ($0.53 in 2023), highlighting margin pressure and weaker earnings performance compared to peers.
- **Valuation Risk:** AMD is trading at a high PE ratio (278x in 2023; 123.9x in 2024E) and elevated EV/EBITDA multiples (57.1x in 2023), implying significant market optimism and limited valuation buffer if growth or profitability disappoints.
- **Competitive Pressure:** EBITDA remains significantly below primary competitor NVDA (Nvidia), which is accelerating EBITDA growth (projected $381B in 2027 vs. AMD’s $8.6B), posing substantial competitive risk in key markets like AI and graphics.
- **Operational Cost Escalation:** Cost of operations and SG&A are rising in both absolute and margin terms (SG&A margin from 8.8% in 2021 to 12.0% in 2025E), potentially eroding profitability if not matched by revenue outperformance.
- **Growth Volatility:** Revenue growth is forecasted to decelerate after 2025 (from 34.3% in 2025 to 4% in 2027), and historical performance shows volatility (e.g., -3.9% in 2023), increasing uncertainty around sustained top-line expansion.
