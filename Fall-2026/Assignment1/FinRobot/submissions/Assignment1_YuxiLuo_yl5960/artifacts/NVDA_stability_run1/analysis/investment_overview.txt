NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) – Investment Update

NVIDIA has delivered exceptional financial performance over the past three years, cementing its position as a leader in the semiconductor industry, especially within AI and accelerated computing. Fiscal 2024 revenue surged to $60.9 billion, more than doubling year-over-year, with a robust 125.9% growth. This momentum is set to continue, as consensus estimates project revenue to reach $130.5 billion in 2025 and $215.9 billion in 2026, reflecting an extraordinary 68.3% CAGR through 2027.

Profitability has scaled in tandem with revenue. NVIDIA’s EBITDA margin expanded dramatically to 58.4% in 2024 and is forecasted to reach 73.5% by 2027, far outpacing peers AMD and Intel. EBITDA is expected to grow from $35.6 billion in 2024 to an impressive $144.6 billion by 2026. Contribution margin has also remained high, consistently above 70% in forward years, highlighting the operating leverage and efficiency in NVIDIA’s business model.

Growth drivers include the explosive demand for AI accelerators in data centers, continued leadership in GPU technology, and expansion into new verticals such as automotive and enterprise AI. NVIDIA’s deep ecosystem and strong developer base create additional competitive moats.

Valuation remains elevated but is normalizing. The forward PE ratio is projected to fall from 37.8x in 2026 to 32.4x in 2027 as earnings per share (EPS) compound rapidly. EV/EBITDA multiples are also moderating, dropping from 81.0x in 2023 to 31.4x by 2026, suggesting improving value as growth persists.

Outlook: NVIDIA’s stellar revenue and earnings growth, expanding margins, and dominant position in AI hardware support a positive long-term outlook. While valuation is still rich, continued execution and sector tailwinds make NVIDIA a compelling investment for growth-oriented portfolios.
