NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) is a global leader in accelerated computing and artificial intelligence (AI) technologies. The company’s business model centers on designing and selling high-performance graphics processing units (GPUs), system-on-chip units, and related software, catering to markets including data centers, gaming, professional visualization, and automotive sectors. NVIDIA’s revenue is primarily driven by sales of its cutting-edge hardware and software platforms, licensing intellectual property, and offering AI-based solutions to enterprise customers.

NVIDIA’s product portfolio is anchored by its GPU architectures (notably the GeForce and data center-focused H100 and A100 lines), which have become the de facto standard for machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI workloads. In addition, NVIDIA offers software development kits (SDKs), libraries, and cloud-based AI services that enable customers to accelerate computational workloads across industries. The company also develops automotive platforms (NVIDIA DRIVE) and edge computing solutions, further diversifying its addressable market.

The company holds a dominant position in the data center and AI acceleration markets, outpacing traditional competitors like AMD and Intel. NVIDIA’s GPUs are integral to AI model training and inference, widely adopted by hyperscale cloud providers and enterprises seeking to leverage AI. Recent financial performance highlights its market leadership: revenue surged from $26.9 billion in FY2023 to $60.9 billion in FY2024—a 125.9% increase—driven by explosive demand for AI infrastructure. Forecasts suggest continued rapid growth, with revenues projected to reach $130.5 billion in FY2025 and $215.9 billion in FY2026, representing an extraordinary compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of over 68%.

Profitability metrics underscore this success: EBITDA margin expanded from 22.2% in FY2023 to 58.4% in FY2024, with projected margins above 70% in coming years. NVIDIA’s contribution margin and earnings per share (EPS) have also risen sharply, reflecting strong operational leverage and pricing power. Compared to peers, NVIDIA’s EBITDA and EV/EBITDA valuations remain industry-leading, reflecting its superior growth prospects and strategic importance in the AI ecosystem.

In summary, NVIDIA is at the forefront of the AI and accelerated computing revolution, with a robust business model, diversified product suite, and market dominance that has translated into exceptional financial performance and growth outlook.
