**Key Investment Risks for NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA):**

- **Valuation Risk:** NVIDIA trades at a high PE ratio (37.8x–106.4x) and elevated EV/EBITDA multiples (43.5x in 2024, 33.8x in 2025), significantly above peers (AMD/INTC), exposing investors to potential multiple compression if growth expectations are not met.

- **Sustainability of High Growth:** Revenue growth forecasts (CAGR 68.3% through 2027) assume continued explosive demand for NVIDIA’s products; any slowdown in AI or data center spending could result in material revenue and earnings shortfalls.

- **Margin Compression Risk:** While EBITDA margin is projected to expand (from 22.2% in 2023 to 73.5% in 2027), increased competition, rising input costs, or customer pricing power (e.g., major cloud providers) could pressure margins below expectations.

- **Peer Competitive Threats:** Peers like AMD and Intel are ramping investments in AI and GPU technologies. If competitors close the technology or performance gap, NVIDIA’s market share and pricing power could erode, impacting future financial performance.

- **Execution and Concentration Risk:** NVIDIA’s recent results reflect outsized growth from a concentrated set of AI/data center customers; any execution missteps, supply chain disruptions, or customer order delays could have an outsized impact on results given the high concentration.
