NVIDIA Investment Update – June 2024

NVIDIA has delivered exceptional financial performance over the past two years, cementing its position as the key enabler of the AI and accelerated computing revolution. Fiscal 2024 revenue surged 126% to $60.9 billion, with guidance for 2025 and 2026 pointing to further robust growth (2025E: $130.5B; 2026E: $215.9B). This translates to a striking 68% CAGR through 2027, far outpacing semiconductor peers.

Profitability has expanded even faster. Contribution margin rose to 72.7% in 2024 and is expected to remain above 71% through 2027. EBITDA soared nearly 6x year-on-year in 2024 to $35.6B, with forward projections reaching $86.1B (2025E) and $144.6B (2026E), driving EBITDA margins above 66% and climbing further. EPS is forecast to nearly double each year, reaching $5.23 in 2025E and $5.88 by 2027E.

Growth Drivers: NVIDIA’s data center segment, powered by demand for its H100 and next-gen AI chips, is the primary engine, as hyperscalers and enterprises race to build AI infrastructure. Software and services are emerging contributors, while automotive and edge computing offer longer-term optionality. Cost leverage is evident, with SG&A as a percentage of revenue declining from 9% in 2023 to just 0.6% by 2027E.

Valuation and Peers: While NVIDIA trades at premium multiples (2024E EV/EBITDA: 43.5x, 2025E: 33.8x), this reflects its outsized growth and profitability. Peers such as AMD and Intel trade at lower multiples but lag in both growth and margins.

Outlook: NVIDIA remains the dominant AI platform provider. While valuation is rich and growth may moderate from current breakneck levels, fundamentals remain robust. Continued product leadership, expanding TAM, and operating leverage support a positive long-term outlook, despite near-term volatility.
