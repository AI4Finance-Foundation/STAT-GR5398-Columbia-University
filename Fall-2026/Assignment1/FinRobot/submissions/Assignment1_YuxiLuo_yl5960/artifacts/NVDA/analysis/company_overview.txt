NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) is a global leader in accelerated computing and graphics processing technologies. Founded in 1993 and headquartered in Santa Clara, California, NVIDIA has evolved from a pioneer in graphics processing units (GPUs) for gaming and professional visualization to a dominant force in artificial intelligence (AI), data centers, and high-performance computing.

Business Model:
NVIDIA’s business model revolves around designing and licensing advanced semiconductor products, particularly GPUs and system-on-chip (SoC) solutions. The company primarily generates revenue through the sale of chips, software, and related services to original equipment manufacturers (OEMs), cloud service providers, and enterprise customers. NVIDIA also licenses intellectual property to third parties, expanding its reach into broader markets.

Products and Services:
NVIDIA’s core product lines include the GeForce series for gaming, the NVIDIA RTX and Quadro lines for professional visualization, and the powerful A100 and H100 GPUs for data centers, AI, and machine learning workloads. The company’s CUDA programming platform and AI software stack drive adoption in scientific research, autonomous vehicles, robotics, and edge computing. NVIDIA’s recent focus on AI infrastructure and cloud-based services, including the NVIDIA DGX systems and Omniverse platform, positions it as a key enabler of next-generation computing.

Market Position:
NVIDIA commands a leading position in the GPU market, outpacing rivals such as AMD and Intel in AI and data center applications. The company’s GPUs are the industry standard for training and deploying deep learning models, making NVIDIA indispensable to hyperscale cloud providers and enterprise AI initiatives. The company’s EBITDA performance and margins far surpass those of its peers; for example, in 2024 NVIDIA posted an EBITDA of $35.6 billion, compared to AMD’s $5.3 billion and Intel’s $1.2 billion. Its premium valuation is reflected in EV/EBITDA multiples consistently higher than industry averages, underscoring investor confidence in its growth trajectory.

Recent Performance:
NVIDIA’s financial performance has been extraordinary, with revenue surging from $26.9 billion in fiscal 2023 to $60.9 billion in 2024, and projected to reach $130.5 billion in 2025—a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 68.3%. Profitability has scaled rapidly, with EBITDA margins expanding from 22.2% in 2023 to 66.0% in 2025E. Contribution margins have surpassed 70%, and SG&A expenses are decreasing as a percentage of sales, reflecting strong operational leverage. NVIDIA’s rapid growth is driven by unprecedented demand for AI infrastructure, positioning it as a foundational technology provider for the AI era.

In summary, NVIDIA is at the forefront of the AI-driven transformation of technology, leveraging its leadership in GPUs and software to deliver exceptional growth and profitability, with a clear runway for continued expansion in high-growth markets.
