**Key Investment Risks for NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA):**

- **Valuation Risk:** NVIDIA trades at a high PE (32.4x–106x) and EV/EBITDA (31x–81x) multiples, significantly above industry peers (AMD, INTC). If growth expectations are not met, the stock could face substantial multiple compression.

- **Revenue Growth Sustainability:** After explosive revenue growth in 2024–2026 (CAGR 68.3%), forecasts show a sharp slowdown to low single-digit rates (4%–6%) by 2027. If actual growth decelerates faster than forecast, this could lead to earnings disappointments.

- **Competitive Pressure:** Peer EBITDA data shows rivals like AMD are growing, and INTC is recovering. Market share gains by competitors or technological disruption could erode NVIDIA’s margins and profitability.

- **Profitability Volatility:** NVIDIA’s EBITDA margin jumped from 22.2% (2023) to an estimated 73.5% (2027), with Contribution Margin also rising. Such rapid margin expansion may be difficult to sustain in the face of cost inflation, supply chain issues, or increased R&D/SG&A needs.

- **Execution and Operational Risk:** The company's cost base (SG&A, operations) is projected to remain a small percentage of revenue despite massive growth. Underestimating these costs or failing to scale operationally could impact earnings and investor confidence.
