NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) continues to demonstrate exceptional financial momentum, cementing its leadership in the AI and accelerated computing space. Fiscal 2024 marked a dramatic inflection, with revenue surging 126% year-over-year to $60.9 billion. This growth is projected to accelerate even further, with consensus estimates pointing to a 114% increase in 2025 and a 65% gain in 2026, reaching $215.9 billion. This implies a robust 68% revenue CAGR through 2027.

The company's profitability metrics have improved substantially in parallel with revenue expansion. EBITDA soared nearly six-fold in 2024 to $35.6 billion, with margins climbing from 22.2% in 2023 to 58.4%, signaling strong operating leverage. Margin expansion is expected to continue, with EBITDA margins forecast to reach 73.5% by 2027. Notably, SG&A as a percentage of revenue is projected to decline, reflecting disciplined cost control amid rapid scale-up.

NVIDIA’s leadership in AI chips, ongoing cloud and hyperscale demand, and expanding software and systems ecosystem remain the core growth drivers. The company’s competitive edge is underlined by peer comparisons: NVIDIA’s projected 2027 EBITDA of $382 billion dwarfs AMD and Intel, and its EV/EBITDA multiple, though high, is declining as earnings catch up with valuation.

Despite its premium valuation (2024 PE: 51.6x), NVIDIA’s rapid earnings growth (EPS forecast to reach $5.88 by 2027) and industry leadership arguably justify the multiples. The investment outlook remains highly favorable, supported by secular AI adoption, robust margin trajectory, and strong free cash flow generation. Near-term risks include supply constraints and competitive responses, but NVIDIA remains well positioned for sustained outperformance.
