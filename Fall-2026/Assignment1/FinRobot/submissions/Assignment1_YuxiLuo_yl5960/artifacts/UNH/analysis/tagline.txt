UnitedHealth Group demonstrates robust top-line growth with a revenue CAGR of 11.7% from 2021 to 2027, outpacing many industry peers. However, margin compression is evident, as EBITDA margin declined from 9.8% in 2023 to 5.2% in 2025, reflecting rising operational costs. Despite these pressures, the company maintains strong relative valuation metrics and earnings resilience within the sector.
