UnitedHealth Group (UNH) continues to demonstrate robust growth, with revenue expanding at a CAGR of 11.7% from 2021 to 2027, outpacing many peers. For 2023, UNH delivered $371.6 billion in revenue and $36.3 billion in EBITDA, maintaining an EBITDA margin near 9.8%. However, margin compression is evident, with EBITDA margin projected to decline to 5.2% in 2025 before gradually recovering to 8.2% by 2027, reflecting rising costs and competitive pressures in the healthcare sector.

On valuation, UNH’s 2023 PE ratio stands at 22.4x, rising to 32.3x for 2024E due to lower forecasted EPS, but normalizing to 21.4x by 2027E as earnings growth resumes. This is above the sector average but justified by UNH’s scale, growth profile, and market leadership. On an EV/EBITDA basis, UNH trades at 14.9x (2023), higher than Pfizer (24.1x) but below Johnson & Johnson (18.4x in 2023). By 2025, UNH’s EV/EBITDA is forecast at 15.4x, still at a premium to Pfizer (12.4x) and slightly above JNJ (13.0x).

Compared to peers, UNH’s premium valuation is supported by stronger revenue growth and a resilient business model, though margin volatility and near-term earnings dips warrant caution. Based on peer multiples (12–18x EV/EBITDA, 15–22x PE), UNH appears fairly valued to modestly overvalued at current levels. Long-term upside is underpinned by growth, but the stock’s premium may be capped unless margin recovery accelerates or earnings growth surprises to the upside. Overall, UNH remains a high-quality, fairly valued leader in managed care, suitable for investors seeking stability with moderate growth potential.
