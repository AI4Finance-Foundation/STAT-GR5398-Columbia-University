UnitedHealth Group (UNH) continues to deliver robust top-line growth, with revenue expanding at a 3-year CAGR of 11.7%. Revenue increased from $288 billion in 2021 to $372 billion in 2023, and is forecast to reach over $518 billion by 2027. This growth is driven by expansion in both UnitedHealthcare and Optum, reflecting increased demand for managed care and healthcare services, as well as successful penetration into government programs and value-based care.

However, margins have come under pressure. Contribution margin declined from 24.5% in 2023 to 22.3% in 2024 and is projected to fall further to 18.5% in 2025 before gradually recovering. EBITDA margin followed a similar trend, falling from 9.8% in 2023 to an expected 5.2% in 2025, although a moderate rebound is anticipated. The margin compression is primarily due to rising costs of operations, heightened medical utilization, and ongoing investments in technology and integration within Optum.

Despite these margin challenges, UNH’s EBITDA performance remains stronger than most peers. In 2023, UNH posted $36.3 billion in EBITDA, outpacing both Johnson & Johnson and Pfizer. The company’s EV/EBITDA multiple (15.4x 2025E) reflects a premium to Pfizer but is in line with sector leaders, justified by UNH’s scale and consistent growth profile.

Earnings per share (EPS) dipped in 2024 due to margin pressures but are expected to recover steadily. The stock currently trades at a forward P/E of 23.7x (2025E), which is reasonable considering its growth and industry position.

Outlook: UnitedHealth is well-positioned for long-term growth thanks to its diversified business model, scale, and continued expansion into high-growth segments. Investors should monitor cost trends and margin recovery, but the company’s leadership in managed care and services supports a constructive long-term view.
