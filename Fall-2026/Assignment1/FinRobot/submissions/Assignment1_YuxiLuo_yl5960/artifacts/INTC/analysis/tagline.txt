Intel Corporation is navigating a period of declining revenues and compressed margins, with EBITDA falling sharply from $33.9B in 2021 to just $1.2B in 2024, before a projected recovery in 2025 and beyond. Profitability remains under pressure, as reflected by negative EPS and a temporarily elevated EV/EBITDA multiple compared to peers. While cost controls and margin improvements are expected to aid recovery, Intel's near-term financial position remains challenged amid heightened industry competition.
