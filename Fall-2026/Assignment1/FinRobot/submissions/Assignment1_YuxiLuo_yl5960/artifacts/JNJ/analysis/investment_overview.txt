Investment Update: Johnson & Johnson (JNJ)

Johnson & Johnson reported robust financial performance in 2023, with revenue growing 1.6% year-over-year to $79.99 billion. Growth accelerated significantly in 2024, with revenue reaching $88.82 billion, up 11.0%. Looking ahead, consensus estimates project continued mid-single-digit revenue growth, with a CAGR of 6.2% through 2027, reaching $109.0 billion.

Profitability metrics remain strong. Contribution margin stabilized around 69-70%, with a modest dip to 67.9% in 2025 before rebounding. EBITDA margin is expected to expand meaningfully—from 27.9% in 2024 to 43.6% in 2025 and further to 47.3% by 2027—suggesting improved operational efficiency and potential benefits from portfolio optimization or cost management initiatives. Earnings per share are projected to rebound sharply from $5.84 in 2024 to $11.03 in 2025, and continue growing to $13.15 by 2027, supporting a declining PE ratio and attractive valuation (16.1x by 2027).

Key growth drivers include JNJ’s diversified healthcare portfolio, ongoing innovation in pharmaceuticals and medtech, and disciplined expense management. Notably, SG&A margins are forecast to steadily decline, further supporting profitability gains.

Compared to peers AbbVie (ABBV) and Pfizer (PFE), JNJ’s forward EBITDA is expected to outpace both, with significant margin improvement and a competitive EV/EBITDA multiple (12.97x in 2025 vs. ABBV at 26.6x and PFE at 12.4x).

Outlook: Johnson & Johnson is well positioned for steady revenue growth and margin expansion, underpinned by scale, innovation, and operational discipline. The company’s defensive qualities, strong balance sheet, and improving profitability profile make it an attractive long-term holding relative to sector peers.
