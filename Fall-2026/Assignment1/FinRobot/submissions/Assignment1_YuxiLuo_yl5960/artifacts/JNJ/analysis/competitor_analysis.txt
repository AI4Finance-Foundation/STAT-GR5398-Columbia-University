Johnson & Johnson (JNJ) stands out among its large-cap pharmaceutical peers, notably AbbVie (ABBV) and Pfizer (PFE), with a balanced financial profile, strong operational performance, and robust growth outlook.

**EBITDA and Profitability:**  
JNJ’s EBITDA remained stable in 2022 ($26.7B) and 2023 ($26.6B), and is forecasted to nearly double by 2025 ($41.1B), significantly outpacing ABBV and PFE. While ABBV’s EBITDA is projected to decline from $24.2B (2022) to $16.6B (2026), and PFE’s EBITDA falls sharply post-pandemic, JNJ’s trajectory indicates superior operational scalability and cost discipline. JNJ’s EBITDA margin is also projected to improve from 33.3% (2023) to 47.3% (2027), suggesting increasing efficiency.

**Revenue Growth:**  
JNJ’s revenue growth accelerates from 1.6% in 2023 to an expected 6.2% CAGR through 2027. This is relatively strong for a diversified healthcare major, reflecting resilience and strategic portfolio management. The company also maintains high contribution margins (~70%), indicating strong pricing power and effective cost controls.

**Valuation:**  
JNJ’s EV/EBITDA multiple, while elevated at 18.4x in 2023 (above ABBV’s 18.7x and PFE’s 24.1x), is forecasted to fall to 12.97x by 2025, making it more attractively valued relative to peers as earnings scale. Its PE ratio is expected to normalize from 24.5x (2024) to 16.1x (2027), further improving its valuation proposition.

**Summary:**  
Compared to ABBV and PFE, Johnson & Johnson demonstrates superior earnings growth, margin expansion, and increasing valuation appeal over the forecast period. Its diversified business model and operational efficiency position it as a more stable and attractive investment relative to peers facing patent cliffs (ABBV) or post-pandemic normalization (PFE).
