AbbVie Inc. (ABBV) Investment Update

Recent financial performance for AbbVie reflects a period of transition, with revenue declining by 6.4% in 2023 to $54.3 billion, primarily due to the U.S. loss of exclusivity for Humira, its flagship product. However, the company stabilized in 2024 with a modest 3.7% revenue rebound, and consensus expectations point to mid-single-digit growth through 2027 (CAGR 2.1%), reaching over $70 billion by that year.

Profitability was pressured in 2023, as EBITDA dropped sharply to $17.2 billion (31.6% margin), down from $24.2 billion (41.6% margin) in 2022. This was driven by both lower revenues and higher costs including R&D and SG&A, as AbbVie ramped up investment to support new launches. However, EBITDA margins are expected to recover meaningfully, rising to 48.8% in 2025 and exceeding 50% by 2027, reflecting successful cost containment and the ramp-up of new products.

Key growth drivers include rapid adoption of immunology drugs Skyrizi and Rinvoq, which are partially offsetting Humira declines, as well as a growing presence in aesthetics and neuroscience. AbbVie’s pipeline and recent approvals position it well for renewed growth, with major launches expected to contribute meaningfully after 2025.

Compared to peers, AbbVie’s 2023 EBITDA was lower than Johnson & Johnson (JNJ) but ahead of Pfizer (PFE). The company’s EV/EBITDA multiple has expanded, reflecting investor optimism about its post-Humira trajectory, though it now trades at a premium to both JNJ and PFE.

Overall, while the near-term earnings outlook remains subdued (EPS troughing in 2024-25), AbbVie’s strong pipeline, improving margins, and revenue acceleration support a constructive long-term outlook. Investors should monitor execution on new product launches and margin recovery as key catalysts.
