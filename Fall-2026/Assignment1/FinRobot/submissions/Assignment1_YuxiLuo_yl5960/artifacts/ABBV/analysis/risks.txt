**Key Investment Risks for AbbVie Inc. (ABBV):**

- **Earnings and Margin Compression:** EBITDA and EBITDA margin have declined significantly from 2021 to 2024 (42.6% to 26.5%), with only a partial recovery forecasted. This raises concerns about the sustainability of profitability.

- **High Valuation Risk:** The PE ratio has increased sharply (from ~21x in 2021 to over 74x in 2024 and ~96x in 2025), suggesting the stock may be overvalued relative to earnings, especially with declining EPS.

- **Peer Underperformance:** ABBV’s EBITDA is lower and recovering more slowly than major peers (JNJ, PFE), and its EV/EBITDA is higher than both, indicating potential competitive and valuation disadvantages.

- **Revenue Volatility:** Revenue growth has turned negative in 2023 (-6.4%) and is projected to remain modest (CAGR 2.1%), signaling potential market share loss or product lifecycle risks.

- **Operational Cost Pressure:** Cost of operations and SG&A remain high relative to revenue, with only marginal improvements forecasted, which could constrain future profit growth if not managed effectively.
