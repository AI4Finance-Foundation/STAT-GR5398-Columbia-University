Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) Investment Update

Alphabet continued to deliver robust financial performance in 2023 and is positioned for strong growth in the coming years. Revenue increased 8.7% in 2023 to $307.4 billion, with growth accelerating to 13.9% in 2024 (guidance: $350.0 billion), and a forecasted 11.8% CAGR through 2027. This growth is primarily driven by resilient digital advertising demand, expanding cloud services, and increasing monetization across Google’s ecosystem.

Operational efficiency is improving. Cost of operations as a percentage of revenue has remained stable, while SG&A margin is expected to decline from 14.4% in 2023 to 11.0% in 2027, reflecting disciplined expense management. Contribution margin improved to 56.6% in 2023 and is projected to rise to 62.7% by 2027, supporting sustained profitability.

EBITDA grew 15% in 2023 to $97.97 billion and is forecasted to more than double by 2027 ($241.1 billion), with margin expansion from 31.9% to 51.7%. This positions Alphabet competitively: its 2023 EBITDA surpassed Meta’s ($59.1 billion) and is on par with Microsoft’s ($105.1 billion), while its EV/EBITDA multiple (18.2x) remains reasonable versus peers.

Earnings per share (EPS) rebounded to $5.84 in 2023 and are expected to reach $13.01 by 2027, underpinned by operating leverage and share repurchases. The stock trades at a forward PE of 23–28x, in line with sector averages, reflecting confidence in Alphabet’s outlook.

Outlook: Alphabet’s leading position in digital ad markets, rapid advances in AI, and strong cloud momentum support a positive long-term view. Margin expansion and disciplined cost control are set to drive continued EPS and EBITDA growth, while valuation remains attractive relative to peers. Alphabet is well-placed for sustained value creation.
