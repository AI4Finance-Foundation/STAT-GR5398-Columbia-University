Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) trades at a 2024E P/E of 23.4x and an EV/EBITDA of 17.3x, with forward 2025E multiples of 27.3x and 21.2x, respectively. These metrics reflect both the company's robust growth outlook and its premium position within the digital advertising and cloud markets. Alphabet’s revenue is expected to grow at a CAGR of 11.8% from 2023 to 2027, with margin expansion driving EBITDA margins from 31.9% in 2023 to 51.7% by 2027, indicating significant operating leverage.

In peer comparison, Alphabet’s 2024E EV/EBITDA of 17.3x is near Meta’s 17.1x and below Microsoft’s 26.1x. While Microsoft commands a higher multiple due to broader enterprise exposure and diversified SaaS revenues, Alphabet’s multiple is justified by its consistent cash flow, dominant ad business, and growing cloud segment. Meta, while cheaper, faces more platform-specific risks and regulatory scrutiny, partially explaining its lower valuation.

On earnings, Alphabet’s 2024E P/E is in line with its growth trajectory and below the 2025E level, suggesting expectations of further earnings acceleration. With EBITDA projections outpacing peers (2024E: $135.4B vs. Meta’s $86.9B and Microsoft’s $133.0B), Alphabet’s scale and efficiency improvements support its premium.

In fair value assessment, Alphabet’s valuation appears reasonable given its strong growth, expanding margins, and leading industry position. The current multiples do not seem stretched relative to peers or historical averages, and sustained double-digit growth supports further upside. Overall, Alphabet remains attractively valued for long-term investors seeking exposure to digital advertising, AI, and cloud infrastructure.
