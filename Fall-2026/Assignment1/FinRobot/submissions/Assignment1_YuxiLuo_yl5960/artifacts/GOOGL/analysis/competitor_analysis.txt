Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) remains a dominant force in the technology sector, competing primarily with Meta Platforms (META) and Microsoft (MSFT). Analyzing recent financial metrics, Alphabet’s revenue has displayed a robust CAGR of 11.8% from 2021 to 2027, underpinned by consistent growth in contribution margin (from 56.9% in 2021 to an estimated 62.7% in 2027) and improving EBITDA margin (from 40.2% to 51.7%). This suggests effective cost management and increasing operational efficiency, particularly as SG&A margins are projected to decline over the period.

In EBITDA terms, Alphabet’s scale is impressive: 2023 EBITDA was $97.97B, outpacing Meta ($59.05B) but trailing Microsoft ($105.14B). By 2027, Alphabet is forecast to reach $211.85B in EBITDA, narrowing the gap with Microsoft ($249.74B) and maintaining a strong lead over Meta ($128.68B). This reflects Alphabet’s ability to leverage its core advertising business and diversify through cloud and other bets.

Valuation, as measured by EV/EBITDA, also positions Alphabet between its peers. In 2023, its EV/EBITDA (18.2x) was higher than Meta (15.4x) but lower than Microsoft (24.5x), indicating a balanced market view of its growth prospects and profitability. Alphabet’s PE ratio (24.1x in 2023) also sits between Meta and Microsoft, reflecting solid earnings growth but with less premium than Microsoft, likely due to perceived risks or slower growth in certain segments.

In sum, Alphabet exhibits strong financial performance and operational improvements relative to its peers. It holds a middle ground in valuation, offers greater EBITDA scale than Meta, and is steadily closing the EBITDA gap with Microsoft, reinforcing its position as a resilient, highly profitable tech leader.
