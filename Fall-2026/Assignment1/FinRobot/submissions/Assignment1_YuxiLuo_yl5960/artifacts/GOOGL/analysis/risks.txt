**Key Investment Risks for Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) Based on Provided Data:**

- **Margin Volatility:** EBITDA margin fluctuated significantly (from 40.2% in 2021 to 30.1% in 2022, then rebounding to 38.7% in 2024 and projected to rise), indicating potential operational cost volatility or revenue quality concerns.

- **Premium Valuation:** GOOGL’s forward EV/EBITDA and PE ratios are generally higher than peers (e.g., higher than META and comparable to or above MSFT in 2024-2025), implying elevated market expectations and risk of multiple contraction if growth underperforms.

- **Decelerating Revenue Growth:** Revenue growth rates are projected to slow materially after 2025 (from 15.1% in 2025 to just 4-6% in 2026-2027), raising concerns about the sustainability of high growth and the company’s ability to find new growth drivers.

- **Competitive Pressure:** Peer EBITDA and EV/EBITDA data show that Microsoft and Meta are improving profitability, suggesting intensifying competition in digital advertising, AI, and cloud markets, which may pressure Alphabet’s market share and margins.

- **Earnings Sensitivity:** EPS and contribution margins have shown both declines and recoveries (EPS dropped in 2022 before rebounding), signaling earnings are sensitive to macroeconomic conditions, ad spending cycles, or internal execution risks.
