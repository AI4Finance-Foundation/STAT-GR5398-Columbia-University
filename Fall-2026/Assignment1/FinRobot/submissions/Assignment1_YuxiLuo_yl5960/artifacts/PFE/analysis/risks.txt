**Key Investment Risks for Pfizer Inc. (PFE):**

- **Revenue Volatility and Decline**: Pfizer’s revenue fell sharply by 40.6% in 2023 and the 2021-2027 CAGR is -6.3%, indicating inconsistent top-line growth and risks to sustainable sales momentum.

- **Margin Compression and Profitability Concerns**: EBITDA margin dropped significantly to 16.0% in 2023 from 40.9% in 2022, and EPS fell to $0.38 in 2023 (down from $5.59 in 2022), reflecting operational challenges and potential profitability headwinds.

- **Relative Underperformance vs. Peers**: Pfizer’s 2023 EBITDA ($9.56B) lags behind both AbbVie ($17.2B) and Johnson & Johnson ($26.6B), pointing to competitive pressures and possible loss of market share.

- **Elevated Valuation Despite Earnings Pressure**: The 2023 PE ratio spiked to 76.7, and EV/EBITDA reached 24.1—well above peers and historical levels—suggesting overvaluation risk if earnings do not rebound as projected.

- **Uncertain Recovery Trajectory**: Future EBITDA and margin projections show improvement, but depend on execution and market factors; missed forecasts or pipeline setbacks could further impair financial recovery.
