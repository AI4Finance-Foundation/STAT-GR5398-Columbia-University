Investment Update: Pfizer Inc. (PFE)

Pfizer’s recent financial performance reflects significant post-pandemic normalization, with 2023 revenue declining 40.6% to $59.6 billion after a COVID-19 vaccine-driven peak in 2022 ($100.3 billion). Revenue is expected to stabilize, rebounding 6.8% in 2024 and growing modestly at a 5-6% annual rate through 2026E, driven by new product launches and expansion in key therapeutic areas such as oncology and rare diseases.

Profitability metrics show a similar reset. EBITDA fell sharply in 2023 to $9.6 billion (16% margin) but is projected to recover to $18.1 billion in 2024 (28.5% margin) and further expand, with margins exceeding 50% by 2026E as the cost base normalizes and higher-margin products ramp. Contribution margins are also set to rise, benefiting from lower operational costs and a more favorable product mix.

Peer comparison shows Pfizer’s EBITDA temporarily lagging AbbVie (ABBV) and Johnson & Johnson (JNJ) in 2023-2024 but catching up as earnings recover. Valuation multiples, particularly EV/EBITDA, have normalized from a 2023 spike (24.1x) to a more attractive 12.4x in 2025E, below ABBV and in line with JNJ, suggesting improved relative value as earnings visibility returns.

Looking ahead, Pfizer’s outlook is anchored by its late-stage pipeline, integration of recent acquisitions, and cost discipline. While the recent earnings reset reflects the post-pandemic environment, the company is positioned for steady growth, margin expansion, and long-term value creation. Continued execution on innovation and capital allocation will be key catalysts for share performance.
