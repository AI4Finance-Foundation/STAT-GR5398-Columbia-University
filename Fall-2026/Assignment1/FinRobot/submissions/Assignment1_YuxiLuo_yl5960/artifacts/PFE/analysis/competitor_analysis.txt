Competitor Analysis: Pfizer Inc. (PFE) vs. AbbVie (ABBV) and Johnson & Johnson (JNJ)

Pfizer stands as a leading global pharmaceutical company, but recent years have shown considerable volatility compared to peers AbbVie and Johnson & Johnson. Pfizer’s revenue peaked in 2022 at $100.3B, driven by COVID-19 vaccine sales, but dropped sharply by 40.6% in 2023 as pandemic-related demand waned. This revenue retracement contrasts with more stable top-line performance at AbbVie and JNJ, whose business models rely less on pandemic-dependent products.

Profitability, as measured by EBITDA, also reveals significant swings for Pfizer. EBITDA margins fell from 40.9% in 2022 to 16% in 2023, though they are projected to recover above 50% by 2026, suggesting management expects operational efficiencies or new product cycles to drive future gains. In comparison, JNJ and AbbVie have maintained steadier EBITDA trends, with JNJ outpacing both peers by 2026. Pfizer’s EV/EBITDA multiples spiked in 2023 (24.1x) amid earnings compression, but are projected to normalize by 2024–2025, aligning more closely with ABBV and JNJ.

Pfizer’s contribution margins are forecast to outperform peers, climbing from 65.8% in 2024 to over 73% by 2027, indicating anticipated cost discipline and higher-value product mix. However, EPS and PE ratios show Pfizer currently trades at a premium (high PE in 2023 due to lower earnings), but this gap is expected to close as earnings recover.

Overall, while Pfizer’s financials have been more volatile due to its COVID-19 exposure, its future margin expansion and stabilization efforts may position it competitively. Yet, JNJ and AbbVie offer greater near-term earnings stability, which may appeal to risk-averse investors. Pfizer’s key challenge will be to diversify revenue and deliver on margin improvement to match or surpass its peers’ performance consistency.
