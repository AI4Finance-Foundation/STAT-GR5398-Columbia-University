NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) is a global leader in visual computing technologies and has established itself as the dominant force in the graphics processing unit (GPU) market. Founded in 1993 and headquartered in Santa Clara, California, NVIDIA’s business model centers on designing and selling high-performance GPUs and related hardware and software products, primarily targeting gaming, professional visualization, data centers, and automotive markets. The company’s recent strategic focus on artificial intelligence (AI) and accelerated computing has further expanded its reach into cloud computing, enterprise AI, and hyperscale data center infrastructure.

NVIDIA’s flagship products include the GeForce line for gaming, Quadro for professional visualization, Tesla and A100/H100 series for data centers, and DRIVE for automotive applications. Its CUDA programming platform and AI-focused software libraries are critical differentiators, allowing developers to harness GPU acceleration for a wide range of scientific, industrial, and enterprise workloads. The company also monetizes software and services through its AI enterprise suites and cloud-based offerings.

NVIDIA holds a commanding market position, particularly in data center AI acceleration, where its GPUs have become the industry standard for training and inference of large AI models. Major cloud service providers and enterprises rely heavily on NVIDIA hardware and software for AI workloads, putting the company at the center of the current AI investment boom. In the gaming market, NVIDIA’s GeForce GPUs hold a substantial market share, prized for their leading performance and robust software ecosystem.

Financially, NVIDIA’s recent performance has been extraordinary. Revenue surged from $26.9 billion in fiscal 2023 to $60.9 billion in fiscal 2024, reflecting explosive demand for AI infrastructure. Contribution margin and EBITDA margin both sharply improved, reaching 72.7% and 58.4% respectively in 2024, and are projected to climb even further in the coming years. EPS also grew rapidly, rising from $0.18 in 2023 to $1.21 in 2024. The company’s projected revenue CAGR from 2024 to 2027 is an exceptional 68.3%, with EBITDA and profit margins expected to remain strong. Compared to peers AMD and Intel, NVIDIA’s EBITDA and valuation multiples are significantly higher, underscoring its superior growth prospects and market leadership.

In summary, NVIDIA is uniquely positioned at the heart of the AI revolution, with a powerful blend of hardware, software, and ecosystem advantages. Its rapid financial growth, robust profitability, and dominant market share in AI and high-performance computing make it one of the most influential and valuable technology companies globally.
