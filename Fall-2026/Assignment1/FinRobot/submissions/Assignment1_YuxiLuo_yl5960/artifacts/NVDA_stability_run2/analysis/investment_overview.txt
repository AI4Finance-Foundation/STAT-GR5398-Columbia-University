NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) Investment Update

NVIDIA has delivered an exceptional financial turnaround, highlighted by explosive revenue and earnings growth in the most recent years. After a flat top-line in 2023 ($26.97B), NVIDIA’s 2024 revenue surged 126% to $60.92B, and is projected to more than double again in 2025 to $130.5B. Growth is expected to remain robust, with consensus forecasts of $226.7B in 2025E and $240.3B in 2026E, implying a 68% revenue CAGR through 2027.

Profitability has scaled even faster. EBITDA climbed from $5.99B in 2023 to $35.58B in 2024, with margins expanding from 22% to 58%. Forward projections show EBITDA margins exceeding 70% by 2027, underlining extraordinary operating leverage as sales scale. NVDA’s EPS jumped from $0.18 in 2023 to $1.21 in 2024 and is expected to reach $5.88 by 2027.

NVIDIA’s growth is powered by surging demand for AI infrastructure, data center chips, and accelerated computing. The company's leadership in GPU technology, combined with new product launches and ecosystem expansion, has positioned it as the clear industry leader. 

Compared to peers, NVIDIA stands out on both growth and profitability. Its EBITDA in 2025E is projected at $86.1B, far ahead of AMD ($7.3B) and Intel ($14.4B). The premium is also evident in valuation: NVDA trades at 43.5x EV/EBITDA (2024E), declining to 31.4x (2026E) as earnings expand, still above peers but justified by unique growth dynamics and dominant market share.

Outlook: NVIDIA’s rapid growth trajectory and margin expansion are expected to continue, driven by AI adoption and data center investments. While valuation remains rich, fundamentals support a positive long-term outlook for investors seeking exposure to the AI and advanced computing revolution.
