Company Overview

Broadcom Inc. (ticker: AVGO) is a global technology company specializing in the design, development, and supply of a broad range of semiconductor and infrastructure software solutions. The company’s business model integrates semiconductor products with software offerings, serving diverse end markets including data centers, networking, broadband, wireless communications, and enterprise storage. Broadcom’s portfolio includes semiconductors for wired and wireless communications, enterprise storage, and industrial applications, as well as infrastructure software solutions that support business-critical operations.

In terms of market position, Broadcom is recognized as a leading player in the semiconductor industry, competing alongside major peers such as NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, and Qualcomm. Its diversified product offering and focus on high-performance technologies have contributed to its strong presence in key growth markets, including data center networking and broadband connectivity.

Financially, Broadcom has demonstrated robust revenue growth over the historical period from fiscal year 2021 to 2025. Revenue increased from approximately $27.45 billion in 2021 to $63.89 billion in 2025, representing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 23.5%. This growth was particularly notable in 2024 with a 44.0% year-over-year increase, followed by 23.9% growth in 2025. The company’s EBITDA proxy, a measure combining operating income and depreciation/amortization components, rose from about $19.96 billion in 2023 to $34.12 billion in 2025. The EBITDA margin showed some variability, recorded at 55.7% in 2023, temporarily declining to 45.2% in 2024, before recovering to 53.4% in 2025. Forecasts for 2026 through 2028 project more moderate revenue growth rates of 5.0%, 6.0%, and 4.0%, respectively, with EBITDA margin assumed to remain steady at the 2025 level of 53.4%.
