Company Overview

QUALCOMM Incorporated (QCOM) is a leading technology company specializing in the design and development of semiconductor products and telecommunications equipment. Its business model revolves primarily around licensing its extensive portfolio of patents related to wireless communications and manufacturing and selling integrated circuits used in mobile devices and other wireless products. This dual approach enables QUALCOMM to generate revenue both from royalties based on its intellectual property and from product sales.

QUALCOMM’s key products include chipsets for smartphones, modems, and other wireless communication devices that support various standards such as 4G LTE and 5G. The company also provides technology solutions for automotive, Internet of Things (IoT), and networking markets, reinforcing its presence across diverse segments within the communications technology ecosystem.

In terms of market position, QUALCOMM is recognized as one of the dominant players in the semiconductor and mobile chipset industry, competing with companies such as NVIDIA, AMD, Intel, and Broadcom. Its strong patent portfolio and early investments in 5G technology have positioned it as a critical supplier and licensor in the wireless communication value chain.

Looking at recent financial performance from fiscal years 2021 to 2025, QUALCOMM’s revenue exhibited variability with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of approximately 7.2%. EBITDA itself showed strong growth in 2022, reaching $17.6 billion, but declined notably in 2023 to $9.6 billion before recovering to $13.95 billion in 2025.
