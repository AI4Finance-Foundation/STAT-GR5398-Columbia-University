NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) has demonstrated remarkable financial growth and operational improvement over the 2022 to 2026 period, with forecasts extending through 2029. The company’s revenue expanded from $26.9 billion in 2022 to an estimated $215.9 billion in 2026, reflecting a robust compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 68.3%. This rapid revenue increase is supported by significant gains in contribution profit, which rose from $17.5 billion in 2022 to $153.5 billion in 2026, with contribution margin improving from 64.9% to around 71.1% in 2026. 

EBITDA trends are particularly notable. Although EBITDA fell to $6.0 billion in 2023 from $11.4 billion in 2022, it is projected to rebound sharply to $144.6 billion by 2026, boosting EBITDA margin from 22.2% in 2023 to nearly 67% in 2026. Forecasts through 2029 suggest further margin expansion to approximately 73.5%, indicating increased operational efficiency and scalability. SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue are expected to decline significantly, from 9.0% in 2023 to just 0.6% in 2029, which supports margin expansion.

Earnings per share (EPS) also show strong growth, from $0.18 in 2023 to an estimated $4.93 in 2026 and further growth through 2029, signaling improved profitability. Compared to peers like AMD, Intel, Broadcom, and Qualcomm, NVIDIA’s EBITDA is projected to outpace competitors significantly, with 2026 EBITDA forecast at $144.6 billion versus AMD’s $14.2 billion and Intel’s $15.3 billion. 

However, valuation multiples such as EV/EBITDA remain high relative to peers, with NVIDIA’s EV/EBITDA at 31.4x in 2026 compared to lower multiples for AMD and Intel, indicating market expectations for continued strong growth but also signaling valuation risk.

The outlook for NVIDIA is characterized by strong growth drivers in its core markets, including data centers, AI, and gaming. The company’s expanding margins and profitability reflect operational leverage and efficient cost management. Key risks include the sustainability of rapid growth rates, competitive pressures, and market volatility affecting valuation multiples. Investors should monitor these factors alongside NVIDIA’s execution on growth opportunities.

In summary, NVIDIA presents a compelling growth profile with improving profitability and strong market positioning, balanced against valuation considerations and execution risks.
