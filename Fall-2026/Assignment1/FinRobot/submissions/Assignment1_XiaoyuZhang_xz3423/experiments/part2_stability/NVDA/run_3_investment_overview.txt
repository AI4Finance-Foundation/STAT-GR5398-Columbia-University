NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) has demonstrated remarkable financial growth, with revenues increasing from $26.9 billion in 2022 to an estimated $215.9 billion in 2026, reflecting a historic compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 68.3% between 2022 and 2026. This surge is accompanied by substantial improvements in profitability metrics. Contribution profit rose sharply from $17.5 billion in 2022 to $153.5 billion in 2026, with contribution margin improving from 64.9% to around 71.1%-75.0% over the same period. EBITDA also shows a strong upward trend, expanding from $11.4 billion in 2022 to an estimated $144.6 billion in 2026, with EBITDA margin increasing from 42.2% to nearly 67%.

Cost of operations has increased, but at a slower pace relative to revenue growth, supporting margin expansion. Selling, General & Administrative (SG&A) expenses as a percentage of revenue are forecasted to decline significantly from 8.0% in 2022 to 2.1% in 2026, indicating improved operational efficiency.

Earnings per share (EPS) have grown impressively, from $0.39 in 2022 to an estimated $4.93 in 2026, underscoring strong bottom-line growth. Comparing to peers, NVIDIA’s EBITDA is substantially higher, with an estimated $144.6 billion in 2026 versus AMD's $14.2 billion and Intel's $15.3 billion, highlighting NVIDIA’s dominant position in the semiconductor space. The company’s EV/EBITDA multiple stood at 31.4x in 2026, reflecting market expectations of sustained growth.

Looking forward, revenue growth is expected to moderate to 5-6% annually between 2027 and 2029, with EBITDA margins continuing to improve, reaching approximately 73.5% by 2029. However, as the company scales, maintaining such high growth rates may be challenging. Key risks include market competition, supply chain constraints, and potential shifts in semiconductor demand.

Overall, NVIDIA’s financial trajectory is robust, driven by strong revenue expansion, margin improvement, and operational efficiency. Investors should consider that forward-looking estimates carry uncertainty, and market conditions may impact these projections. The external analyst consensus target price is $339.17 with a Buy rating, but this does not constitute an independent valuation.
