- **High Revenue Growth Volatility**: NVIDIA's revenue growth exhibits extreme jumps, e.g., from 0.2% in 2023A to 125.9% in 2024A and 114.2% in 2025A, indicating potential execution risk in sustaining such rapid expansion.

- **EBITDA Margin Fluctuations**: EBITDA margin fell sharply from 42.2% in 2022A to 22.2% in 2023A before rebounding to 58.4% in 2024A and above 70% in forecasts, signaling volatility in profitability that may affect cash flow predictability.

- **Cost of Operations Growth Outpacing Revenue in Some Years**: Cost of operations more than doubled from 2024A to 2026A (from ~$16.6B to ~$62.5B), which, if not controlled, could compress margins despite revenue growth.

- **High Valuation Multiples Relative to Peers**: NVIDIA's EV/EBITDA multiples are significantly higher than Intel and AMD for overlapping years (e.g., 81.05x in 2023A vs. Intel’s 22.48x), suggesting elevated market expectations and valuation risk if growth falters.

- **Uncertainty in SG&A Expense Trends**: SG&A margins have an unusual pattern, rising from 8.0% in 2022A to 9.0% in 2023A, then sharply declining to 0.6% by 2029E, which may be optimistic and pose risk if expense efficiencies are not realized.
