- **Revenue Concentration and Growth Volatility:** NVIDIA’s revenue shows extreme growth volatility with a 125.9% increase from 2023A to 2024A and 114.2% from 2024A to 2025A, then slowing to low single-digit growth by 2027E-2029E. Such rapid fluctuations could pose execution and sustainability risks.

- **Rising Cost of Operations:** The Cost of Operations is projected to increase substantially from $11.6B in 2023A to $62.5B in 2026A, indicating potential margin pressure or operational scalability challenges despite improving Contribution Margin percentages.

- **Declining SG&A Expenses and Potential Underinvestment:** SG&A expenses as a percentage of revenue decline sharply from 9.0% in 2023A to 0.6% in 2029E, which may risk underinvestment in sales, marketing, and administration functions critical for sustaining growth.

- **High Valuation Multiples:** NVIDIA’s EV/EBITDA ratio remains elevated compared to peers, e.g., 81.05 in 2023A versus AMD at 57.12 and INTC at 22.48, implying significant market expectations and vulnerability to valuation corrections.

- **Dependence on Continued Profitability Improvements:** EBITDA margin recovers from a low of 22.2% in 2023A to 66.0%+ by 2025A and above 70% in later years. Failure to achieve these margin improvements could materially impact earnings and stock performance.
