Revenue Growth: NVIDIA's revenue exhibited minimal growth between 2022A and 2023A at 0.2%, followed by a dramatic surge of 125.9% in 2024A and 114.2% in 2025A, leading to a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 68.3% from 2022A to 2026A. However, revenue growth is expected to slow significantly in the forecast period, with growth rates of 5.0%, 6.0%, and 4.0% in 2027E, 2028E, and 2029E respectively.

Gross Profit Margin: Gross Profit Margin data is not explicitly provided, but Contribution Margin, which is a proxy for profitability after cost of operations, improved from 56.9% in 2023A to a high of 75.0% in 2025A. This margin is forecasted to remain strong and stable, hovering around 72.1% to 74.1% from 2027E through 2029E, indicating efficient cost management relative to revenue.

SG&A Expense Margin: SG&A Margin peaked at 9.0% in 2023A but has since decreased sharply to 2.7% in 2025A and is forecasted to continue declining, reaching an exceptionally low 0.6% by 2029E. This significant reduction suggests improved operating leverage or cost control in selling, general, and administrative expenses as the company scales.

EBITDA Margin Stability: NVIDIA's EBITDA Margin saw a substantial dip from 42.2% in 2022A to 22.2% in 2023A, followed by a strong recovery to 58.4% in 2024A and further improvement to 66.9% in 2026A. The margin is projected to increase gradually, reaching 73.5% by 2029E, reflecting enhanced profitability and operational efficiency over time.
