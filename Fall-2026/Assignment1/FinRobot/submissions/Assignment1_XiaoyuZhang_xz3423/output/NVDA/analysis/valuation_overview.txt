NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) demonstrates robust growth and improving profitability metrics over the forecast period from 2027E to 2029E. The company’s revenue is projected to grow at a historical CAGR of 68.3% from 2022A to 2026A, reaching approximately $250 billion by 2029E. Contribution Margin remains strong, improving from 71.1% in 2026A to 74.1% in 2029E, indicating efficient control over direct costs relative to revenue. EBITDA and EBITDA Margin show significant expansion, with EBITDA increasing from $144.6 billion in 2026A to $183.7 billion in 2029E and the margin improving from 66.9% to 73.5%, reflecting enhanced operational leverage and profitability. Meanwhile, SG&A expenses decline as a percentage of revenue, dropping from 2.1% in 2026A to 0.6% in 2029E, further boosting earnings potential.

When compared to peers AMD and Intel, NVIDIA’s EBITDA is substantially higher and is forecasted to reach $159.85 billion by 2027E, dwarfing AMD's $8.6 billion and Intel's $47.4 billion. NVIDIA’s EV/EBITDA multiples, though high, trend downwards from 54.7x in 2022A to an estimated 31.4x in 2026A, indicating a potential moderation in valuation premium as the company matures. This compares with AMD’s fluctuating EV/EBITDA multiples peaking around 57x and Intel’s multiples varying widely, reflecting different market sentiments and growth prospects.

Considering NVIDIA’s dominant market position, exceptional growth trajectory, and improving margin profile, the current elevated valuation multiples appear justified. However, the downward trend in EV/EBITDA suggests that market expectations are adjusting to a more sustainable growth phase. Overall, NVIDIA’s fair value assessment should factor in its premium growth and operational efficiency, positioning it as a high-growth technology leader with valuation metrics that, while above peers, align with its superior financial performance and market dominance.
