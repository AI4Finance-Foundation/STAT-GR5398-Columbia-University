NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) is a leading technology company primarily engaged in designing and manufacturing graphics processing units (GPUs), system-on-a-chip units (SoCs), and related software. Its products and services cater to a broad range of markets, including gaming, professional visualization, data centers, and automotive applications, with a strong emphasis on artificial intelligence (AI), machine learning, and high-performance computing.

The company's business model revolves around developing high-performance hardware and software solutions that drive advancements in graphics rendering, AI acceleration, and data processing. NVIDIA’s GPUs are widely used in gaming consoles, personal computers, and data centers, while its AI-focused platforms and software solutions are key enablers for industries including autonomous vehicles, cloud computing, and scientific research.

NVIDIA holds a prominent market position as a dominant player in the GPU market, competing with peers such as AMD and Intel. Its competitive edge lies in its innovation capabilities, extensive ecosystem, and strong customer base in both consumer and enterprise segments.

Financially, NVIDIA has demonstrated remarkable growth, particularly between 2022 and 2026, with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 68.3% in revenue, increasing from approximately $26.9 billion in 2022 to $215.9 billion in 2026. This growth is driven by expanding demand for its AI and data center products. Contribution Profit also rose significantly, reaching $153.5 billion in 2026 with a Contribution Margin fluctuating around 70%, indicating strong profitability from core operations.

NVIDIA’s EBITDA has shown substantial expansion from $11.35 billion in 2022 to $144.6 billion in 2026, with the EBITDA Margin improving from 42.2% to nearly 67%, reflecting enhanced operational efficiency and scale. Selling, General & Administrative (SG&A) expenses as a percentage of revenue declined markedly over this period, from 8.0% in 2022 to 2.1% in 2026, indicating better cost management.

Earnings per share (EPS) also improved notably, rising from $0.39 in 2022 to an estimated $4.93 in 2026, signaling strong shareholder value creation. While price-to-earnings (PE) ratios are not available, NVIDIA’s valuation metrics such as EV/EBITDA suggest it trades at a premium compared to peers, with a forecast EV/EBITDA of approximately 31.4 in 2026, reflecting investor confidence in its growth prospects.

In comparison to peers AMD and Intel, NVIDIA’s EBITDA in 2026 is substantially higher, underscoring its leadership position in profitability and scale. NVIDIA’s future outlook remains robust, with continued revenue growth expected albeit at a moderated pace (forecasted revenue CAGR slows after 2026), supported by sustained innovation and market demand in AI and data-intensive applications.

In summary, NVIDIA Corporation is a technology powerhouse with a diversified product portfolio centered on GPUs and AI computing. Its strong market position, impressive financial growth, and efficient cost structure underpin its attractive long-term prospects in a rapidly evolving technology landscape.
