NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) has demonstrated remarkable financial growth over the 2022A to 2026A period, with revenues expanding from $26.9 billion in 2022 to $215.9 billion in 2026, representing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 68.3%. This exceptional revenue growth is supported by a significant improvement in contribution profit, which reached $153.5 billion in 2026, while the contribution margin stabilized around 71.1% in 2026, indicating strong operational efficiency.

EBITDA has shown a robust upward trend, increasing from $11.4 billion in 2022 to $144.6 billion in 2026, with the EBITDA margin improving markedly from 42.2% in 2022 to 66.9% in 2026. This margin expansion signals effective cost control and scalability of NVIDIA’s business model. Notably, selling, general, and administrative (SG&A) expenses as a percentage of revenue have declined from 8.0% in 2022 to just 2.1% in 2026, further supporting profitability gains.

Earnings per share (EPS) have also improved significantly, rising from $0.39 in 2022 to $4.93 in 2026, reflecting strong bottom-line growth. Compared to peers AMD and Intel, NVIDIA’s EBITDA is substantially higher, and its EV/EBITDA multiple, while premium at 31.4x in 2026, suggests investor confidence in its growth prospects.

Looking ahead, forecasts for 2027E through 2029E indicate continued revenue growth, reaching nearly $250 billion by 2029, with EBITDA margins further improving to approximately 73.5%. SG&A expenses are expected to decline even further as a percentage of revenue, enhancing profitability. EPS is projected to increase steadily to $5.88 by 2029.

Overall, NVIDIA’s strong financial performance is driven by its leadership in high-growth markets such as AI, gaming, and data centers. The outlook remains positive, supported by scalable operations and expanding margins, positioning NVIDIA well for sustained long-term growth.
