Revenue Growth: AMD’s revenue grew from $16.43 billion in 2021A to $34.64 billion in 2025A, representing a strong historical CAGR of 20.5%. However, revenue growth rates fluctuated notably year-to-year, including a decline of 3.9% in 2023A and a sharp increase of 34.3% in 2025A, with more moderate growth projected in the forecast years 2026E to 2028E (4-6%).

Gross Profit Margin: The Contribution Margin, which reflects profitability after cost of operations, improved from 48.2% in 2021A to 49.5% in 2025A and is forecasted to increase further to 52.5% by 2028E. This indicates a gradually enhancing gross profitability over both historical and forecast periods.

SG&A Expense Margin: SG&A margin increased from 8.8% in 2021A to 12.0% in 2025A, reflecting rising operating expenses relative to revenue. Forecasts suggest a slight reduction in SG&A margin to 10.5% by 2028E, indicating improving expense control in the longer term.

EBITDA Margin Stability: AMD’s EBITDA margin declined from 25.3% in 2021A to a low of 18.3% in 2023A but improved to 21.0% in 2025A. Forecasts show a significant expansion to 42.0% by 2028E, implying strong expected operational leverage and profitability improvements going forward.
