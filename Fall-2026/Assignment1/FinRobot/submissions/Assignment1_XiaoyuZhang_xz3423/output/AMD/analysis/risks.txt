- Revenue Volatility Risk: AMD's revenue declined by 3.9% in 2023A after a strong 43.6% growth in 2022A, indicating potential volatility in demand that may affect future revenue stability.

- Profitability Pressure Risk: EBITDA margin decreased from 25.3% in 2021A to 18.3% in 2023A, showing margin compression risks despite forecasted improvement; maintaining or expanding margins may be challenging.

- High SG&A Expense Risk: SG&A margin increased from 8.8% in 2021A to 12.0% in 2025A, potentially pressuring net profitability if revenue growth does not sufficiently offset rising operating expenses.

- Competitive Intensity Risk: AMD's EBITDA is significantly lower than peers Intel and Nvidia, with AMD's 2023A EBITDA at $4.15B versus Nvidia's $5.99B and Intel's $11.24B, suggesting challenges in competing effectively in profitability and scale.

- Valuation Multiples Uncertainty: AMD's EV/EBITDA multiple has shown high variability (e.g., 57.12x in 2023A and 47.85x in 2025A) without consistent trend, implying market valuation may be volatile and sensitive to company performance and sector dynamics.
