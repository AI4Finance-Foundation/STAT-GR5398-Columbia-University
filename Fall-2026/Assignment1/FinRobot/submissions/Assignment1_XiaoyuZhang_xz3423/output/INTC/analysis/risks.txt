- Revenue Decline Risk: Intel's revenue has declined significantly from $79.0B in 2021A to $52.9B in 2025A, representing a historical CAGR of -9.6%, indicating a risk of continued top-line contraction.

- Profitability Risk: EBITDA dropped from $33.9B in 2021A to a low of $1.2B in 2024A before partially recovering to $14.4B in 2025A, with EBITDA margin falling sharply to 2.3% in 2024A, showing volatility and risk in sustaining profitability.

- Earnings Per Share (EPS) Risk: EPS turned negative starting 2024A with -$4.38 and remains negative in forecasts through 2028E, reflecting risk in delivering positive shareholder returns.

- Competitive Pressure Risk: Compared to peers AMD and NVIDIA, Intel’s EBITDA and EV/EBITDA multiples show higher volatility and lower growth prospects, suggesting competitive challenges impacting financial performance.

- Margin Compression Risk: Contribution margin declined from 55.4% in 2021A to 34.8% in 2025A and is forecasted to stay below 38% through 2028E, indicating ongoing pressure on operational efficiency and cost management.
