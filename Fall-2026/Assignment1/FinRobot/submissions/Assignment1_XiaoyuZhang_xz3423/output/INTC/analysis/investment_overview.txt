Intel Corporation (INTC) has experienced a notable decline in revenue from 2021 to 2025, with revenues decreasing from $79.0 billion in 2021A to $52.9 billion in 2025A, reflecting a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of -9.6%. This revenue contraction has been accompanied by pressure on profitability metrics. Contribution Profit fell significantly from $43.8 billion in 2021A to $18.4 billion in 2025A, and the Contribution Margin declined from 55.4% to 34.8% over the same period. EBITDA also dropped sharply from $33.9 billion in 2021A to $14.4 billion in 2025A, with the EBITDA Margin decreasing from 42.9% to 27.2%.

Earnings per share (EPS) have shown a steep decline, moving from $4.89 in 2021A down to a loss of $0.059 in 2025A, indicating challenges in maintaining profitability. The 2024A year was particularly difficult, with an EPS loss of $4.38. This suggests operational and market headwinds impacting Intel’s financial performance.

Looking ahead, forecasts for 2026E through 2028E indicate a modest recovery. Revenue is expected to grow gradually, reaching approximately $61.2 billion by 2028E. Contribution Profit and EBITDA are also projected to improve, with EBITDA rising to about $18.7 billion and EBITDA Margin expanding to 30.6% by 2028E. However, EPS is forecasted to remain negative through 2028E, indicating continued challenges in restoring net profitability.

Compared to peers, Intel’s EBITDA remains substantial but has declined more noticeably than AMD’s and NVIDIA’s, with NVIDIA showing strong EBITDA growth and higher EV/EBITDA multiples. Intel's EV/EBITDA ratio spiked to 106.9 in 2024 but is expected to normalize to around 14.5 by 2025, suggesting market expectations of a recovery.

In summary, Intel faces a challenging environment marked by revenue and profitability declines in recent years, but forecasts indicate potential stabilization and gradual improvement in operational performance. Continued monitoring of profitability trends and EPS recovery will be crucial for assessing investment prospects.
