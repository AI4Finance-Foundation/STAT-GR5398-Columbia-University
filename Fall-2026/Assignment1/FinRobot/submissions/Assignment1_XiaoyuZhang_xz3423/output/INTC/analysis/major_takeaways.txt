Revenue Growth: Intel's revenue declined significantly from $79.02 billion in 2021A to $52.85 billion in 2025A, representing a historical CAGR of -9.6%. However, forecasts indicate a modest recovery with revenue growing to $61.18 billion by 2028E.

Gross Profit Margin: Gross profit margin data is not explicitly provided, but Contribution Margin declined from 55.4% in 2021A to a low of 32.7% in 2024A before slightly improving to 34.8% in 2025A and forecasted to reach 37.8% by 2028E, indicating ongoing margin pressure with some recovery expected.

SG&A Expense Margin: SG&A margin increased from 8.3% in 2021A to 11.1% in 2022A, then gradually decreased to 8.7% in 2025A and is forecasted to continue declining to 7.2% by 2028E, suggesting improved operational efficiency in SG&A expenses over time.

EBITDA Margin Stability: EBITDA margin sharply declined from 42.9% in 2021A to just 2.3% in 2024A, before rebounding to 27.2% in 2025A. Forecasts show stabilization with EBITDA margin improving gradually to 30.6% by 2028E, reflecting volatility but a positive trend in profitability.
