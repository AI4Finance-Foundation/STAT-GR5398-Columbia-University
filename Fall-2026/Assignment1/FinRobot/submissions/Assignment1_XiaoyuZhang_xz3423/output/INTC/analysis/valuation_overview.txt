Intel Corporation (INTC) has experienced a significant decline in revenue from 2021A to 2025A, with a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of -9.6%, falling from $79.0 billion in 2021A to $52.9 billion in 2025A. Contribution margin also declined from 55.4% in 2021A to 34.8% in 2025A, reflecting margin pressure. EBITDA decreased sharply, hitting a low of $1.2 billion in 2024A before rebounding to $14.35 billion in 2025A. Correspondingly, EBITDA margin plummeted from 42.9% in 2021A to 2.3% in 2024A, recovering to 27.2% in 2025A. Earnings per share (EPS) turned negative in 2024A (-4.38) and remained negative in 2025A (-0.0589), indicating profitability challenges.

Peer comparison shows Intel's EBITDA remains substantially higher than AMD’s, which ranged from approximately $4.1 billion to $7.3 billion during 2021-2025, but lower than NVIDIA’s EBITDA, which surged from roughly $11.35 billion in 2022A to an estimated $86.1 billion in 2025A. Intel’s EV/EBITDA multiple fluctuated considerably, reaching an unusually high 106.9x in 2024A, likely reflecting market concerns during the profitability trough, before normalizing to 14.5x in 2025A. Comparatively, AMD’s EV/EBITDA was higher in most years except for 2022A, and NVIDIA’s EV/EBITDA remained elevated, peaking around 81x in 2023A and declining to 33.8x in 2025A.

Given the substantial recent earnings pressure, negative EPS, and volatile EBITDA margins, Intel’s valuation appears to be under stress. The sharp decline and recovery in EBITDA and margins suggest operational challenges and restructuring effects. Without current market price data, a precise fair value calculation cannot be performed. However, the normalization of EV/EBITDA to around 14.5x in 2025A aligns with a more typical semiconductor industry multiple, indicating that if Intel can sustain its EBITDA recovery and stabilize margins, its valuation could reflect a more normalized peer-relative level. Investors should monitor Intel’s ability to return to consistent profitability and revenue growth before considering it fairly valued.
