Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) has demonstrated robust revenue growth over the period from 2021 to 2025, with revenues increasing from approximately $27.45 billion in 2021 to $63.89 billion in 2025, reflecting a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 23.5%. This strong expansion is highlighted by a substantial revenue jump of 44.0% in 2024, followed by a 23.9% increase in 2025. The company’s EBITDA proxy, a preliminary measure based on operating income plus depreciation and amortization, showed growth from about $19.96 billion in 2023 to $34.12 billion in 2025. The EBITDA margin, although fluctuating, remains healthy at 53.4% in 2025, indicating solid operational profitability.

Forecasts for 2026 to 2028 suggest a moderation in revenue growth, with annual increases of 5.0%, 6.0%, and 4.0%, respectively. EBITDA margins are expected to stabilize at the 2025 level of 53.4%, resulting in EBITDA estimates of approximately $35.8 billion in 2026, rising to about $39.5 billion by 2028. These projections imply that while rapid growth may slow, Broadcom is anticipated to maintain strong profitability levels.

Data on certain key financial metrics such as cost of operations, SG&A expenses, contribution profit and margins, as well as earnings per share (EPS) and price-to-earnings (PE) ratios, are unavailable, limiting a more detailed margin and valuation analysis. Comparisons with peers such as Nvidia, AMD, Intel, and Qualcomm show Broadcom’s EBITDA figures remain competitive, though EV/EBITDA multiples for Broadcom are not provided, preventing a full valuation comparison.

In summary, Broadcom’s recent financial performance reflects strong revenue and earnings growth driven by its diversified semiconductor portfolio. The company’s outlook suggests continued profitability with moderated growth rates in the near term. Investors should consider the limited availability of certain financial details and the preliminary nature of EBITDA measures when evaluating the company’s financial position.
