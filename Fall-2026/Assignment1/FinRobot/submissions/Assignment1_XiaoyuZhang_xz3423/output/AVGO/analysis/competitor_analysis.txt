Broadcom Inc. (AVGO) demonstrates robust revenue growth and strong profitability compared to its semiconductor and technology peers Nvidia (NVDA), AMD, Intel (INTC), and Qualcomm (QCOM). From 2021 to 2025, Broadcom’s revenue is projected to grow at a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of approximately 23.5%, reaching $63.9 billion in 2025 from $27.5 billion in 2021. This growth rate notably outpaces typical industry expansion, reflecting Broadcom’s diversified product portfolio and strategic acquisitions.

In terms of EBITDA, Broadcom reported $19.96 billion in 2023, rising to $34.12 billion in 2025. The company maintains a strong EBITDA margin around 53-55%, indicating efficient cost management and high profitability. This margin is superior to many peers, though direct margin comparisons are limited by data unavailability for all peers. For instance, Nvidia’s EBITDA fluctuates significantly, with a notable increase to $86.1 billion forecasted in 2025, but the comparability is constrained due to differing definitions of EBITDA and non-harmonized data sources.

While Broadcom’s EV/EBITDA multiples are unavailable, peers like Nvidia and AMD exhibit high multiples reflecting strong market expectations and growth prospects, with Nvidia’s EV/EBITDA ranging from about 33.8x to 81.0x between 2021 and 2025 and AMD showing even higher multiples. Intel’s EV/EBITDA multiples are relatively lower but volatile, peaking dramatically in 2024.

Overall, Broadcom’s combination of high revenue growth and sustained EBITDA margins positions it competitively within the semiconductor industry. However, limited availability of certain financial metrics, such as cost of operations, SG&A, and valuation multiples for Broadcom, restricts a fully detailed comparative analysis. Unlike peers Nvidia and AMD, which are more growth and innovation-focused in GPUs and CPUs, Broadcom’s strength lies in diversified semiconductor solutions and infrastructure software, which may contribute to its stable profitability profile.
