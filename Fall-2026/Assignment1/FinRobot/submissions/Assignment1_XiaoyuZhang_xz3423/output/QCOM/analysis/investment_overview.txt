QUALCOMM Incorporated (QCOM) has exhibited a mixed financial performance over the 2021 to 2025 period, with revenue showing a compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 7.2%. Revenue increased significantly from $33.57 billion in 2021 to $44.28 billion in 2025, despite a notable dip in 2023 to $35.82 billion from a peak of $44.2 billion in 2022. The company’s EBITDA also demonstrated volatility, peaking at $17.62 billion in 2022 before dropping to $9.6 billion in 2023 and rebounding to $13.96 billion in 2025. The EBITDA margin followed a similar pattern, declining from 39.9% in 2022 to 26.8% in 2023, then recovering to 31.5% in 2025.

Looking ahead, forecasts for 2026 to 2028 assume moderate revenue growth rates of 5.0%, 6.0%, and 4.0%, respectively, with revenues reaching approximately $51.26 billion by 2028. The EBITDA margin is projected to stabilize at the 2025 level of 31.5%, resulting in an EBITDA of around $16.16 billion by 2028. These projections suggest a gradual recovery and steady growth trajectory after recent fluctuations.

Key growth drivers for QUALCOMM include its leadership position in semiconductor technology, particularly in mobile and wireless communications, and ongoing investments in 5G and emerging technologies. However, the absence of detailed cost of operations, SG&A, and contribution margin data limits a deeper margin analysis. Additionally, peer comparison shows that QUALCOMM’s EBITDA is generally competitive but lower than some peers like Broadcom (AVGO) and Intel (INTC) in recent years.

In summary, QUALCOMM’s recent financial performance reflects some cyclical challenges but a solid underlying growth potential supported by technology trends. Investors should consider data limitations and monitor future earnings releases for comprehensive margin and profitability insights.
