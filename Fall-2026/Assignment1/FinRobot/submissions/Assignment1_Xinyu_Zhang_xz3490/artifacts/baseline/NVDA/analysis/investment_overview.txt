NVIDIA Corporation (NVDA) – Investment Update

NVIDIA has delivered exceptional financial performance over the past three years, cementing its position as the leading provider of AI and accelerated computing solutions. Revenue reached $60.9 billion in FY2024, a year-on-year increase of 125.9%, and is projected to more than double again in FY2025 to $130.5 billion. This rapid top-line expansion is being driven by surging demand for NVIDIA’s data center GPUs and AI platforms, as cloud providers, enterprises, and governments ramp up investments in generative AI and high-performance computing.

Profitability has scaled even faster. EBITDA jumped from $6.0 billion in FY2023 to $35.6 billion in FY2024, and is forecast to reach $86.1 billion in FY2025, equating to a margin of 66.0%. Contribution margin has expanded sharply, reflecting strong operating leverage and disciplined cost control, while SG&A as a percentage of revenue is set to decline further to just 2.7% in FY2025. EPS is also on a steep upward trajectory, expected to rise from $1.21 in FY2024 to $2.97 in FY2025 and $4.93 in FY2026.

Compared to peers, NVIDIA’s EBITDA outpaces both AMD and Intel by a wide margin, and the company’s EV/EBITDA multiple, while still elevated at 33.8x for FY2025, is quickly normalizing as earnings scale. With a three-year revenue CAGR of 68.3% and robust margin expansion, NVIDIA’s long-term outlook remains highly favorable. The company is well positioned to benefit from sustained AI infrastructure buildout, new product launches, and ecosystem expansion, supporting continued growth and shareholder value creation.
