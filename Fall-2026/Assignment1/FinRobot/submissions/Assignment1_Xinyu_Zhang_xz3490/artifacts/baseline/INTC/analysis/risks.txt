- **Sustained Revenue Decline:** Intel's revenue has contracted at a -9.6% CAGR from 2021 to 2024, with continued declines forecast through 2025, raising concerns about long-term demand, competitive positioning, and market share loss.

- **Profitability Pressure:** EBITDA margin has plummeted from 42.9% (2021) to a low of 2.3% (2024), with only a gradual recovery projected, highlighting risks around cost control, operational efficiency, and pricing power.

- **Earnings Volatility and Negative EPS:** Earnings per share have dropped sharply, moving negative in 2024 (EPS: -$4.38) with continued losses expected through 2027, undermining investor confidence and dividend sustainability.

- **Competitive Disadvantage:** Intel's EBITDA and EV/EBITDA metrics lag key peers (notably NVIDIA and, in several years, AMD), indicating weaker operating leverage and market position amid intensifying industry competition.

- **Valuation Risk:** Extreme volatility in the PE ratio (e.g., -614.6 in 2025) and a spike in EV/EBITDA (106.9x in 2024) suggest the stock could be overvalued relative to deteriorating fundamentals, exposing investors to significant downside if expectations are not met.
