- **Valuation Risk**: NVIDIA’s PE and EV/EBITDA multiples are significantly higher than peers (e.g., 2024 EV/EBITDA: NVDA 43.5x vs. AMD 38.3x, INTC 106.9x; 2024 PE: NVDA ~51.6x), indicating premium pricing and potential for sharp correction if growth expectations are not met.

- **Growth Sustainability Risk**: Revenue growth rates are expected to decelerate sharply after 2026E (from >100% CAGR in recent years to low single digits), raising concerns about whether current high valuation is justified by future performance.

- **Margin Compression Risk**: While EBITDA margins are projected to expand (from 22.2% in 2023 to 73.5% in 2027E), any increase in competition, input costs, or R&D/SG&A spending could pressure margins below these optimistic forecasts.

- **Peer Competition Risk**: Competitors such as AMD and Intel are also investing heavily; any major technological breakthrough or pricing action by peers could erode NVIDIA’s market share and profitability.

- **Execution/Operational Risk**: The scale of projected growth (e.g., revenue from ~$26B in 2023 to ~$250B in 2027E) requires flawless execution in supply chain, product development, and go-to-market—any missteps could materially impact financial performance.
