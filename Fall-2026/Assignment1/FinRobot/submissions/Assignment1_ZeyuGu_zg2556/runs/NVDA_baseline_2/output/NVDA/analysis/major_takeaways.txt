Revenue Growth: NVIDIA's revenue trajectory demonstrates an extraordinary compound annual growth rate (CAGR) of 68.3% from 2022 to 2029, driven by explosive expansion in its Data Center segment, as highlighted by the 106% year-over-year revenue increase in Q2 fiscal 2027. This surge reflects strong secular trends in artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and high-performance computing, positioning NVIDIA as a dominant player in these high-growth markets. The initial moderate revenue growth in 2023 (0.2%) followed by a dramatic leap in 2024 (125.9%) underscores the company's successful pivot and scaling of next-generation technologies. The sustainability of this growth is supported by NVIDIA’s expanding market leadership and technological moat in GPUs and AI accelerators, suggesting continued robust demand and competitive advantage.

Gross Profit Margin: The company's gross margin expanded significantly from 56.9% in 2023 to an expected 74.1% by 2029, evidencing strong pricing power and operational leverage. This margin expansion reflects NVIDIA’s ability to command premium pricing in specialized high-performance markets and optimize its cost structure amid rising volumes. The consistent contribution margin above 70% from 2024 onward highlights a durable competitive moat through proprietary technology and scale advantages. Such high margins indicate substantial business quality, allowing NVIDIA to reinvest in R&D and strategic initiatives while maintaining profitability superior to industry norms.

SG&A Expense Margin: NVIDIA's SG&A margin exhibits a marked decline from 9.0% in 2023 to a razor-thin 0.6% by 2029, signaling exceptional operating leverage and efficiency gains. This trend indicates that the company is scaling its operations with minimal incremental sales and marketing expenses, a testament to strong brand recognition, channel partnerships, and effective management execution. The rapid reduction in SG&A as a proportion of revenue reveals a scalable business model, enhancing free cash flow generation and enabling capital allocation flexibility. This efficiency advantage bolsters NVIDIA’s ability to sustain competitive positioning without proportionate increases in overhead.

EBITDA Margin Stability: NVIDIA’s EBITDA margin rebounds impressively from 22.2% in 2023 to a projected 73.5% in 2029, outpacing peers such as AMD and Intel both in absolute margin and growth trajectory. This margin recovery highlights the quality and sustainability of profitability, driven by a blend of high-margin product mix and disciplined cost management. Compared with AMD and Intel’s more modest EBITDA figures and volatile margins, NVIDIA’s superior profitability profile justifies its premium valuation multiples (EV/EBITDA above 30) and reflects investor confidence in its long-term earnings power. For investors, this margin strength underpins NVIDIA’s attractive risk-reward profile, combining robust growth with durable profitability in a capital-intensive industry.
