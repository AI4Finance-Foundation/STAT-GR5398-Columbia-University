Revenue Growth: Broadcom's revenue nearly doubled from $35.8B in 2023 to $63.9B in 2025, driven primarily by a substantial 44% growth in 2024 and a strong 24% in 2025 before moderating to single-digit growth thereafter. This surge reflects successful expansion in both product and subscription/service lines, as confirmed by the 10-Q data showing significant increases in net revenue for products and subscriptions. The rapid growth is likely fueled by strategic acquisitions, strong market demand, and an expanding product portfolio. While growth moderates over time, the high initial CAGR of 33.6% underscores Broadcom’s strengthening market position and capacity to capitalize on industry trends, positioning it as a dominant player in semiconductors and enterprise software.

Gross Profit Margin: Contribution margins demonstrate a rebound from 63.0% in 2024 to 70.8% by 2028, indicating improving operational efficiency and pricing power. The margin dip in 2024 aligns with elevated cost of operations but recovers as scale and cost controls take effect. This margin expansion signals effective cost optimization and reinforces Broadcom's competitive moat, reflecting a high-quality business with sustainable profitability. The gross margin recovery also suggests resilience in managing supply chain and input costs, which is critical in a capital-intensive semiconductor environment.

SG&A Expense Margin: SG&A margin spiked to 9.6% in 2024, likely due to integration costs or investments supporting the rapid revenue ramp, before declining steadily to 5.1% by 2028. This trend reveals strong operating leverage and improving cost discipline as the company scales, showcasing management’s execution capability in controlling expenses relative to growth. The declining SG&A margin enhances free cash flow potential and underscores scalability, which is a positive signal for long-term operating efficiency and margin sustainability.

EBITDA Margin Stability: Broadcom's EBITDA margin experienced volatility but is projected to improve markedly from 45.5% in 2024 to 65.7% by 2028, reflecting a quality earnings profile. Compared to peers like Nvidia, which reported EBITDA of $35.6B in 2024 versus Broadcom’s $23.5B, Broadcom maintains competitive profitability on a slightly smaller scale. The margin expansion and solid EBITDA growth enhance valuation attractiveness by supporting robust cash flow generation. This positions Broadcom favorably within its peer group for investors seeking a combination of growth, profitability, and margin resilience in the semiconductor and infrastructure software sectors.
