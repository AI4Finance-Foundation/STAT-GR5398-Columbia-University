Thesis Confirmed

Key Takeaways:
- Apple reported strong Q3 fiscal 2026 results with revenue of $109.4 billion, up 16% year over year, significantly beating prior year growth.
- Gross margin improved to 50.1%, aided by tariff refunds contributing approximately 2 percentage points.
- Diluted EPS rose 29% year over year to $2.02, including a favorable $0.11 impact from tariff refunds.
- Management remains confident in growth drivers, particularly from services and wearables, and highlighted operational efficiencies.
- The company’s EV/EBITDA multiple remains attractive relative to peers, supporting continued valuation upside.

Performance Analysis:
Apple's fiscal Q3 2026 performance surpassed expectations with revenue increasing 16% year over year to $109.4 billion, a notable acceleration compared to the moderate revenue growth trends seen in prior years. The gross margin expanded to 50.1%, driven not only by operational improvements but also a favorable tariff refund impact of about 2 percentage points. Earnings per share rose 29% to $2.02, reflecting both top-line strength and margin expansion. This represents a clear beat versus market consensus and historical trends, underpinned by robust demand for Apple's services segment and wearables, offsetting competitive pressures in hardware. The company’s operational discipline and favorable macro developments contributed to this solid quarter.

Thesis Impact:
The recent quarterly results reinforce the core investment thesis centered on Apple's ability to sustain strong revenue growth through diversification into high-margin services and expanding its ecosystem. The margin expansion and EPS growth validate management's focus on operational efficiencies and tariff-related cost benefits, which support improved profitability. The positive momentum in services and wearables segments further strengthens the thesis that Apple can drive recurring revenue streams less susceptible to hardware cycle fluctuations. Despite modest revenue growth in previous years, the acceleration witnessed this quarter confirms the company’s resilience and innovation-led growth strategy. The valuation remains reasonable relative to peers like AMD and NVIDIA, supporting a favorable risk/reward profile. In summary, these developments affirm confidence in Apple's long-term growth trajectory and justify maintaining or incrementally increasing exposure to the stock.
